Changelog

What’s new in ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC

1.0.0 - First release

Release date: to be added when the app is published.

  • Product reviews block with a star summary, sorting, search, a photos-only filter, helpful votes and abuse reports. Several layouts to choose from.
  • Photo and video reviews, up to 5 files per review. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB, videos MP4, MOV or WebM up to 30 MB.
  • Record a video review right in the review form, in the browser, up to 60 seconds, with no app for the customer to install. Where recording is not possible, customers can still upload a clip.
  • Video reviews block: a row of vertical video cards that open in a full-screen viewer with swipe, sound on and off, and a link to the product.
  • Review questions: up to four multiple-choice questions you choose (fit, quality, value, recommend or your own). Answers show on each review and as share bars under the star summary.
  • Collect reviews with a QR code or a link: pick a product, get a link that opens the review form (or the video recorder), copy it or download the QR code for packaging and flyers.
  • Google Shopping product reviews feed: one address to paste into Google Merchant Center, so your published reviews and star ratings can appear next to your products in Google. Includes brand, SKU and barcode when you have them.
  • Verified buyer badge for reviews matched to an order, through the link in a review request e-mail, the order status page or a logged-in customer account.
  • More theme blocks: star rating, review carousel, photo wall, all reviews page, trust badge and questions and answers, plus two app embeds: product card stars and review rich results for search engines.
  • Store reviews: customers can review your store as a whole, meaning your service, the delivery and how the order went. Add the Store reviews block to your home page or any other page. Store reviews are kept apart from product reviews: they never change a product's star rating, and they do not appear in the review carousel, the photo wall or the Google Shopping feed.
  • Product groups: if you sell the same item as several products, for example one product per color or size, put them in a group under Settings, Product groups. Every product in the group shows the reviews of all of them, with one star rating for the group. Each review stays with the product it was written for and is labeled with that product on the others. Up to 100 groups, with 2 to 25 products each.
  • Share a review from your store: every review has a Share button. On phones and tablets it opens the device's own share sheet. On a computer it opens a menu with Copy link, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Pinterest (when the review has a photo) and E-mail. The link opens the product page at that review. You can turn the button off under Settings, Widget, Features.
  • Share a review from the app: open a published review to copy its link, open it in X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Pinterest or an e-mail, or download an image of it as a square post or a story. The image shows the stars, words and name exactly as the customer wrote them.
  • Review prompt on the order status page: add the Product review prompt block to your Order status page in the checkout and accounts editor. It lists the products of the order, each with a Write a review button, and can offer Record a video too. For a customer who is signed in to their account it lists the products that have shipped, and a review written through it is marked Verified buyer.
  • Integrations: under Settings, Integrations, send review activity to the tools you already use. A signed webhook works with Zapier, Make, Omnisend or your own server, and Klaviyo receives events you can build flows on. Webhook events never contain a customer's name or e-mail address. Both are off until you turn them on.
  • Questions and answers on product pages, with your own answers and a moderation queue.
  • Moderation rules: automatic publishing, link, e-mail and phone checks, blocked words, spam and duplicate detection. A review is never held because of its star rating.
  • Review request e-mails: a delay after fulfillment, up to two reminders, your own wording, an unsubscribe link in every message, a test e-mail, and a daily limit. Off until you turn it on.
  • Optional discount code for reviews with a photo or video, with a disclosure shown next to the review form.
  • Import the reviews you already have from a CSV or JSON file, with a column check and preview.
  • Reviews are shown exactly as customers wrote them. You can reply, publish, hide, mark as spam or delete a review, but the app has no way to change its rating, words, name, photos or videos, and no way to type one in yourself.
  • Analytics: rating over time, star distribution, common topics and your reply times.
  • Optional AI help, only when you press the button: moderation suggestions for pending reviews and reply drafts.
  • Shopify Flow triggers: Review created, Low-rated review created and Question asked.
  • Export every review (all statuses, with photo and video links) as a CSV or JSON file from the Reviews page. Your reviews are yours.
  • Widget text in 12 languages besides English: French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese (Brazil), Dutch, Polish, Turkish, Japanese, Korean, Swedish and Danish. It follows the language of the store.
  • Three places to work: Dashboard (numbers, insights and what needs attention), Reviews (reviews, questions and import in tabs) and Settings (widget, moderation rules, review requests, product groups and integrations in tabs).
  • Reviews, questions and settings are stored in your own Shopify store. Shopify's privacy requests are handled.
  • A Get started guide in the app and a public help center.
