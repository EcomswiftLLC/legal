Install and use guide

From install to your first reviews

Five steps, in the order most stores do them. Each step says what to click and what you should see. The same steps are in the app under Get started. After them come five things you can add when you want them.

Before you start

  • You need a Shopify store and permission to install apps.
  • ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC shows reviews with theme app blocks, which Shopify supports in Online Store 2.0 themes. If your theme cannot add app blocks to the product page, the blocks cannot be placed.
  • Verified buyer badges and review request e-mails read your orders. Shopify requires an approval called protected customer data access for that. Everything else works without it, and the Dashboard tells you when it is missing.
  • ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC is currently free to use.

The steps

  1. Install and connect

    Install ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC from the Shopify App Store. Shopify lists the permissions the app asks for and asks you to approve them. The reasons are explained on the privacy page.

    The app opens inside your Shopify admin. The first time, it prepares the places where reviews, questions and settings are kept in your own store, and connects your store to the part that receives reviews from your storefront. There is nothing to copy or paste.

    What you will see

    The Dashboard with a card called Finish setting up ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC, a progress bar and one row for each unfinished step. The first two rows, Review storage is set up in your store and Connected for storefront reviews and e-mails, change to Ready and Connected after a few seconds. If a row shows an error, press Check again.

  2. Add the reviews block to your theme

    Reviews appear on your store through blocks that you place in the theme editor.

    1. In ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC, open Get started (or the Dashboard) and press Add the reviews block. Your theme editor opens in a new tab.
    2. If it does not open on the product page, go to Online Store, Themes, Customize in your Shopify admin and choose the product template.
    3. Add the block named Product reviews (under Apps) below the product description, then press Save in the theme editor.
    4. Back in ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC, press Add star ratings and add Star rating near the product title.
    5. Press Mark as done on the Dashboard. Shopify does not let an app see which blocks a theme uses, so this one is your confirmation.

    Other blocks you can add the same way: Review carousel, Photo wall, All reviews (for a page of its own), Trust badge, Questions and answers and Store reviews (see Store reviews below).

    Two features are app embeds, which you switch on under App embeds in the theme editor’s left sidebar instead of placing them on a page: Product card stars, which adds a small rating to product cards on collection and search pages, and Review rich results, which prints the structured data search engines read. Whether a search engine shows stars is up to the search engine.

    What you will see

    In the theme editor, the block appears in the list on the left and a preview in the middle. Until the product has reviews, the block shows a short invitation to write the first one. On your live store, the same block shows the star summary, the reviews and a Write a review button.

  3. Set how the widget looks

    Open Widget settings.

    • Look: choose a layout, light, dark or automatic colors, and the colors themselves.
    • Features: switch photos, videos, helpful votes, search, sorting, filters, the verified badge, abuse reports, the Share button, the review form and questions on or off.
    • Lists: how many reviews load per page, and which reviews the carousel, photo wall and all reviews page highlight.
    • Wording: replace any text shoppers see. An empty field keeps the default.

    The preview next to the form follows your choices before you save. When you change something, a save bar appears at the top of the admin with Save and Discard.

    What you will see

    A preview of a rating summary and one review, which changes as you edit. If you pick colors that are hard to read, a warning banner says which ones.

  4. Import the reviews you already have

    Import a file. Open Import. Choose a CSV or JSON export from another review app, or paste the text.

    1. Choose your export. ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC tells you which format it recognised. Files can be up to 25 MB and 20,000 rows.
    2. Check the columns. Confirm which column holds the rating, text, name, date and so on. You see a preview before anything is written. Only the rating is required.
    3. Match products. Reviews are matched to your products by id, handle or title. You can pick a product for the ones that do not match.
    4. Run the import. It runs in steps and you can pause it. Photo and video links are fetched by Shopify from the original addresses. A list of rows that could not be imported can be downloaded.

    Reviews stay as customers wrote them. Only import reviews your customers really wrote. After that, every review comes from the review form on your store. In Reviews you can reply, publish, hide, mark as spam or delete a review. You cannot change its rating, words, name, photos or videos, and you cannot type a review in yourself.

    What you will see

    Reviews lists every review with its status: Published, Pending, Hidden or Spam. Click a review to read it, reply publicly, publish, hide or delete it.

  5. Turn on review requests

    Review requests are off until you choose a mode. Open Review requests.

    • Send e-mails after orders are fulfilled: ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC e-mails the customer after the number of days you set (5 by default), with up to two reminders. Each e-mail lists the products of the order with a row of stars and a link that opens the review form. Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link.
    • No e-mails: ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC sends nothing. Shoppers can still write a review from the product page, and you can still ask on the order status page (see Ask on the order status page below). A prompt on the Shopify thank-you page is not available.
    • Off: no requests.

    In the e-mail mode, set the sender name, the reply-to address, the subject and the introduction. Add a postal address for the footer, which e-mail law in many countries requires. By default e-mails go out through a shared sending service operated by Ecom Swift LLC with a limit per store per day (25 by default). You can instead paste your own Resend or Brevo key, which is stored encrypted. Use Send a test e-mail to see one first. You can skip orders with certain tags, for example wholesale.

    The first time you switch e-mails on, ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC looks at orders fulfilled in the last 14 days only. Orders older than 60 days never get a request. The check runs once an hour, so an e-mail goes out within about an hour of becoming due.

    Optionally you can give a discount code for reviews that include a photo or video. The settings page requires a disclosure to be shown next to the review form, and reviews are published whether they are positive or negative.

    What you will see

    A live preview of the e-mail on the right of the settings, using a sample product. On the Dashboard, the row Asking customers for reviews changes from Off to E-mail on.

More you can set up

These five are optional. Set up the ones you want, in any order, after the steps above.

Store reviews

Let customers review your store as a whole: your service, the delivery, how the order went. Store reviews are kept apart from product reviews. They never change a product’s star rating, and they do not appear in the review carousel, the photo wall or the Google Shopping feed.

  1. In your Shopify admin go to Online Store, Themes, Customize.
  2. On the home page, or any other page, add the block named Store reviews (under Apps) and press Save.
  3. Open that page on your store. The button Write a store review opens the form Review our store.
  4. In ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC open Reviews and set Review type to Store reviews to see these alone. You can publish, hide, mark as spam, reply or delete, as with any review. You cannot change what the customer wrote.

If you installed the app before store reviews existed, open the app once after the update. That prepares the place in your store where the block reads its data.

What you will see

Before the first review, the block says No store reviews yet with the button. After that it shows your store’s star average, the rating breakdown and the reviews. In Reviews, a store review has a Store review badge where the product name would be. The Dashboard shows store reviews on a line of their own.

Product groups

If you sell the same item as several products, for example one product per color or size, put those products in a group. Every product in the group then shows the reviews of all of them, with one star rating for the group.

  1. Open Settings, then the Product groups tab, and press Add group.
  2. Type a Group name. Only you see it.
  3. Press Add products, choose the products, then press Done. A group needs at least 2 products and can hold up to 25. A product can be in one group.
  4. Press Save in the bar at the top and keep the page open until the banner Updating star ratings is gone.

Nothing is copied or changed. Each review stays with the product it was written for, and on the other products of the group it is labeled, for example “Reviewed: Blue ceramic mug”. Take a product out of its group and it shows its own reviews and rating again. Group products only when they really are the same item.

What you will see

Each product page of the group lists the reviews of the whole group with the same star rating and count. In Reviews, every review is still listed under its own product.

Share a review

On your store. Every review has a Share button next to Helpful and Report. On a phone or tablet it opens the share sheet of the device. On a computer it opens a menu with Copy link, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Pinterest (when the review has a photo) and E-mail. The link opens the product page at that review. To remove the button, open Settings, Widget, Features and untick Share button on reviews.

From the app.

  1. Open Reviews and click a published review.
  2. Under the moderation buttons find Share this review. Press Copy link, or one of the buttons that open X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Pinterest or an e-mail with the link and the review’s own words.
  3. To post it as a picture, choose the Image format (square post or story), the Image colors and, if the review has photos, the Customer photo, then press Download image.
What you will see

A preview of the image next to the choices. It shows the stars, words and name exactly as the customer wrote them; a review that is too long for the image ends with an ellipsis. Nothing in this section can be typed into. A store review has no product page to link to, so it offers the image and no link.

Ask on the order status page

Customers come back to the order status page to follow their delivery. The Product review prompt block asks for a review there. It needs the new customer accounts.

  1. In your Shopify admin go to Settings, Checkout, and press Customize.
  2. Choose the Order status page, press Add block and choose Product review prompt.
  3. Optional: type your own Heading, and tick Offer a video review too to add a Record a video link.
  4. Press Save.

For a customer who is signed in to their account, the block lists the products of the order that have shipped, and a review written through it is marked Verified buyer. It shows nothing until something has shipped, nothing for a cancelled order, and it leaves out products that are not on your online store. For a customer who is not signed in, it lists the products of the order with a plain link, and the review is not marked verified.

The block works with Shopify only and sends nothing to our server. To tie a review to the order, it stores one random code on the customer’s own order in your store. If you installed the app before this block could do that, Shopify asks you to approve two new permissions for customer accounts when you update the app. Until you approve them, reviews written through the block are not marked Verified buyer.

What you will see

In the editor, a preview made from the editor’s sample order. On a real order, the heading How was your order? (Review your purchase for a customer who is not signed in) and one row for each product with a Write a review button, which opens the product page with the review form open.

Integrations: webhook and Klaviyo

Open Settings, then the Integrations tab. Both are off until you turn them on.

Webhook, for Zapier, Make, Omnisend or your own server:

  1. Paste the address of your receiver into Webhook address. It must start with https://.
  2. Press Create signing secret and copy the secret into your receiver. It is shown once.
  3. Tick Send events to a webhook address, choose the events under Events to send, and press Save in the bar at the top.
  4. Press Send test event. The page tells you what your receiver answered.

Klaviyo:

  1. In Klaviyo create a private API key with full access to Events.
  2. Paste it into Klaviyo private API key, tick Send events to Klaviyo, choose the events and press Save.
  3. Under Test Klaviyo type your own e-mail address and press Send test event. This also creates the two metrics in Klaviyo, “ReviewFlow Review Submitted” and “ReviewFlow Review Request Sent”, so you can choose them as a flow trigger.

Webhook events carry the review or question and the product, and never a customer’s name or e-mail address. Klaviyo events include the buyer’s e-mail address, so only reviews written through an order link (the review request e-mail or the order status page) are sent to Klaviyo. Each event is sent once: if your receiver is down or takes longer than 3 seconds, that event is not sent again. The privacy policy lists what is sent.

What you will see

Each card starts with its state: Off: nothing is sent, or On: events are sent to your address for the webhook and On: events are sent to Klaviyo for Klaviyo. After you save, the signing secret and the key are never shown again, only their last four characters.

Day to day

  • Reviews is your queue. Filter by status, rating, buyer, photos or product. Select several reviews to publish, hide or mark as spam together.
  • Moderation rules decide what is published automatically. By default everything is published unless it contains a link, an e-mail address or phone number, a blocked word, spam-like text or a duplicate. A review is never held because of its star rating. The Test a review box shows what a rule would do.
  • Questions lists questions shoppers asked on product pages. A question is shown on the product page after you answer and publish it.
  • Dashboard shows what needs attention, your numbers, and further down insights: rating over time, common topics and your reply times. Under Maintenance is a button to recompute star ratings if a number ever looks wrong.
  • Optional AI help runs only when you press its button: moderation suggestions for pending reviews and reply drafts. Each store has a small number of AI uses per day.

If something is wrong

The questions and answers cover the common cases: reviews not showing, photos still processing, a missing verified badge and e-mails not arriving. If you are still stuck, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com with your store address and what you see.

Uninstalling

Remove the ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC blocks in the theme editor, then in your Shopify admin open Settings, Apps and sales channels, choose ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC and uninstall. Your reviews are stored in your own store. Shopify removes the app’s data definitions when an app is uninstalled and keeps the entries for a period it does not publish, so export anything you want to keep first. The details are in the privacy policy.
