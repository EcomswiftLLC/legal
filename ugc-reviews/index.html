Help for ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC

ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC shows customer reviews of your products and of your store, with photos, videos and questions, and helps you collect new ones. Start with the guide, or look up an answer.

The five steps in short

  1. Connect. Open ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC once from your Shopify admin. It prepares the places where reviews are kept in your own store.
  2. Add the reviews block to your product page in the theme editor, and the star rating block where you want stars.
  3. Set the look of the widget in Widget settings.
  4. Import the reviews you already have from a CSV or JSON file. After that, every review comes from the review form on your store.
  5. Turn on review requests so customers are asked after their order is fulfilled.

Read the full guide. The same five steps are in the app under Get started.

More you can set up

These five are optional. Set up the ones you want, in any order, after the steps above. Each links to its part of the guide.

  • Store reviews. Customers can review your store as a whole: your service, the delivery, how the order went. Add the Store reviews block to your home page or any other page. Store reviews are kept apart from product reviews and never change a product’s star rating.
  • Product groups. If you sell the same item as several products, for example one product per color or size, put them in a group under Settings, Product groups. Every product in the group shows the reviews of all of them, with one star rating for the group. Each review stays with the product it was written for.
  • Share a review. Every review on your store has a Share button. From the app you can copy the link to a published review or download an image of it as a square post or a story. The stars, words and name are shown exactly as the customer wrote them.
  • Ask on the order status page. Add the Product review prompt block to your Order status page in the checkout and accounts editor. It lists the products of the order, each with a Write a review button. For a customer who is signed in it lists the products that have shipped, and a review written through it is marked Verified buyer.
  • Integrations. Under Settings, Integrations, send review activity to a signed webhook (Zapier, Make, Omnisend or your own server) or to Klaviyo. Webhook events never contain a customer’s name or e-mail address. Both are off until you turn them on.
