Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions merchants ask most. For the full walk-through, read the install and use guide.

Getting started

Does UGC Reviews app cost anything?

UGC Reviews app is currently free to use. If that ever changes, the App Store listing and this site will say so before it takes effect.

Which themes does it work with?

UGC Reviews app places its widgets as theme app blocks. Shopify supports these in Online Store 2.0 themes. If your theme cannot add an app block to the product page, the reviews block cannot be placed there.

Where are my reviews stored?

In your own Shopify store, as app-owned data (metaobjects and metafields), with photos and videos in your store’s Files. You can see the entries in your Shopify admin. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Reviews on your store

My reviews are not showing on my store

Check in this order. First, the UGC Reviews app block is on the product template and the theme is saved. Second, the review is Published: open Reviews and look at its status. A Pending or Hidden review is not shown. Third, the review belongs to the product page you are looking at. Fourth, reload the page without a cached copy.

If stars look wrong after editing reviews directly in Shopify, open the Dashboard, expand Maintenance and choose Recompute all ratings. If nothing helps, write to us with the product link.

Stars do not show on collection pages

Switch on the Product card stars app embed: in the theme editor open App embeds in the left sidebar and turn on UGC Reviews app’s Product card stars, then save. It adds a small rating to product cards on collection and search pages, and skips cards that already show a rating.

Will stars appear in Google search results?

The Review rich results app embed prints the structured data that search engines can read. Whether and when a search engine shows stars is its own decision, and UGC Reviews app cannot promise it.

How many reviews does a product page show?

The product reviews block carries up to the 100 newest published reviews of a product. Star averages and counts include every published review. Use the All reviews block on a page of its own for a longer list.

How do I change the colors or wording?

Open Widget settings. Colors, layout, features and every shopper-facing text can be changed there. A preview updates as you type, and nothing changes on your store until you press Save.

Photos and videos

What files can shoppers upload?

Up to 5 files per review. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB each. Videos can be MP4, MOV or WebM up to 30 MB each.

Photos or videos are stuck on “processing”

Shopify processes every uploaded file before it can be shown, and the review says the file is processing meanwhile. Keep the Reviews page open for a minute or so: UGC Reviews app checks again every few seconds and finishes the review when Shopify is done. Opening the app later does the same. If a file is still processing after a long time, delete it from the review and add it again, or write to us.

Verified buyers

How do verified buyers work?

A review gets the Verified buyer badge when UGC Reviews app can match it to an order. That happens when the shopper opened the link in your review request e-mail, or was logged in to their customer account and has a not-cancelled order for that product. Other reviews are published normally, without the badge.

This needs Shopify’s approval for protected customer data. Without it, reviews still work, no badge is shown and review request e-mails cannot be sent. The Dashboard says when this is the case. Reviews you add by hand never get the badge. Imported reviews keep the verified flag from your file when it has one.

Moderation

How do I hide a review?

Open Reviews, click the review and choose Hide. You can also tick several reviews and use Hide in the bar above the list. A hidden review disappears from your store but stays in your admin, and Publish brings it back. Delete removes it for good.

Why was a review held instead of published?

UGC Reviews app never holds a review because of its star rating. A review is held for what it contains (a link, an e-mail address or phone number, a blocked word, spam-like text, a duplicate), or because your Moderation rules say to approve everything, or only verified buyers, by hand. Open the review: a message at the top says why it was held.

Can I reply to a review?

Yes. Open the review, write the reply under Public reply and press Post reply. Shoppers see it under the review. You can ask the AI helper for a draft, which you can edit before posting.

Import

Can I bring my reviews from another app?

Yes. Open Import and choose a CSV or JSON export. UGC Reviews app shows the column mapping and a preview before it writes anything, matches reviews to your products, and imports in steps. Photo and video links are fetched by Shopify from the original addresses. Files can be up to 25 MB and 20,000 rows. A reviewer’s e-mail address in the file is only used to build a one-way fingerprint and is not stored.

Review requests

Why are no review request e-mails going out?

Check that the mode in Review requests is set to e-mails, not Off. Check that the Dashboard does not show customer data access as Not approved, because without Shopify’s approval the app cannot read order e-mails. Check that the delay since fulfillment has passed, that the order does not carry a skipped tag, and that the daily limit is not used up. Requests are checked once an hour. If you use your own Resend or Brevo key, check the key and the sender address with Send a test e-mail.

Which orders get a request?

Orders that have been fulfilled, after the delay you set. When you first switch e-mails on, only orders fulfilled in the last 14 days are considered, and orders older than 60 days never get one. Each order is asked once, plus the reminders you chose.

Can customers opt out?

Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link. It records a one-way fingerprint of the address so the person is not e-mailed again.

Can I reward customers for reviews?

You can offer a discount code for reviews that include a photo or video. The settings require a disclosure shown next to the review form, and reviews are published whether they are positive or negative.

AI help

Does UGC Reviews app use AI?

Only when you press a button. Moderation with AI suggests what to do with pending reviews, and Draft a reply writes a first version of a reply. The text of the reviews involved is sent to the AI service for that request. Nothing is applied until you confirm it, and each store has a small daily allowance.

Your data and uninstalling

How do I uninstall, and what happens to my data?

Remove the UGC Reviews app blocks in the theme editor if you like, then in your Shopify admin open Settings, Apps and sales channels, choose UGC Reviews app and uninstall.

Your reviews, questions and settings are stored in your own store. When an app is uninstalled, Shopify removes the app’s data definitions and keeps the entries for a period Shopify does not publish, so do not count on getting them back after a reinstall. Export what you want to keep before uninstalling.

On our side, the access token is removed at uninstall, and about 48 hours later Shopify asks us to erase what we still hold for the store. See the privacy policy.

How do I get a copy of, or delete, a shopper’s data?

Shopify sends us customer data and erasure requests. For an erasure request, that customer’s reviews and questions are kept but the name is replaced with Anonymous, the link to the person is removed and their photos and videos are deleted. You can also delete any review yourself in Reviews. See the privacy policy.
