Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

Short answers to the questions merchants ask most. For the full walk-through, read the install and use guide.

Getting started

Does ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC cost anything?

It is free. ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC has no plans, no monthly price, no usage fees and no charges of any kind. Sending review request e-mails through your own Brevo or Resend account is billed by that provider, not by us.

Which themes does it work with?

ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC places its widgets as theme app blocks. Shopify supports these in Online Store 2.0 themes. If your theme cannot add an app block to the product page, the reviews block cannot be placed there.

Where are my reviews stored?

In your own Shopify store, as app-owned data (metaobjects and metafields), with photos and videos in your store’s Files. You can see the entries in your Shopify admin. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Reviews on your store

My reviews are not showing on my store

Check in this order. First, the ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC block is on the product template and the theme is saved. Second, the review is Published: open Reviews and look at its status. A Pending or Hidden review is not shown. Third, the review belongs to the product page you are looking at. Fourth, reload the page without a cached copy.

If stars look wrong, for example after an import that was interrupted, open the Dashboard, expand Maintenance and choose Recompute all ratings. If nothing helps, write to us with the product link.

Stars do not show on collection pages

Switch on the Product card stars app embed: in the theme editor open App embeds in the left sidebar and turn on ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC’s Product card stars, then save. It adds a small rating to product cards on collection and search pages, and skips cards that already show a rating.

Will stars appear in Google search results?

The Review rich results app embed prints the structured data that search engines can read. Whether and when a search engine shows stars is its own decision, and ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC cannot promise it.

How many reviews does a product page show?

The product reviews block carries up to the 100 newest published reviews of a product. Star averages and counts include every published review. Use the All reviews block on a page of its own for a longer list.

How do I change the colors or wording?

Open Widget settings. Colors, layout, features and every shopper-facing text can be changed there. A preview updates as you type, and nothing changes on your store until you press Save.

Photos and videos

What files can shoppers upload?

Up to 5 files per review. Photos can be JPEG, PNG or WebP up to 5 MB each. Videos can be MP4, MOV or WebM up to 30 MB each.

Photos or videos are stuck on “processing”

Shopify processes every uploaded file before it can be shown, and the review says the file is processing meanwhile. Keep the Reviews page open for a minute or so: ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC checks again every few seconds and finishes the review when Shopify is done. Opening the app later does the same. If a file is still processing after a long time, delete it from the review and add it again, or write to us.

Can customers record a video review?

Yes. In the review form, the Record a video button opens the camera of the phone or computer for up to 60 seconds, and nothing has to be installed. Shoppers see a preview and can record again before they send. The browser asks for camera and microphone permission. If recording is not possible on a device, shoppers can still upload a clip from their gallery. Add the Video reviews block in the theme editor to show clips as vertical cards that open in a full-screen viewer. Turn recording off under Widget settings, Features.

How do I get reviews from a QR code or a link?

Open Review request settings and find Collect reviews with a QR code or link. Choose a product and copy the link, or download the QR code to print on a packing slip or flyer. The link opens the product page with the review form already open, and with the video recorder if you leave that box ticked.

How do I show my reviews on Google?

Open Review request settings and find Show your reviews on Google. Copy the feed address. In Google Merchant Center open Products, then Product ratings, add a feed, choose a scheduled fetch and paste the address. The feed carries your newest 2,000 published reviews that have text, with the rating, the name as shown on your store, the date and the product, plus brand, SKU and barcode when the product has them. E-mail addresses and order details are never included. Google decides whether and when ratings appear.

Verified buyers

How do verified buyers work?

A review gets the Verified buyer badge when ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC can match it to an order. That happens when the shopper opened the link in your review request e-mail, used the review prompt on the order status page while signed in to their account, or was logged in to their customer account and has a not-cancelled order for that product. Other reviews are published normally, without the badge.

This needs Shopify’s approval for protected customer data. Without it, reviews still work, no badge is shown and review request e-mails cannot be sent. The Dashboard says when this is the case. Imported reviews keep the verified flag from your file when it has one.

Moderation

How do I hide a review?

Open Reviews, click the review and choose Hide. You can also tick several reviews and use Hide in the bar above the list. A hidden review disappears from your store but stays in your admin, and Publish brings it back. Delete removes it for good.

Why was a review held instead of published?

ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC never holds a review because of its star rating. A review is held for what it contains (a link, an e-mail address or phone number, a blocked word, spam-like text, a duplicate), or because your Moderation rules say to approve everything, or only verified buyers, by hand. Open the review: a message at the top says why it was held.

Can I edit a review or add one myself?

No. A review is the customer’s own statement, so ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC shows it exactly as it was written. You cannot change the rating, the words, the name, the photos or the videos, and you cannot type a review in yourself. You can reply to a review in public, hide it, mark it as spam or delete it. If a customer wants to correct a review, ask them to send a new one and delete the old one.

Can I reply to a review?

Yes. Open the review, write the reply under Public reply and press Post reply. Shoppers see it under the review. You can ask the AI helper for a draft, which you can edit before posting.

Import

Can I bring my reviews from another app?

Yes. Open Import and choose a CSV or JSON export. ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC shows the column mapping and a preview before it writes anything, matches reviews to your products, and imports in steps. Photo and video links are fetched by Shopify from the original addresses. Files can be up to 25 MB and 20,000 rows. A reviewer’s e-mail address in the file is only used to build a one-way fingerprint and is not stored.

Review requests

Why are no review request e-mails going out?

Check that the mode in Review requests is set to e-mails, not Off. Check that the Dashboard does not show customer data access as Not approved, because without Shopify’s approval the app cannot read order e-mails. Check that the delay since fulfillment has passed, that the order does not carry a skipped tag, and that the daily limit is not used up. Requests are checked once an hour. If you use your own Resend or Brevo key, check the key and the sender address with Send a test e-mail.

Which orders get a request?

Orders that have been fulfilled, after the delay you set. When you first switch e-mails on, only orders fulfilled in the last 14 days are considered, and orders older than 60 days never get one. Each order is asked once, plus the reminders you chose.

Can customers opt out?

Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link. It records a one-way fingerprint of the address so the person is not e-mailed again.

Can I reward customers for reviews?

You can offer a discount code for reviews that include a photo or video. The settings require a disclosure shown next to the review form, and reviews are published whether they are positive or negative.

Can I ask for reviews on the order status page?

Yes. In your Shopify admin go to Settings, Checkout, Customize, choose the Order status page, press Add block and choose Product review prompt, then save. The block lists the products of the order, each with a Write a review button that opens the review form for that product. You can type your own heading and tick Offer a video review too.

For a customer who is signed in to their account, the block lists the products that have shipped and the review is marked Verified buyer. For a customer who is not signed in, it lists the products of the order and the review is published without the badge. It needs the new customer accounts and is not available on the Shopify thank-you page.

The block works with Shopify only and sends nothing to our server. To tie a review to the order, it stores one random code on the customer’s own order in your store. If you installed the app before this block could do that, Shopify asks you to approve two new permissions for customer accounts when you update the app. Until you approve them, reviews written through the block are not marked Verified buyer.

The review prompt does not show on the order status page

Check four things. The block Product review prompt has been added to the Order status page in the checkout and accounts editor (Settings, Checkout, Customize) and saved. Something from the order has shipped: for a signed-in customer the block shows nothing before that, and it appears when the page is opened again after the shipment. The order is not cancelled. The product is active and on your online store, because a product without a page on your store gets no link.

Store reviews, product groups, sharing and integrations

Can customers review my store, not just a product?

Yes. In the theme editor add the Store reviews block to your home page or any other page. Customers press Write a store review and can add photos or a video, as they do for a product. You moderate store reviews in Reviews, with Review type set to Store reviews.

Store reviews are kept apart from product reviews. They never change a product’s star rating, and they do not appear in the review carousel, the photo wall, the trust badge or the Google Shopping feed. Review request e-mails ask for product reviews, so store reviews come from the block. A link to the page that ends in ?ugc_open=1 opens the form at once, which is useful for a QR code.

I added the Store reviews block but a store review does not show

Check that the review is Published in Reviews, and that the block is on the page you are looking at and the theme is saved. If you installed the app before store reviews existed, open the app once after the update: that prepares the place in your store where the block reads its data. If the numbers still look wrong, open the Dashboard, expand Maintenance and choose Recompute all ratings.

Can several products share the same reviews?

Yes, when they are the same item, for example one product per color or size. Open Settings, Product groups, press Add group, name it, add the products and save. Every product in the group then shows the reviews of all of them, with one star rating and one count for the group.

Nothing is copied or changed. Each review stays with the product it was written for and is labeled with that product on the others, for example “Reviewed: Blue ceramic mug”. A group needs at least 2 products and can hold up to 25, a product can be in one group, and you can have up to 100 groups. Questions and answers are not shared.

A grouped product still shows its old rating

After you save a group, the app updates the star ratings of the products involved, which takes a few seconds. If the page was closed before the banner Updating star ratings finished, open Settings, Product groups again and press Update star ratings again if it is offered. Otherwise open the Dashboard, expand Maintenance and choose Recompute all ratings.

Can shoppers share a review?

Yes. Every review on your store has a Share button. On a phone or tablet it opens the share sheet of the device. On a computer it opens a menu with Copy link, X, Facebook, WhatsApp, Pinterest (when the review has a photo) and E-mail. The link opens the product page at that review. The buttons are ordinary links: nothing from a social network is loaded on your store. To remove the button, untick Share button on reviews under Settings, Widget, Features.

You can share a review yourself too. Open a published review in Reviews: Share this review has the link, the same share buttons and an image of the review to download, as a square post or a story. The image shows the stars, words and name exactly as the customer wrote them.

Does it work with Klaviyo, Zapier or Make?

Yes. Open Settings, Integrations. The Webhook card sends review activity to any tool that accepts a webhook, such as Zapier, Make, Omnisend or your own server: enter an address that starts with https://, press Create signing secret, choose the events and save. The Klaviyo card sends events to your own Klaviyo account with a private API key you create there, as the metrics “ReviewFlow Review Submitted” and “ReviewFlow Review Request Sent”, so you can build flows on them. Each card has a Send test event button.

There are three events: a review is submitted, a question is asked, and a review request e-mail is sent. Each event is sent once, right after it happens. If your receiver is down or takes longer than 3 seconds, that event is not sent again.

What data is sent to Klaviyo or my webhook?

Nothing is sent until you turn an integration on. A webhook event carries the review (rating, title and text, whether it is published, whether the buyer is verified, whether it has a photo or video) or the question, and the product. For review request e-mails it carries the order number and the review links. It never contains a customer’s name, e-mail address or customer id.

Klaviyo attaches every event to an e-mail address, so Klaviyo events include the buyer’s e-mail address with the review’s rating, title, text and product, or with the order number and review links for a request that was sent. For that reason a review is sent to Klaviyo only when it came through an order link (the review request e-mail or the order status page). Reviews written directly on a product page are not sent to Klaviyo. ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC passes the address on and does not store it. See the privacy policy.

My webhook test fails

The test counts as delivered when your receiver answers with a 2xx status within 3 seconds. The address must start with https:// and be a public host name: an IP address, localhost or a private name is refused, and redirects are not followed, so enter the final address. The message under the button says what your receiver answered.

Nothing shows up in Klaviyo

Only verified reviews that came through an order link are sent to Klaviyo; a review written directly on a product page is not. Check that Send events to Klaviyo is ticked and saved, and that the key is a private API key with full access to Events. Press Send test event with your own address: if Klaviyo refuses the key, the page says so. An event can take a minute to appear on the profile.

AI help

Does ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC use AI?

Only when you press a button. Moderation with AI suggests what to do with pending reviews, and Draft a reply writes a first version of a reply. The text of the reviews involved is sent to the AI service for that request. Nothing is applied until you confirm it, and each store has a small daily allowance.

Your data and uninstalling

How do I uninstall, and what happens to my data?

Remove the ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC blocks in the theme editor if you like, then in your Shopify admin open Settings, Apps and sales channels, choose ReviewFlow: Reviews & UGC and uninstall.

Your reviews, questions and settings are stored in your own store. When an app is uninstalled, Shopify removes the app’s data definitions and keeps the entries for a period Shopify does not publish, so do not count on getting them back after a reinstall. Export what you want to keep before uninstalling.

On our side, the access token is removed at uninstall, and about 48 hours later Shopify asks us to erase what we still hold for the store. See the privacy policy.

How do I get a copy of, or delete, a shopper’s data?

Shopify sends us customer data and erasure requests. For an erasure request, that customer’s reviews and questions are kept but the name is replaced with Anonymous, the link to the person is removed and their photos and videos are deleted. You can also delete any review yourself in Reviews. See the privacy policy.
