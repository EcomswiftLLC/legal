Sunjar Chore Chart for Kids: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Sunjar (Chore Chart for Kids) is a parenting app made by Ecom Swift LLC for parents and caregivers raising children aged 3 to 12. It is used and set up by an adult. You don't need an account. Everything you enter about your family stays on your phone and is never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Sunjar stores on your phone

  • Children's profiles: the name or nickname, age (stored as an approximate birth month), avatar and color a parent chooses. No photos of children are needed, and no birthdays, schools or addresses are asked for.
  • Family activity: chores, routines, habits, stars, rewards, screen-time bank minutes, pretend allowance amounts and savings goals, feelings check-ins, value moments, reading log, life skills, milestones noticed, strengths journal notes, family meeting notes and planned activities.
  • Photo proof (optional): if a parent adds a photo to an approved chore, a downscaled copy is kept in the app's private folder. It is not saved to your gallery and is never shared unless you include it in a backup you make.
  • Settings: family values, goals, reminders, theme, currency symbol and similar choices.
  • Parent PIN: stored only as a salted, one-way hash. It is never included in backups.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Sunjar excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not leave your phone that way. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and Settings > Backup, restore and export > Delete all data removes it at any time.

Children's data

Children's information is entered by a parent, stays on the device, is never uploaded to us and is never used for advertising or ad personalisation. The supervised kid view shows no ads, no links to websites or other apps, no purchases and no text input, and it can only be left through a parent gate (your PIN, or a sum question for grown-ups).

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: "Save a backup file", "Share a backup" and "Save CSV" save or share a file to the place or app you pick, and sharing a weekly report, a family meeting note or the screen-time plan sends it to the app you pick. That place or app then handles it under its own privacy policy.

Pretend money

The allowance, jars and savings goals are pretend money for learning. Sunjar never moves real money, never connects to a bank and never asks for card or bank details.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on (daily chores, approvals waiting, routine start times, family meeting, value of the week, allowance day) and the screen-time timer. Asked only when you switch a reminder on. Children's names are hidden on the lock screen.
  • Run at startup: so reminders you turned on come back after the phone restarts.
  • Camera and photos: Sunjar asks for no camera or storage permission. When you add photo proof, Android's own camera app or photo picker opens, and only the picture you choose is copied into the app.

Sunjar asks for no contacts, location, microphone or storage permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Sunjar is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob in the parent part of the app, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Banners appear only on the parent Home screen and the Learn list, never in chores, rewards setup, reports or the kid view. There are no full-screen ads between screens. A rewarded video plays only when a parent chooses to watch one, to unlock a single Premium item. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Nothing you enter about your family is ever shared with the ad service.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline. Purchases are only offered in the parent part of the app.

Ratings

At a calm moment in the parent view, for example after a week of chores done or your first weekly report, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens. The kid view never asks for a rating.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Sunjar has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

General information, not advice

Articles, activities, stories, milestones and calm-down ideas are general information for families, written in our own words from widely published public guidance. They are not medical, psychological or developmental advice. Every child develops differently; talk with your child's doctor or another professional if you are worried.

Who the app is for

Sunjar is made for adults: parents and caregivers. It is not directed at children, and children do not create accounts or send any information. Children may use the supervised kid view on a parent's phone, where no data leaves the device and no ads are shown.

Changes to this policy
