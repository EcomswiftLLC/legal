Professional PDF Editor Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Publisher: Ecom Swift LLC · support@ecomswiftllc.com

Professional PDF Editor is an extension for Google Chrome. This policy describes exactly what the extension does with data. It is written from the implemented code, not from a template.

The short version

  • Your documents are opened and processed inside the browser on your own computer. The extension has no server and does not upload your files, text, signatures, passwords or page contents.
  • It stores your settings, saved signatures (only if you save one), your Premium status (the email you paid with, the plan and its end date) or an optional license key, and optionally your own AI key, in the extension's local storage on your computer. Downloaded OCR language files are kept in the browser's IndexedDB. Nothing is synced.
  • There is no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting code.
  • The AI tools use Chrome's built-in AI by default, which runs on your computer and sends nothing.
  • Three optional features contact the internet: unlocking Premium, downloading an OCR language, and (only if you choose your own key instead of the built-in AI) sending text to the AI provider you picked. None runs unless you start it, and the last two ask you on screen first.
  • The extension loads and runs only code that ships inside its package. It downloads no code.

Single purpose

Work with PDF files: edit, sign, organize, convert and read them on your own computer.

Data we collect

  • Personal information: only if you buy or unlock Premium. The email address you type to unlock it is sent to the Ecom Swift LLC website (www.ecomswiftllc.com) to look up your paid plan, and is kept on your device. It is not used for anything else, not sold, and the extension sends no marketing.
  • The extension does not collect health, financial, authentication or location data, web history, user activity or website content.
  • We do not sell or transfer user data to third parties, and we do not use or transfer data for purposes unrelated to the single purpose above.
  • The extension uses no remote code.

Your AI key and consent

  • The key is never part of the package. It is held in memory unless you tick "Remember the key", then it is stored in chrome.storage.local of this browser profile (not synced).
  • Consent is recorded per provider host, can be withdrawn on the AI page, and "Delete saved settings, signatures, AI key and OCR languages" in Settings removes everything above.

Retention and deletion

Everything lives on your device. Removing the extension deletes it. Settings has a button to delete saved data without removing the extension (the Premium status is kept; remove it on the Premium page).

Children

Professional PDF Editor is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the "last updated" date will change. Any future feature that sends data off your device will be optional and disclosed here and in the store listing before it ships.
