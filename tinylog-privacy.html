Mooncot Baby Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Mooncot (Newborn Baby Tracker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC for parents and caregivers. You don't need an account to use it. Everything you record about your baby is stored on your phone and is never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Mooncot stores on your phone

  • Baby profiles: the name or nickname, birth date, due date (if your baby was born early), optional sex (used only to choose the WHO growth chart), optional photo, and the emergency card details you enter (blood type, allergies, conditions, doctor, clinic and emergency contacts).
  • Logs: feeds, sleep, diapers, pumping, medicines and vitamins with the dose you type, temperatures, symptoms, baths, tummy time, outdoor time, mood and custom entries, with the caregiver name you chose on this phone.
  • Health records: growth measurements, vaccine doses you mark as given, appointments and question lists, and your medicine list.
  • Memories: milestones, teeth, firsts and photo journal entries with captions. Photos you add are copied into the app's private storage and re-saved without location or camera details.
  • Settings: units, reminders, night mode, theme, caregiver names and running timers.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Mooncot excludes this data from Android cloud backups. If you move to a new phone with Android's direct phone-to-phone transfer, the transfer may copy it to your new phone; that transfer goes straight between your devices. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and Settings, Delete all data removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to. "Back up" creates one file with your logs and photos; "Paediatrician report" creates a PDF; "Export spreadsheet" creates a CSV; memory cards and collages create an image; the caregiver summary and emergency card create a text. Each is saved or shared to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker or share sheet, and that place or app then handles it under its own privacy policy. Mooncot does not connect to any health service or cloud.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked when you switch on a reminder, for reminders you choose and for the notification shown while a feed, sleep, tummy time or pumping timer runs. You can decline.
  • Run at start-up: after the phone restarts, Mooncot shows the running timer notifications again. Reminders are kept by Android's WorkManager.
  • Foreground service (media playback): used only while a soothing sound you started is playing, so it can continue with the screen off. A notification with a Stop button is shown.

Mooncot asks for no camera, microphone, location, contacts or storage permission. Photos come from Android's photo picker or your camera app, only when you choose to add one. The app uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Mooncot is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your baby's information, logs, photos and health records are never shared with the ad service and are never used for ad personalisation. Banners appear only on the Today, Insights and Learn list screens and never in night mode. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished log, export or article, limited in number and spacing, never at night, never during a feeding timer and never over a logging, growth or health screen. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to unlock a Premium extra once.

Mooncot is made for adults. It is not directed at children and does not join the Designed for Families program. You can change your ad consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after a week of logging or your first milestone, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. It never asks during a timer or in night mode. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Mooncot has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health information

Mooncot is an organiser and a source of general information, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice. Growth percentiles use the public WHO Child Growth Standards as a reference; vaccine schedules are simplified summaries of public schedules; medicine doses are only what you enter.

Changes to this policy
