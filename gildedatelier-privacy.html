Gilded Atelier: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Gilded Atelier is a single-player item-merging game made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to play. We don't run a server that collects your game data, and we don't sell personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and settings: your board, items, collection, orders, level, currencies, daily-gift streak, theme, and sound and haptic settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Plan status: the app remembers which membership Google Play last confirmed, so it keeps working briefly without a connection.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears only on the home screen, never on the board. Full-screen ads appear only after a completed order and are limited in number, and rewarded videos appear only when you choose to watch one. Members of Gilded Pass or Royal Pass see no ads.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices. Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Memberships and purchases

Gilded Pass and Royal Pass are subscriptions handled by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that a plan is active, so the game can unlock the features included. We never see your payment details. See the Terms of Use for billing and cancellation.

Ratings

The game may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Children

Gilded Atelier is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
