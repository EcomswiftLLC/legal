Privacy policy

PixelPress: Compress, Resize & Convert Images for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 31 October 2026.

In short

  • PixelPress converts, resizes and compresses your images on your PC. It never uploads an image, a file name or any other content to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • PixelPress itself makes no internet connections. The only online parts are Microsoft Store purchases and licence checks, which Windows and the Microsoft Store handle, and pages that open in your browser or the Microsoft Store app when you click a link.

What the app reads

PixelPress reads the image files and folders you add (by dropping, choosing, pasting or "Open with"), and, when you turn on a watched folder (Pro), new image files that appear in the folder you chose. It reads the pixels to convert them and the metadata inside the files (for example EXIF camera details, dates and GPS location) to show you what a file contains and to keep or remove that information as you choose. It also asks Windows which image formats Windows can decode on this PC. Nothing it reads is sent anywhere.

What the app writes

PixelPress writes the converted images where you tell it to: a folder you choose, next to the originals, or in place of the originals. When you choose to replace originals, or to replace an existing file with the same name, the old file is first moved to the Windows Recycle Bin. Converted files contain only the metadata you chose to keep; by default all metadata is removed.

What the app stores, and where

PixelPress keeps a small amount of data in its own app data folder on your PC: your settings, your presets, your watched-folder rules and their activity log, the history of runs (file paths, file sizes, image dimensions and outcomes, used for the History screen and to resume a run that was cut short), and log files that help diagnose problems (your Windows user name and profile path are removed from them). Images you paste are saved there as PNG files so they can be converted. A saved licence check is stored there, encrypted for your Windows account, so Pro keeps working offline for a limited time. You can clear the history in Settings. Uninstalling PixelPress removes this folder.

Purchases

PixelPress Pro is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons (monthly or yearly subscriptions and a one-time purchase). The Microsoft Store handles payment, renewal, refunds and cancellation under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. PixelPress never sees your payment details. To check your plan, PixelPress asks Windows' Store service for the licences of your Microsoft account; Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your account details.

Background helper

If you turn on a watched folder (Pro), a small background part of PixelPress runs while at least one watched folder is on, and can start when you sign in to Windows. It only watches the folders you chose and converts new images there. You can turn this off in PixelPress or in Windows Settings, Apps, Startup.

Children

PixelPress is a general utility, does not collect personal information and has no communication features.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
