Changelog

What's new

1.1 - October 22, 2026

  • New order weight limit (minimum or maximum, in kilograms).
  • Search, multi-select with pause, resume and delete, and private notes on the rules lists.
  • Rule health panel on Home that points out contradicting limits, rules that block every checkout and rules that have ended.

1.0 - October 22, 2026

  • Order rules: minimum and maximum quantity, order value, number of products, order multiples, and blocking rules, with your own message in 12 languages.
  • Payment rules: hide, rename and reorder payment methods.
  • Delivery rules: hide, rename and reorder delivery options.
  • Conditions on the cart, customer, address, language and products, with a schedule.
  • 23 templates, a Test a cart page, an activity log with undo, and export and import.
  • Optional storefront helpers for quantity boxes and the cart.
