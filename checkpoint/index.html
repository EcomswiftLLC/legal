Help for RuleGate: Orders & Checkout

RuleGate: Orders & Checkout lets you set order limits, block orders that don't qualify, and hide, rename or reorder payment and delivery options at checkout. It is free.

In short

  1. Open the app from your Shopify admin and pick a template, or build a rule from scratch.
  2. Test it on the Test a cart page. Nothing is ordered.
  3. Turn on the storefront helpers (optional) so quantity boxes and the cart explain your limits.

Contact us

Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com with your store address (the one ending in myshopify.com), what you were trying to do and what you saw. A screenshot helps. We aim to reply within one business day, Monday to Friday.
