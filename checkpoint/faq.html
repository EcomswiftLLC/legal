FAQ

Questions and answers

Is RuleGate: Orders &amp; Checkout really free?

Yes. There are no plans, no limits on the number of rules from us and no setup fee.

Which Shopify plans work?

Order rules, payment rules and delivery rules run as Shopify Functions on Shopify's current checkout. If your store still uses the old checkout.liquid checkout, you need to upgrade first.

A rule doesn't seem to apply. What should I check?

Open Test a cart and describe the cart. Check the rule is Active, that its dates and weekdays include today, and that its conditions are true. Address conditions only apply once the shopper has entered an address. Then check the rule's Space used bar isn't full: a rule that can't be saved is never half-applied.

Why can a shopper add one item below my minimum order value?

A minimum can't be met before the shopper has added anything, so minimums, multiples and order value are checked at checkout. Only maximum quantities stop a shopper in the cart.

Where do shoppers see my message?

At the top of checkout, above the contact section, in the shopper's language when you filled it in. Shoppers can't continue until the cart or address meets the rules.

Can I use different messages for different languages?

Yes, in the rule editor under Message shoppers see. Twelve languages are supported. A language you leave empty falls back to a built-in message, then to English.

What does 'Add them up' versus 'Check each product' mean?

For a quantity rule on several products, add them up means the total across all matching products must meet the limit. Check each product means every matching product is checked on its own.

Can I hide a payment method for one country?

Yes. Create a payment rule that hides the method, and add a condition for the delivery country. Country conditions apply once the shopper has entered their address.

Why is a delivery move skipped?

Shopify requires the cheapest shipping option to stay first. A rule that would put a more expensive shipping option in front of it is skipped. Pickup options can go first.

Does RuleGate: Orders &amp; Checkout change my theme?

Only if you turn on the optional storefront helpers app embed. It's a theme app extension, so nothing is written into your theme files, and turning it off removes it completely.

What happens to my rules if I uninstall?

The rules are stored in your own store as app data and are removed with the app. Export your rules from Settings first if you want to keep a copy.

Does RuleGate: Orders &amp; Checkout see my customers' data?

Only what Shopify gives a checkout Function while it runs, for example the cart, the customer's tags and number of orders, and the delivery address. This happens inside Shopify and nothing is sent to us. See the privacy policy.

How do I get help?

Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com with your store address and what you saw. We reply within one business day, Monday to Friday.
