Calculator & Converter: Abaqo: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Abaqo (Calculator & Converter: Abaqo) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Every calculation, conversion and finance result is worked out on your phone. Your history stays on your phone. We don't run a server that receives your calculations, and we don't sell personal information.

What Abaqo stores on your phone

  • History: each calculation you finish with =, its result, the time, and any pin or note you add. It is kept in the app's private storage, up to 2,000 lines.
  • Memory and settings: the memory value, the last answer, the line you were typing, number format, angle unit, theme, keypad options, the answer you gave in the short setup (for example "Money and loans"), and a few counters used to space out ads, the rating request and Premium offers.
  • Currency rates: a copy of the European Central Bank's daily reference rates file, so conversion works offline.

Abaqo does not read your contacts, messages, photos, files or location. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Delete all my data" in Settings removes it at any time.

Currency rates

When you open the currency converter, Abaqo downloads the public daily reference rates file from the European Central Bank (www.ecb.europa.eu), at most every few hours. No personal data is sent with this request; like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the ECB's server. The rates are shown with their date and are for information only: banks and card providers use their own rates and fees.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: copying a result, sharing a result or a summary, exporting your history or a loan schedule as a spreadsheet (CSV), and sharing the app. These go to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, and that app handles them under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for the currency rates file, ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Vibrate: for key-press feedback, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Abaqo asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission.

Advertising

Abaqo is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your calculations and history are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear at the end of the converter, tools, history and settings screens, never on the calculator keypad. A full-screen ad may appear when you go back to the tools list after finishing a result, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to use a Premium tool for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly with a free trial, or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them. During a free trial the app may show a note on your phone about when the trial ends; it never sends push notifications about it.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after a number of calculations or an export, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Abaqo has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Accuracy and advice

Abaqo uses exact decimal arithmetic and published conversion factors, and its results are tested against known values. Finance tools show estimates based on the numbers you enter; projected returns assume a steady rate. Results are not financial, tax, legal or medical advice.

Children

Abaqo is a general-audience tool. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
