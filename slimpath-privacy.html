Wellpace Weight Loss Workout: Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Wellpace (Weight Loss Workout - Wellpace) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your workouts, food diary, weight and photos are stored on your phone and never leave it. We don't run a server that receives your health or fitness data, and we don't sell personal information. None of the data described below is collected by Ecom Swift LLC.

Your data stays on your phone

  • What the app keeps: the answers you give when you set up your profile (for example age, sex, height, weight, goal weight, activity level and preferences), your food diary, custom foods, recipes and meal plans, your weight entries and body measurements, your progress photos, your intermittent fasting sessions, water entries, steps you type in, sleep and mood notes, habits, your workout history and ratings, your reminder times and your settings.
  • Where it is kept: only in the app's private storage on your phone. Calorie targets, BMI and trends are calculated on your phone. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • No account: there is no sign-up, no sign-in and no profile on any server.
  • No cloud backup: the app is set so that Android's automatic cloud backup (android:allowBackup) is turned off. Your data is not copied to Google Drive or any other cloud by the system on our behalf.
  • No health platforms and no sensors: the app does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or any wearable. It reads no sensors. Steps are numbers you type in yourself.
  • Backups and exports you start yourself: if you save a backup file or export your diary and weight history as CSV, the app writes a file to the place you pick (for example your Downloads folder, a cloud drive or an email). Restore reads only the file you pick. If you share a workout card image, it goes to the app you choose. The app never sends these files anywhere by itself, and once you hand a file to another app or service, that service's own privacy policy applies.
  • Progress photos are private: photos you add are stored in the app's private storage, not in your gallery. They are not uploaded. You choose a photo with Android's system photo picker, which gives the app only the pictures you select, so the app needs no storage permission. If you take a new photo, the app opens your phone's camera app through the system camera intent, and the picture is handed back to the app. Wellpace does not hold the camera permission itself.
  • Deleting your data: Settings > Erase all my data deletes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app removes it too.

Health data is never sent to advertisers or anyone else

Your weight, food, measurements, fasting, workouts and photos are never given to the ad service, to Google Play, to us or to any other third party. They are not used to choose or target ads.

Notifications and the workout timer

Wellpace asks for notification permission only when you turn on reminders. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. While a workout is running, the app shows an ongoing notification through a foreground service (type: health), so the timer and voice coach keep running with the screen off. The service only keeps the workout timer going. It reads no sensors, and the notification ends when the workout ends. If you decline notifications, the app still works.

Other permissions

Wellpace asks for no contacts, microphone, location, storage or body sensor permission. The voice coach uses the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone and does not listen. The app uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases. Workouts, the food database, recipes and your diary work offline.

Advertising

Wellpace shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads, in which case there are no ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). You can change your choice later in Settings, under ad privacy choices, where it is shown.

AdMob may process your device's advertising ID, device and usage data (such as device model, operating system, language, app version and how you interact with ads) and your approximate location derived from your IP address, to serve, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where consent is required, this depends on the choices you make in the consent form. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

A small banner can appear on the Today, Progress and Settings screens. A full-screen ad appears only after you finish a workout, create a meal plan or save a backup, and it is limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, and it unlocks one Premium item once. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience (PG rated).

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Wellpace has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Food data attribution

Food nutrient data: U.S. Department of Agriculture, Agricultural Research Service. FoodData Central (FNDDS 2021-2023 and SR Legacy 2018). fdc.nal.usda.gov. Public domain (CC0 1.0). The USDA does not endorse this app.

Health note

Wellpace is a wellness and fitness app and is not a medical device. Calorie targets and timelines are estimates. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise or eating plan.

Children

Wellpace is not for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. People aged 13 to 17 get a habits mode with no weight or calorie targets, and intermittent fasting is not offered to anyone under 18. Ads in the app are limited to PG-rated content.
