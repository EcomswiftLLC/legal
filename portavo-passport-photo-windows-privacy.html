Portavo Privacy Policy

Passport Photo Maker: Portavo for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Effective 2026-11-23.

Summary

Portavo works on your PC. Faces are found and backgrounds removed by models that run inside the app on your computer. Your photos and faces are never uploaded by Portavo. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and Portavo does not analyse your photos for anyone but you.

Data stored on your PC

  • The photos you open (a working copy of each), the cutout of the person, your edits and the document you chose, in the app's data folder, so your people are there next time.
  • Your settings, favourite documents and the add-on licence cache from Microsoft Store.
  • Files you export (photos, digital files, print sheets, ZIP files and backups) are saved only where you choose.

You can remove a person at any time in the app, and everything is removed when you uninstall the app. Files you saved yourself are kept.

Camera

If you choose to take a photo with the camera, Portavo shows the live video on your PC and keeps only the one picture you take. Video is not recorded or sent anywhere and the microphone is never used. Windows privacy settings control camera access.

Network use

  • Microsoft Store: to check purchases, show prices, process purchases and open the rating page, the app uses Microsoft Store services. Payments are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your card details.
  • Official pages: Portavo opens a government page about photo rules in your browser only when you choose to. That website has its own privacy policy.
  • Nothing else: Portavo does not send your photos, faces, files or usage data to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

Children

Parents can make photos of their children with Portavo. Those photos stay on the PC like all others. Portavo does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Not affiliated with any government

Portavo builds photos to the published size and layout rules of each document. The office that issues the document decides whether it accepts a photo.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. Questions: support@ecomswiftllc.com, Ecom Swift LLC.
