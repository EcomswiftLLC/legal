Privacy Policy — CSS Inspector & Selector Copier

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

CSS Inspector & Selector Copier lets you hover an element on a page to get a unique CSS selector and its computed styles. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you click "Inspect an element", the extension runs a small highlighter on the current tab. When you click an element, it builds a CSS selector and reads that element's computed styles in your browser, copies the selector to your clipboard, and stores the capture locally so the popup can show it.
  • It only acts on the tab you choose to inspect (activeTab), and only when you start it. It does not run in the background or on pages you don't inspect.
  • The selector, styles and any export are produced and stored entirely on your device. Nothing is sent to us or any third party.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — only to run the highlighter on the tab you choose, when you press "Inspect an element", to read the clicked element's selector and computed styles.
  • storage — to save the last captured element and your settings locally.
  • alarms — only for the optional reminder below.
  • host access (ecomswiftllc.com) — only to verify a Pro purchase (license check). No other hosts.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages or elements you inspect, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
