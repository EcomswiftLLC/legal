Using CCWasher safely

You choose what is cleaned. Every scan shows what it found before anything is removed, and the app only cleans a fixed list of cache, temporary and log folders and refuses protected places such as Windows, Program Files and your personal folders. Your own files are moved to the Recycle Bin. Even so, deleting files cannot always be undone: emptying the Recycle Bin, privacy cleaning and cache deletion are permanent. Review the list before you clean and keep backups of important files. Uninstalling apps runs each app's own uninstaller; the result depends on that uninstaller.

What CCWasher does not promise

CCWasher reports what Windows and your drives report, and the sizes it measured. It does not promise that your PC will become faster, and it does not include a registry cleaner or memory booster. Disk health values come from Windows and your drive's own reporting and are not a guarantee about the drive's future.
