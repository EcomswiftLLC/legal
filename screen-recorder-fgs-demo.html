Screen Recorder & Screenshot: Foreground Service

Ecom Swift LLC. Demonstration for the Google Play foreground service declaration.

Video 1: media projection and microphone

  1. The user opens the app and taps Start recording.
  2. Android shows its own screen capture consent dialog and the user accepts it.
  3. After the countdown, recording starts. A notification with Pause, Stop and Screenshot stays visible for the whole recording. This is the FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION service. When the user chose a microphone option the notification reads "With Microphone" (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE).
  4. The user taps Stop. The video is saved on the device and plays back in the app gallery.

Video 2: optional facecam (camera)

  1. The optional Premium facecam shows the front camera in a small draggable bubble on screen while a recording that the user started is running (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA).
  2. The camera is only used while the user's recording is running. Nothing is uploaded; recordings stay on the device.
