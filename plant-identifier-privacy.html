Privacy Policy — Plant Identifier

Last updated: 29 September 2026 · Publisher: Ecom Swift LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how the Plant Identifier Android app ("the app", "we") handles information. We built the app to respect your privacy: plant identification runs entirely on your device.

Information we do not collect

  • Your photos. Photos you take or upload are processed on your device by an on-device model and are never uploaded to us or any third party.
  • Accounts. The app has no login and does not collect your name, email, contacts, or precise location.

Information collected by third parties

The free version of the app shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and process data such as device identifiers, approximate (non-precise) location, IP address, and app-usage signals. This processing is governed by Google's policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data (AdMob)

If you buy the one-time Remove Ads option, no ads are shown and the ad SDK stops requesting them. The purchase is processed by Google Play Billing; we never see your payment details.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalization in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). Where required, an in-app consent prompt is shown before personalized ads are served.

Data security & retention

Because the app does not collect personal data or upload your photos, there is no personal data for us to store or retain. App settings (such as your Remove Ads status) are stored only on your device.

Changes

We may update this policy; the "Last updated" date will change accordingly.
