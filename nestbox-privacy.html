Home Inventory: Nestbox: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Nestbox (Home Inventory: Nestbox) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your rooms, items, photos, receipts and jobs are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your inventory, and we don't sell personal information.

What Nestbox stores on your phone

  • Your inventory: the rooms, items, names, brands, models, serial numbers, prices, values, purchase dates, stores, warranty dates, tags, notes and maintenance jobs you enter. They are kept in a database in the app's private storage.
  • Your photos and files: pictures and PDFs you attach to an item, such as a photo of the item, a receipt or a manual. They are copied into the app's private storage, so they stay even if you delete the original from your gallery.
  • Settings: currency, theme, reminder time, warranty lead time and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

Nestbox does not read your contacts, messages, location or microphone. It has no camera permission: when you choose "Take a photo", Android's own camera app opens and hands the picture back. Photos and files are only added through Android's photo picker, camera app and file picker, and only the ones you pick. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Delete all my data" in Settings removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: sharing a spreadsheet (CSV), an insurance record (PDF), a QR label sheet or a QR picture, saving a backup file, restoring one, and sharing the app. These go to the app or folder you pick in Android's share sheet or file picker, and that app or place handles them under its own privacy policy. The insurance record and spreadsheet contain what you entered, which can include serial numbers and values, so share them only with people you trust.

A backup file is encrypted with AES-256 using a password you choose, and it includes your items, photos and receipts. We cannot recover the password. If you forget it, the backup cannot be opened by us or by anyone else.

A QR label carries only a nestbox:// link made of a random identifier. It does not contain the name, price or serial number of the item. Scanning it with a phone that has no matching Nestbox inventory shows nothing.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for warranty and maintenance reminders and the occasional backup reminder. Android asks when you first add something with a date, and you can decline. Reminders may arrive a few minutes early or late.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Nestbox plans your reminders again.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Nestbox asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission, and it does not use exact alarms.

Advertising

Nestbox is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your item names, photos, serial numbers and values are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear only on the main Home, Items and Care screens, never in a form, a PDF or a backup. A full-screen ad may appear on the Home screen after you finish an export or a backup, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature for a day or to add room for more items.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have added ten items, finished your first export or completed a second job, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while you are filling in a form.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Nestbox has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Values and advice

Nestbox records what you enter. It does not appraise your belongings and is not legal, tax or insurance advice. Check with your insurer what it needs for a claim.

Children

Nestbox is a general-audience tool for adults managing a household. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
