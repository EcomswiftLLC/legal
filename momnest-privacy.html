Mamacove Pregnancy Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Mamacove (Pregnancy Tracker - Mamacove) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your pregnancy, postpartum and health entries are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Mamacove stores on your phone

  • Profile and dates: the mode you choose (pregnant, after birth, or trying to conceive), your due date and how it was calculated (last period and cycle length, conception, scan or IVF transfer date), your baby's birth date, an optional name or nickname for you and your baby, units, and whether you expect twins.
  • Health logs you enter: kick counts, contraction times, weight, height and weight before pregnancy, blood pressure, blood glucose, symptoms, moods, sleep, water, feeding times, and notes.
  • Plans and lists: vitamins and medicines you add yourself and when you marked them taken, appointments and your questions, checklists such as the hospital bag, your birth plan choices, favourite baby names, and completed movement sessions.
  • Belly photos: photos you take or pick for the photo diary are copied into the app's private storage. They are not added to your gallery and are not included in backups.
  • Settings: reminders, theme, color and similar choices.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Mamacove is excluded from Android's cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so your health data is not copied to a Google account by Android. Uninstalling the app removes everything. Settings > Delete all my data removes it at any time, and Settings > Delete all belly photos removes the photos.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: "Back up my data", "Export logs as a spreadsheet", the appointment report and the birth plan PDF create a file that you share or save to a place you pick in Android's share sheet or file picker. "Share with your partner", the birth plan text and the baby name shortlist send a message through the app you pick. That place or app then handles the file or message under its own privacy policy. Mamacove does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or any other health service, and has no community or chat.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on a reminder (weekly update, kick count, water, daily check-in, medicines or appointments). Notification text stays hidden on the lock screen until your phone is unlocked.
  • Camera and photos: Mamacove does not request the camera or storage permission. "Take photo" opens your phone's own camera app, and "Choose from gallery" uses Android's photo picker, which shares only the photo you select.

Mamacove asks for no location, contacts, microphone or storage permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Mamacove is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your pregnancy details and health entries are never shared with the ad service and are never used to choose ads. Banners appear only on the Today screen and the Learn list. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished action, such as reading an article to the end or finishing a movement session, limited in number and spacing. Ads never appear on the kick counter, the contraction timer, health logs or any screen where you enter health information. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after your hospital bag is packed or after your first week with the app, Mamacove may ask whether you are enjoying it. It never asks while you use a health tool. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Mamacove has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health information

Mamacove offers general information and organisation tools. It is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, predict or prevent any condition, does not interpret your readings and does not recommend medicines or doses. Due dates, baby sizes and weight ranges are estimates. Always follow the advice of your midwife, doctor or other health professional, and contact them or your local emergency number if you are worried about you or your baby.

Children

Mamacove is made for adults who are pregnant, recently gave birth or are planning a pregnancy. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Information about a baby (such as a name or birth date) is entered by the parent and stays on the parent's phone.
