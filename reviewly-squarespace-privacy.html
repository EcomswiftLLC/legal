Privacy Policy - Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews is a product reviews service published by Ecom Swift LLC. A store owner ("the merchant") adds one line of code to their Squarespace site. Shoppers on that site can then read and write product reviews, with photos and short videos. The merchant can also connect their Squarespace store so the service can read orders and mark real buyers. This policy explains what data the service handles, where it lives and why.

Short version. We store the merchant's account details, the reviews shoppers write (including any photos or short videos they add) and, if the merchant connects Squarespace, the buyers found in their orders. We use no cookies, no advertising and no analytics trackers, and we never sell data. A shopper's IP address is turned into a salted one-way code that is used only to limit spam. The merchant decides what happens to the reviews on their store.

1. Who we are

The service is published by Ecom Swift LLC. Contact us at squarespace@ecomswiftllc.com. For the review content on a store, the merchant is the data controller and Ecom Swift LLC acts as their processor.

An email address typed into a review form is never shown publicly. It is visible only to the merchant. Emails to shoppers are sent only if the merchant connects their own email account (Brevo or Resend) and switches automatic emails on. Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews then builds each email (a review request, up to two reminders, a note when the merchant replies to a review, and thank-you messages after a review or an order) and hands it to the merchant's email provider with the merchant's own API key, so it goes out under the merchant's sender name. For this it uses the customer's email address, first name and the product from the order, or the email address typed into the review form for a thank-you after a review or a note about the merchant's reply. Every email carries an unsubscribe link, and a person who unsubscribes is not emailed again by that store. The API key is stored encrypted and is never shown again. The email provider processes the message under its own terms.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies. The review widget may keep a short-lived copy of review data in the shopper's browser session storage so a page does not reload the same data repeatedly. It is cleared when the browser tab is closed.

4. Where data lives and who sees it

Data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers and D1) on its global network. The merchant can see all reviews on their store, including shopper emails. Reviews are shown publicly on the merchant's store only after the merchant's moderation rules allow it. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers and we do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Reviews are kept until the merchant deletes them or deletes their account. Deleting an account in the dashboard permanently removes the account and all of its reviews. Salted IP hashes, photos and videos are removed together with the review they belong to. If the merchant disconnects Squarespace, uninstalls the extension or lets the connection lapse, the saved access tokens and the buyer list are deleted; reviews stay.

6. Your choices and rights

Merchants can export every review as CSV or JSON, edit or delete any review, disconnect Squarespace and delete their saved order data, and delete their whole account at any time. A shopper who wants a review changed or removed should contact the store that published it, or write to us at the address above and we will help route or act on the request. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We honor such requests.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Admin keys are stored only as one-way hashes. Review text is displayed as plain text so it cannot run code on a store, and uploaded photos and videos are checked by their file contents and served only as images or video. We limit submissions per network and filter obvious spam, but no system is perfectly secure.

8. Children

The service is for store owners and their adult customers and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the dashboard.
