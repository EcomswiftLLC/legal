Movera: Workout Foreground Service

Ecom Swift LLC · demonstration for the Google Play foreground-service declaration

What the video shows

  1. The video starts with a workout already running, which the user started by tapping Start on the day's workout. The player shows the animated exercise, its name and a countdown timer. The app runs the workout foreground service (type health).
  2. The workout moves from the exercise to a rest period with the next exercise previewed. Timer and exercise continue without the user touching the phone.
  3. The notification shade shows the Movera Workout · Day 1: Full body notification with the current step, a progress bar and Pause, +20 s and Next actions.
  4. The user returns to the app and the workout continues in the foreground.

Why a foreground service is required

A guided workout is a timed sequence of exercises with optional spoken cues. It has to keep running when the user locks the phone or switches app, otherwise the timer stops in the middle of an exercise, and it cannot be paused and resumed by the system without breaking the session. The work runs in WorkoutService, a foreground service of type health (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH). It is started only when the user taps Start on a workout, always shows a notification with Pause, +20 s and Next, and stops when the workout ends or the user ends it. The service reads no sensors and sends no data anywhere; everything stays on the device.

How to reproduce in the app

  1. Open Movera and tap Start on the workout for the day, then Start now.
  2. Lock the screen or press Home, then pull down the notification shade to see the workout notification.
  3. Return to the app and tap the close button, then End workout, to stop the service.
