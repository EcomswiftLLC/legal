WorkZen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Publisher: Ecom Swift LLC · support@ecomswiftllc.com

WorkZen is a productivity extension for Google Chrome. This policy describes exactly what the extension does with data. It is written from the implemented code, not from a template.

The short version

  • WorkZen has no account, no server and no analytics.
  • Everything you enter stays in your own Chrome profile on your own device.
  • We never receive, see, sell or share your tasks, notes, time entries or settings.
  • WorkZen does not read the websites you visit or your browsing history.

What WorkZen stores, and where

WorkZen saves the following in chrome.storage.local, which lives inside your Chrome profile on your computer:

  • Tasks (title, notes, due date and time, priority, status, project, tags, estimate)
  • Projects (name and colour)
  • Focus sessions and time entries (start, end, duration, linked task title)
  • Planner time blocks
  • Your settings (timer lengths, reminder preferences, theme, optional display name, daily goal)
  • Internal state: the running timer, the install time, and flags for onboarding, the 48-hour recap and the review request

This data is not synced by WorkZen. If you use Chrome sync, chrome.storage.local is not synced by Chrome either. The data is not encrypted by WorkZen beyond the protection your operating system and Chrome profile provide. Anyone with access to your unlocked computer and Chrome profile can read it.

Data that leaves your device

By default, none.

The only network activity is a page that opens when you click a link: the Chrome Web Store review page (once configured), this privacy policy, our website, or a mail or WhatsApp link. Those pages are run by their own operators under their own policies. WorkZen sends them nothing from your data.

WorkZen loads no remote code, fonts, scripts or images. Fonts are bundled inside the extension.

Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhy
storageSaves your tasks, time and settings on your device.
alarmsWakes the extension at the exact moment a timer ends or a reminder is due, even when the popup is closed.
notificationsShows timer, reminder and recap notifications. You can switch every notification off in Settings.
offscreenPlays the short chime when a timer ends. Chrome requires an offscreen document for sound from a background worker. It plays a locally generated tone. Nothing is recorded.

WorkZen requests no host permissions. It cannot access any website's content.

Your controls

  • Export all your data as a JSON file in Settings.
  • Import a previously exported file (merge keeps your existing records, replace swaps them after confirmation).
  • Delete all your data, or reset WorkZen completely, in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the extension removes all of its stored data from Chrome.
  • Turn off notifications, sounds and each kind of reminder in Settings.

Engagement messages

About 48 hours after you first use WorkZen it can show a one-time recap built from your own local records. It can then invite you to leave a Chrome Web Store review. You can choose "Not now" or "Don't ask again". Premium plans are described honestly as upcoming. No purchase is possible in this version, and WorkZen never collects payment details.

Children

WorkZen is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at the same address and the "last updated" date will change. If a future version adds any feature that sends data off your device, that feature will be optional and will be disclosed here and in the store listing before it ships.
