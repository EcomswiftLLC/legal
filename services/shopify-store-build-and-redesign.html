How the service runs

Four clear steps.

Step 1

Brief and review

We learn your products, customers and goals, and review your current store if you have one.

Step 2

Written scope

You approve the pages, features, package and timeline in writing.

Step 3

Build on a preview

Step 4

Review and launch

You approve the final result, then we publish and check everything on the live store.

Your control

Your store stays yours.

  • Access is through Shopify collaborator permissions, limited to the agreed scope.

Questions

Common questions.

Do I need a Shopify plan before you start?

Yes, you need a Shopify store, which can be on a trial or development plan while we build. We can guide you through creating one.

How long does a store build take?

Most Launch builds take one to two weeks once we have your products and brand assets. The written scope gives the exact timeline.

Will you migrate products from another platform?

Yes. We can import products, collections and customers from other platforms. Large migrations are scoped separately.

Who owns the theme and content?

You do. Everything we build is yours when the project is delivered.

Can you redesign my live store without downtime?

Yes. We work on a preview copy and publish only when you approve, so customers see no interruption.

What happens after launch?

You get a handover guide, and we offer an ongoing care plan if you want us to keep the store healthy.
