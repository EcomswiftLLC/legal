Questions

Common questions.

Is the audit really free?

Yes. It is a written review of your store with named issues. You decide afterwards whether you want us to fix them.

Will my sales go up?

We do not promise it. We remove what stands in the way of orders and tell you honestly what we measured.

Do you run A/B tests?

We can, where traffic is high enough for a test to mean something. Otherwise we apply proven fixes and measure the change.

Do I need to install new apps?

Only if an app clearly helps. We prefer fewer, lighter apps, and we remove ones that slow the store down.

How long does it take?

A standard store takes about one to two weeks after the scope is approved.

Can you keep optimizing every month?

Yes. We offer monthly plans that include ongoing improvements and reports.
