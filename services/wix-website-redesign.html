Questions

Common questions.

Will I lose my Google rankings?

We plan to avoid it by keeping addresses where we can and redirecting the rest. Rankings can still move for a while after any redesign, and we do not promise otherwise.

Can you keep my existing content?

Yes. We keep what works and tighten what does not, and you approve the result.

Can you move my site from another platform?

Yes. We migrate pages and content to Wix and set redirects. Larger migrations are scoped separately.

Will my site be offline during the redesign?

No. We build away from your live site, so visitors see no interruption until you approve.

How long does a redesign take?

A standard site takes about one to two weeks after the scope is approved.

Do I keep my domain?

Yes. Your domain stays in your name.
