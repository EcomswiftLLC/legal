Questions

Common questions.

Wix or Wix Studio, which should I choose?

Wix suits most small business sites. Wix Studio suits sites that need more layout control and room to grow. We recommend one after a short call.

Can I edit the site myself afterwards?

Yes. You get a short handover guide and can edit text and images yourself.

Who pays for the Wix plan and domain?

You do, directly to Wix. Our fee is separate.

How long does a website take?

A standard business website takes about one to two weeks once we have your content.

Can you write the content?

Yes, if the scope includes it. You approve all text before it goes live.

What if I want changes later?

We offer small-task pricing and care plans for updates after launch.
