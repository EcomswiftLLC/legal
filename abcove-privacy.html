Privacy Policy: ABC Games for Kids: Abcove

Android app by Ecom Swift LLC (package com.ecomswiftllc.abcove). Last updated: November 2026.

Abcove is a set of alphabet games for children aged 3 to 8. It is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We built it so that no personal information about your child is collected by us.

Children's privacy (kids section)

  • There is no account, no login and no sign-up. We never ask for a name, birthday, email, photo, voice or location.
  • The player profile is a cartoon friend and a nickname chosen from a fixed list. Nothing is typed by the child.
  • There is no chat, no sharing, no user-generated content and no push notification.
  • The app does not use the camera, the microphone, contacts, location or storage.
  • We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • Purchases, links that leave the app, rating and settings are behind a parent gate (an arithmetic question for grown-ups).

What stays on the device

Stars, stickers, play time, the daily limit and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on the device. They are not sent to us. Cloud backup of this data is turned off. A grown-up can erase progress in the grown-ups corner; uninstalling the app removes everything.

Advertising

The free version shows ads through Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), an ads SDK that Google lists as self-certified for the Families Policy. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and to users under the age of consent, ads are limited to content rated G, and ads are not personalised (no interest-based ads and no remarketing). The app does not read the advertising ID. Google's ads SDK may collect a device identifier, an IP address and basic diagnostics in child-directed mode only as Google describes in its policies, to deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud. Ads are never shown during a game. Learn more at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Buying "Remove ads" or "Abcove Premium" turns all ads off; the ads SDK is then not started at all.

Purchases

In-app purchases are one-time purchases handled by Google Play. We do not receive payment details. Google Play tells the app only which purchases this Google account owns.

Voices, sounds and offline use

All voices, sounds and pictures are built into the app and work without an internet connection. Nothing the child says or does is recorded.

Parents' rights

Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is nothing on our servers to review or delete. If you have a question about your child's privacy, contact us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
