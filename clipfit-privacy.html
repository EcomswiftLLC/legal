Privacy policy

Video Converter & Compressor: ClipFit for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • ClipFit converts and compresses video and audio files on your PC. The files you choose, the converted files, the conversion history and the settings stay on your computer and are never uploaded.
  • ClipFit has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Network use

The app connects to the internet only to talk to Microsoft Store: to check whether you have Premium, to show prices, to make a purchase you start, and to open the Store rating window when you choose to rate the app. Microsoft handles payments and ratings under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Conversion history

The history stores file names, folder paths, sizes and dates of your conversions so you can find them again. You can remove entries or clear the history in the app, and uninstalling ClipFit removes its data folder. Your converted files are never deleted by the app.
