Wardline for Windows: privacy policy

Effective 2026-11-23. Publisher: Ecom Swift LLC. Contact: support@ecomswiftllc.com.

Wardline is a privacy and security monitor. It is not an antivirus. To do its job it reads information on your PC; this policy says what it reads, what it keeps and what, if anything, leaves your PC.

What Wardline reads on your PC

  • Windows security and privacy settings (registry values, Windows Security and Microsoft Defender status, firewall profiles, Windows Update history, local accounts and their settings).
  • Network connections of programs on this PC, listening ports, network adapter traffic totals, and the devices in the Windows neighbour table.
  • Which apps Windows allowed to use the camera, microphone, location and other permissions, and the last time Windows recorded a use.
  • Startup entries, scheduled tasks, services, installed programs, browser extension manifests, trusted certificates, drivers, the hosts file, DNS and proxy settings.
  • A few privacy settings of Edge, Chrome, Brave and Firefox from their settings files. Wardline never opens saved passwords, cookies or browsing history.

What Wardline keeps

Scan results, the starting point and change history, alerts, an activity log of changes and undo values, device names you enter, and your settings. All of it is stored only in Wardline's own folder on this PC and is removed when you uninstall Wardline. Ecom Swift LLC never receives it.

What leaves your PC

  • Nothing by default. Wardline has no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting.
  • Password breach check, only if you turn it on: when you select Check, Wardline sends the first 5 characters of the SHA-1 fingerprint of the password you typed to api.pwnedpasswords.com (Have I Been Pwned). The password, the full fingerprint and anything that identifies you are never sent. You can turn this off at any time on the Accounts and passwords page.
  • Purchases and licences go through Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement. Wardline receives only whether Premium is active.
  • The country shown for a network address comes from a table inside the app; no address is looked up online.

Changes to Windows

Wardline changes a Windows setting only after you review the exact change and approve the Windows administrator prompt. Previous values are kept on your PC so you can undo.

Children

Wardline is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page and its effective date if what Wardline does with information changes.
