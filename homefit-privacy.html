Movera Home Workout: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Movera (Home Workout & Fitness: Movera) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your workouts, plan, history and body numbers are stored on your phone and never leave it. We don't run a server that receives your fitness data, and we don't sell personal information.

Your fitness data

  • What the app keeps: the answers you give when you set up your plan (goal, focus areas, level, workout days, session length), an optional first name, and the body numbers you choose to enter (height, weight, goal weight, optional age and, with Premium, body measurements). It also keeps your workout history, ratings of how a workout felt, plan progress, streaks, badges, favourites, the workouts you build, your reminder times and your settings.
  • Where it is kept: only in the app's private storage on your phone. Calorie values and BMI are calculated on your phone from these numbers. Nothing is uploaded to us or to anyone else.
  • No health platforms: this version does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or any wearable, and it reads no sensors such as heart rate, steps or location.
  • Backup and export: if you tap Back up everything or Export workout history (CSV), the app writes a file to the place or app you pick (for example your Downloads folder, a cloud drive or an email). Restore reads only the file you pick. The app never sends these files anywhere by itself. If you share a file or a workout code with another app, that app's own privacy policy then applies.
  • Deleting your data: You > Erase my data deletes everything the app stores. Uninstalling the app removes it too.

Voice coach

Spoken cues use the text-to-speech engine already installed on your phone. The app only speaks short workout phrases, such as exercise names and countdowns. It does not use the microphone and never listens.

Notifications and the workout timer

Movera asks for notification permission only when you turn on reminders, either during setup or on the Reminders screen. Workout, missed-day, streak and water reminders are scheduled on your phone and respect the quiet hours you set. While a workout is running, the app shows an ongoing notification with the current exercise and Pause, Next and +20 s controls, so the timer and voice coach keep working with the screen off. This notification ends when the workout ends. If you decline notifications, the app still works fully.

Other permissions

Movera asks for no camera, microphone, contacts, location or storage permission. It uses vibration for optional workout cues and keeps the phone awake only while a workout is running. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases. Workouts, the voice coach and your history work offline.

Advertising

Movera is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your workouts and body numbers are never given to the ad service.

Ads never appear during a workout, a rest, a timer or while you type in your details. A small banner can appear on the Home, Progress and You screens. A full-screen ad may appear after you leave the finish screen of a workout, never after your first one, and limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, for example to try a PRO workout or plan day once.

You can change your consent at any time in You > Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After a few finished workouts or a milestone such as a 7-day streak, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Movera has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health note

Movera offers general fitness guidance and is not a medical device. Consult a doctor before starting an exercise program, especially if you have a health condition, an injury or are pregnant.

Children

Movera is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
