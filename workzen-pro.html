Questions

Does Premium send my tasks anywhere?
No. Your tasks, notes and time stay on your device. The only thing checked online is whether your purchase email has an active plan.
Can I cancel the monthly plan?
Yes, any time, from the Premium page inside WorkZen or from your account. Premium features switch off when the paid period ends and your data stays.
What happens to my data if I stop paying?
Nothing is deleted. Free features keep working. Premium-only extras such as deep reports and templates are locked until you come back.
Can I use it on more than one computer?
Yes. Unlock with the same email in each Chrome profile.
