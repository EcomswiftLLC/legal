Compass & Level: Needlewise: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Needlewise (Compass & Level: Needlewise) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your position, your saved waypoints and every sensor reading are processed on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your location, and we don't sell personal information.

Location

Needlewise asks for your location only the first time you use something that needs it, and it explains why on the screen before Android asks. You can say no, and the compass, the level and the inclinometer keep working. You can also type a place by hand instead.

  • What it is used for: working out true north (the magnetic declination for where you are, from the World Magnetic Model stored inside the app), showing your coordinates, your altitude, where the sun and moon are, the Qibla direction, and the distance and direction to your saved waypoints.
  • Where it stays: on your phone. The calculations run on the phone with no internet connection. Your coordinates are not sent to us and are not given to the ad service.
  • Only while you use the app: Needlewise reads your position while it is open on the screen. It does not track you in the background and does not record a trail.
  • What it remembers: the last position, so the app and the home-screen widget can still show the sun and your place when there is no fresh fix. "Erase my waypoints and place" in Settings removes it at any time.

Sensors

The compass reads the magnetic field sensor, the motion sensors and, where the phone has one, the barometer. The readings are used live to draw the compass and the level and to work out altitude. They are not stored and not sent anywhere.

What Needlewise stores on your phone

  • Your waypoints: the names, notes, coordinates, altitude, saved bearings and times you choose to save. They are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Settings: true or magnetic north, units, dial theme, smoothing, level zero, sea-level pressure, the last position, and a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off, so waypoints are not copied to your Google account. A direct phone-to-phone transfer when you set up a new phone can carry them across. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: copying or sharing coordinates, or exporting waypoints as a GPX or CSV file. These go to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, and that app handles them under its own privacy policy. Waypoint names and notes are in the file, so share it only with people you trust.

Permissions

  • Location (precise and approximate): as described above, asked in context.
  • Vibration: for the small haptic ticks at north and when the level is flat. You can turn them off in Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases. The compass, level, place and sky screens work without a connection.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.
  • Billing: for Premium and Remove ads through Google Play.

Needlewise asks for no camera, microphone, contacts, photos or storage permission, and it does not use background location.

Advertising

Needlewise is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them, and it may work out an approximate location from your IP address. Needlewise never gives the ad service your coordinates, your waypoints or your sensor readings. There are no ads on the Compass and Level screens. Banners appear only on the Place, Sky and Saved screens. A full-screen ad may appear on the Saved screen after you finish saving a place or an export, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature for a day or to add room for more waypoints.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have saved a few waypoints, exported for the first time or calibrated the compass, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while you are measuring.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Needlewise has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Accuracy and safety

Needlewise is not a certified instrument. Phone sensors can be off by several degrees and are disturbed by metal, magnets and electronics. Do not rely on it for navigation in the wild, for aviation or sea use, or for any measurement where a wrong reading could cause harm or loss. See the Terms of Use.

Children

Needlewise is a general-audience tool. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
