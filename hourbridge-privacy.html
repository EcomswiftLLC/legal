Hourbridge Time Zone Planner: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Hourbridge (Time Zone Planner) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it, and it never asks for your location. The cities you add, your groups and your settings are stored on your phone and never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Hourbridge stores on your phone

  • Cities and groups: the cities you add to the Board, the names you give them, their order, the groups you save and any working hours you set.
  • Settings: your home city, 12 or 24 hour clock, working hours, weekend choice, theme, share line and alert switch, plus the meeting time you last picked.
  • Purchase and ad state: whether Premium or Remove ads is owned and when a 24 hour unlock ends.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own backup (to your Google account, if you turned it on in Android settings) may include it; that backup is controlled by you and by Google. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Time zone data

Time and clock change rules come from your phone's own time zone database, which Android keeps up to date. Hourbridge contains a list of about 5,000 cities with their population and time zone, based on GeoNames (geonames.org, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0). The list is inside the app, so no lookup is sent over the internet.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: Share sends a text with the meeting time to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. That app then handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on "Tell me the day before" for clock changes (a Premium feature). A daily background check on your phone looks for clock changes in your saved cities.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, unless you own Premium or Remove ads.

Hourbridge asks for no location, contacts, photos, microphone, calendar or storage permission.

Advertising

Hourbridge is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. The cities you add and the times you plan are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on the Groups, Clock changes and Settings screens, never on the Board, the meeting finder or the compare screen. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish sharing a meeting time, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to unlock planning tools for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a good moment, for example after you have shared a few meeting times, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Hourbridge has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Hourbridge is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
