Privacy Policy — Sitemap Finder & Viewer

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Sitemap Finder & Viewer locates a site's XML sitemaps and lets you read the URLs inside them. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • The extension uses the current tab's domain to look for sitemaps. Your browser requests the site's /robots.txt and common sitemap locations (and, with Pro, the nested sitemaps) directly to read and parse them. These requests go to the site whose sitemap you are viewing — never to us.
  • Parsing and searching run locally in your browser. Settings and any history are stored locally. CSV exports are saved by you, to your computer.

Permissions

  • Host access is required so your browser can request robots.txt and the sitemap files. It is used only for sites you choose to view.
  • activeTab lets the extension read the current tab's domain to know which site's sitemap to find.
  • alarms is used only for the optional reminder described below.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the sites you view, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
