Professional PDF Editor for Windows: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This app is made by Ecom Swift LLC. It edits, signs, merges, splits, compresses, converts, prints and reads PDF files on your Windows PC. Your files stay on your computer. We do not run a server that receives your documents, and we do not sell personal information. The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking code, and it has no account or sign-in of its own.

What stays on your computer

  • Your documents, signatures, passwords and text: merge, split, compress, convert, sign, edit, print and the other tools run entirely on this computer. Nothing is uploaded.
  • Settings, saved signatures (only if you save one) and downloaded OCR languages are kept in the app's data folder on your PC. Nothing is synced or sent anywhere. "Delete saved settings" in the app's Settings removes all of it, and uninstalling the app removes its data folder.
  • Files you save are written to the folder you choose, by default Downloads, and stay there until you delete them.

When something leaves your computer

Only when you use a feature that needs it. There are exactly three cases:

  • Extra OCR languages (Premium). The open-source Tesseract language file is downloaded once from the jsDelivr CDN (cdn.jsdelivr.net) after you agree. Only that file is requested. Your documents are never uploaded, and text recognition runs on your computer. English is built in.
  • Webpage to PDF. The app loads the web address you enter, like a browser would, and turns the page into a PDF on your computer. The page's own server sees an ordinary visit from your computer.
  • Premium. Premium is an optional upgrade bought and managed in the Microsoft Store. The app asks Windows whether your Microsoft account has an active Premium add-on. No documents, file names or usage data are involved. Payment is handled by Microsoft; the app never sees your card details.

Permissions

The app asks Windows for no special account access and does not use your camera, microphone or location.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
