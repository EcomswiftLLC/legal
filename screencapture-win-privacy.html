Screen Recorder & Screenshot Tool for Windows: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

This app is made by Ecom Swift LLC. It captures screenshots and screen recordings on your Windows PC and lets you edit them. Your captures stay on your computer. We do not run a server that receives your screenshots, recordings, webcam or microphone, and we do not sell personal information. The app has no ads and no analytics.

What the app captures, and where it goes

  • Screenshots and recordings: captured only when you start a capture. They are saved to the folders you choose (by default Pictures and Videos) and shown in the app's Library. They are not uploaded anywhere.
  • Microphone, system sound and webcam: used only while you are recording and only if you turn them on. The audio and video are mixed into your recording file on your PC.
  • Mouse clicks and key presses: if you switch on click effects or the keystroke display (Pro), the app listens for clicks and keys while a recording is running, solely to draw them into the video. Nothing is stored or sent, and what you type is shown only as the key labels on screen.
  • Text recognition and smart redaction: run on your PC with a recognition engine bundled in the app. The picture is not sent anywhere.
  • Web page capture: when you type a web address, the app loads that page in a private, temporary browser window to take the picture, then discards the window's cookies and data. The page's own server sees an ordinary visit from your computer.

Settings and history

The app keeps your settings (folders, shortcuts, quality choices) and a list of where your captures were saved on your PC, so the Library can show them. Uninstalling the app removes its settings. Your capture files stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Pro licence

The app is free to use. Pro is an optional upgrade. When you activate Pro you type the email address you paid with. The app sends that email address, over an encrypted connection, to Ecom Swift LLC's licence service at ecomswiftllc.com to check whether the plan is active, and repeats the check about once a day while you use Pro. We use it only to confirm your plan. Payment is taken on our website and is handled by our payment provider; the app never sees your card details.

Permissions

The app asks Windows for no special account access. Windows may ask you to allow microphone or camera use the first time you turn them on, and you can change that in Windows Settings under Privacy.

Ratings

After you have used the app successfully a few times it may ask whether you would like to rate it. If you agree, the Microsoft Store's own review window opens, and the review is handled by Microsoft.

Children

The app is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
