What to include

These details let us look at your problem without writing back to ask for them.

Store address
For example https://your-store.myshopify.com.
Example page
The full address of a page where the problem shows, such as an example product page.
Theme version
Shown in the theme editor under Theme settings.
Browser and device
For example Safari on an iPhone.
Screenshots
A screenshot or a short screen recording. You can attach files with the form.

Please do not send passwords or customer data.
