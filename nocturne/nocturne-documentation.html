About Nocturne

Nocturne is a Shopify theme for fashion and clothing stores. Each page reads as a sequence of scenes: full width images, large serif headings and quiet text, with dark and light sections taking turns.

This guide covers how to install the theme, how to set it up in the theme editor, and what every setting does. Setting names in this guide match the names you see in the editor, so you can search this page for a label and find its description.

What you get in the theme:

  • A home page made of 15 sections that you can reorder, edit, hide or remove, from a hero slideshow to a newsletter signup.
  • The Index, a full screen navigation that replaces dropdown menus. It supports three levels of links and can show a picture beside each top level link.
  • Product pages with a frame index beside the images, variant and color swatch pickers, a size guide, a "Complete the look" block, trust badges, complementary and related products, and recently viewed products.
  • Collection pages with filtering, sorting, optional campaign tiles and numbered pages or a load more button.
  • A cart drawer or cart page, predictive search, a gift card page, customer account pages, a blog, a contact form, a 404 page and a password page.

Nocturne is an Online Store 2.0 theme. Every page is built from sections, and apps can add blocks to the product page and to the featured product section.

About the starter content

The text and images that come with the theme are examples. Replace them with your own before you publish. The launch checklist lists the places that need your attention.

Installation

  1. Open the Nocturne page in the Shopify Theme Store and add the theme to your store's theme library.
  2. In your Shopify admin, go to Online Store, then Themes. Nocturne appears in the theme library, below your live theme.
  3. If you already have a live theme, make a backup first. Open the actions menu on the live theme and choose Duplicate.
  4. Select Customize next to Nocturne to open the theme editor. While Nocturne is unpublished, nothing you change affects your live store.
  5. To see Nocturne on a phone or to share it with a colleague, open the actions menu on Nocturne and choose Preview.
  6. When you are ready, select Publish. Nocturne replaces your live theme right away.

Your products, collections, pages, navigation menus and store policies belong to your store, so they stay in place when you switch themes. Theme settings and the content you type into sections belong to a theme, so they do not carry over from another theme.

Updates

When we release a new version, Shopify shows an update notice on the theme in your theme library. Make a backup copy of your live theme before you update, and check the updated theme in a preview before you publish it. The theme version number is shown in the theme editor under Theme settings.

Theme editor basics

Open the editor from Online Store, Themes, then Customize.

Pages and sections

  • Use the page selector at the top of the editor to move between the home page, product pages, collection pages, the cart, search, blog pages, the 404 page, the password page and others.
  • The left panel lists the sections on the page you are editing. The Header group at the top and the Footer group at the bottom appear on every page. The header group holds the Top bar and the Header.
  • Select a section to see its settings. Many sections also contain blocks, such as slides, chapters or menu columns. Add, reorder, hide or remove them from the same panel.
  • Use Add section to place a new section on a page. Each section is available only where it makes sense. For example, Hero slideshow can be added to the home page, pages and collection pages, and Related products only to the product page.
  • Drag sections to reorder them, or use the eye icon to hide a section without losing its settings.

Settings every section shares

Color scheme
Chooses one of the color schemes defined in Theme settings, Colors. Each scheme sets the background, text, accent and button colors for the whole section.
Top padding, Bottom padding
Space above and below the section, in pixels. Padding is reduced automatically on small screens.

Text that is not in a section

Some words come from the theme itself, such as "Add to bag", "Sold out" and the labels in the cart. To change them, open the actions menu on Nocturne in your theme library and choose Edit default theme content. Text you type into a section in the editor is edited in that section.

What the editor does not show

The gift card page and the customer account pages are code templates, so they do not appear in the page selector. Their text can still be changed with Edit default theme content, and their colors follow your theme settings.

Launch checklist

Go through this list before you publish. Each item names the place where the starter text or a default lives.

  • Trust badges and policy text on product pages. The product template ships with a trust badges block ("Tracked delivery", "Returns", "Secure checkout") and three collapsible rows ("Details", "Materials and care", "Shipping and returns"). The rows mention order preparation times and returns of unworn pieces. Keep a statement only if it is true for your store, and rewrite the rest.
  • Brand and campaign text. The starter copy in Brand story, Promotional campaign, Image with text, Rich text and the hero slides is written as an example. Replace it with your own words, products and offers.
  • Testimonials and press. Add only feedback and coverage you have really received, quoted exactly, with permission where it is needed.
  • Logo, favicon and menus. Upload your logo in the Header section, set a favicon in Theme settings, and check that the Menu setting points to your main menu.
  • Cart message. The default says "Shipping and taxes are calculated at checkout." Keep it only if that is how your store works.
  • Free shipping bar. It is off by default. Turn it on in the Cart drawer section only if you really offer free shipping above the amount you enter.
  • Search and Discovery app. Install Shopify's free Search and Discovery app to set up filters, product recommendations and complementary products.
  • Store policies. Add your refund, shipping, privacy and terms policies under Settings, then Policies. The footer can link to them.
  • Image alt text. Add a short description to each important image in Content, then Files, or in the product media editor.
  • Test on a phone. Open the preview on a phone. Try the Index, add a product to the bag, change a variant, and place a test order.

Theme settings

Theme settings apply to the whole store. In the editor, open Theme settings from the left rail. They are grouped as described below.

Keep a strong difference between a scheme's background and its text. A ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 is the common target for body text. On dark backgrounds the keyboard focus outline uses the accent color, and on light backgrounds it uses the text color, so check that your accent is visible on its background.

Typography

Heading font
Pick any font from Shopify's font library. The default is Cormorant. Large, light headings and italic captions are part of the look, so a serif with an italic style works best.
Heading size
Scales every heading from 80% to 130%.
Body font
Pick any font from the library. The default is Instrument Sans.
Body text size
From 14 to 18 pixels.

Nocturne loads the regular, italic and bold styles of each font, and a lighter weight of the heading font when one exists. Fonts with fewer styles load faster. Only fonts from Shopify's library are available. You cannot upload a custom font in theme settings.

Layout

Page width
Choose Narrow (content up to 1360 pixels wide), Standard (1680 pixels), Wide (1920 pixels) or Full width. Full width also uses smaller side margins.
Image corner radius
From 0 to 24 pixels. The default of 0 keeps images as sharp rectangles.

Buttons

Button corner radius
From 0 to 40 pixels. The default of 0 gives square buttons.

The Index and menu images

The Index is Nocturne's main navigation. It opens as a full screen panel with large serif links. It replaces dropdown and mega menus, so deep menus work the same way on a phone and on a desktop.

How shoppers use it

  • Select Index in the header to open it. Close or the Escape key closes it.
  • A link with sub-items has a plus button beside it. Select the button to open the sub-items, up to two more levels. On a device with a mouse, pointing at a link for a moment also opens its sub-items.
  • At the bottom of the panel are a search field, a link to the account (when accounts are on), the country and language selectors, and social media icons. Each can be switched off in the header settings.
  • The panel works with a keyboard. Focus stays inside it while it is open and returns to the Index button when it closes.

Adding an image to a menu item

On screens 990 pixels wide and up, the Index can show a picture beside the links. The picture changes when a visitor points at, or moves keyboard focus to, a top level link. On smaller screens the picture is not shown.

  1. In the Header section, select Add block and choose Index image.
  2. Choose an Image. A 4:5 aspect ratio is recommended.
  3. In Navigation item title, type the exact title of a top level link from your menu, for example Women. The match ignores capital letters and extra spaces at the ends, but the words must be the same.
  4. Optionally add a Caption. If you add both a Link label and a Link, a link appears under the picture.
Image
The picture for the matching link.
Navigation item title
The exact title of a top level link. Leave it empty to use this block as the default picture, shown when a link has no picture of its own.
Caption
A short line in italic under the picture.
Link label, Link
Both are needed to show the link.

If a picture does not appear

Check that the title matches the menu link exactly, that the block has an image, and that you are looking at a screen at least 990 pixels wide. If no block has an empty title, links without a match use the first Index image block.

Home page sections

The home page ships with 15 sections in this order: hero slideshow, editorial campaign, featured collections, new arrivals, image with text, campaign film, bestsellers, shop the look, brand story, promotional campaign, testimonials, press and recognition, editorial gallery, blog posts and newsletter. Reorder, hide or delete any of them, and add the others listed below from Add section.

Image sizes in the settings are recommendations for sharp results on large screens. Nocturne serves a smaller version to smaller screens. Where a setting lets you choose an image shape, the image is cropped to that shape around the focal point you set in Shopify's file editor.

A slideshow holds up to six slides. Slides change as a crossfade or a cut. With more than one slide, visitors get previous and next controls, and a pause or play control when the slides rotate or a video plays.

SEO tips

Nocturne outputs a page title, a meta description, Open Graph and Twitter card tags, a canonical link and structured product data. Search rankings depend on your content, so these steps help most:

  • Fill in the Search engine listing on products, collections, pages and articles. Write a clear page title and a one or two sentence description.
  • Give each product a unique title and description. Avoid copying manufacturer text.
  • Add alt text to your images. Describe what the image shows in a short sentence. Mark purely decorative images by leaving the alt text empty.
  • Each page has one main heading. In your own page content, use headings in order and keep them meaningful.
  • Set a social sharing image on pages you share often. The theme uses the page's image when there is one.
  • Use descriptive link text such as "Read our size guide" rather than "Click here".
  • Keep your navigation menu names short and plain, since they appear in the Index.

Accessibility notes

Nocturne is built with accessibility in mind. We make no claim of full conformance to any standard, because your content, colors and apps affect the result. What the theme does:

  • A skip link at the top of every page, and landmarks for the header, main content, navigation and footer.
  • One main heading per page.
  • Visible keyboard focus on links, buttons and form fields.
  • The Index, the search panel, the cart drawer, the quick view, the size guide and the image zoom behave as dialogs. Focus moves into them, stays inside while they are open, closes with Escape and returns to the control that opened them.
  • Slideshows and rotating top bar messages have pause controls, and they stop while hovered or focused. The hero slideshow does not start rotating by itself for visitors who prefer reduced motion.
  • Form fields have labels. Errors are written out in text.
  • The cart and search announce changes to screen readers through live regions.
  • Sold out and unavailable variant options carry a text status, not only a strikethrough.
  • Touch targets are sized for fingers.

What stays with you:

  • Write alt text for images, and captions or transcripts for videos.
  • Choose color schemes with enough contrast between text and background. A ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 is a common target for body text.
  • Do not put important text inside images.
  • Check apps that you add. Their output is not part of the theme.

Performance tips

Nocturne loads styles and scripts per section where it can, serves images at sizes that fit the screen, loads the first image on a page right away and the rest as visitors scroll, and loads campaign film and product video players only after a visitor asks for them. Your content has the larger effect, so:

  • Upload images close to the sizes suggested in the editor. A 4000 pixel photo costs more than it gains. Compress large images before uploading.
  • Keep the home page focused. Each section you add is more to load. Hide or remove sections you do not use.
  • Keep hero videos short, and use the mobile image setting so phones do not download video.
  • Choose fonts with few styles. Each style is a download.
  • Add apps carefully. Apps often add scripts that slow every page. Remove apps you no longer use, and check for leftover code.
  • Use a reasonable number of products per page in collections.

Speed results vary with your content, apps and visitors' devices and connections. Shopify's online store speed report shows how your store performs.

Frequently asked questions

Can I change the fonts?

Yes. Pick any font from Shopify's library under Typography in theme settings. Uploading a custom font file is not available.

Does Nocturne use countdown timers or fake stock numbers?

No. There are no countdown timers, no invented stock counts and no automatic sale badges. The "Only 3 left" style text on a product appears only when you track inventory in Shopify and the real quantity is at or below the threshold you set. A sale badge appears only when a product's compare-at price is higher than its price.

Does "Complete the look" give a discount?

No. It adds the selected products to the cart at their own prices using Shopify's standard add-to-cart request. If you want a bundle discount, create an automatic discount in Shopify.

Are the trust badges verified?

No. They are text you write. Keep them accurate and in line with your policies.

Why is there no dropdown menu?

Nocturne uses the Index, a full screen navigation, so that menus with many links work the same way on phones and desktops. It supports three levels of links.

Can I show an Instagram feed?

The Editorial gallery shows images that you upload. It does not connect to Instagram or other social media accounts, and the theme has no social media feed feature.

Can I add a wishlist or customer reviews?

The theme does not include them. They can be added with apps. Apps are not covered by theme support.

How do I change "Add to bag" to another word?

Use Edit default theme content from the actions menu on Nocturne in your theme library, and change the text for the button.

How do I make the logo show over the hero image?

Upload a light version in Logo over images in the header, and keep Show header over the home page hero on.

Can I use the theme on more than one store?

A Theme Store purchase is for one store. Check the license terms on the Nocturne page in the Shopify Theme Store.

Where do I find the theme version?

In the theme editor, open Theme settings. The theme name and version are listed there. Please include the version when you write to support.
