Motionly Photo Animator: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

Motionly is a photo animator and video loop maker made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos, videos and songs are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your files, we don't build profiles of people, and we don't sell personal information.

Your photos, videos and music

  • You choose what Motionly sees: the app opens only the photos and videos you pick with Android's photo picker or send to it from another app, and only the songs you pick with the system file picker. It does not browse your gallery or music library.
  • Everything happens on your device: flow animation, camera moves, 3D depth, effects, looks, text, stickers, cinemagraphs, video loops, slideshows, beat detection and GIF, WebP and MP4 encoding are all processed locally.
  • On-device person model: for the 3D depth effect Motionly finds the person in a photo with Google ML Kit selfie segmentation. The model ships inside the app and runs on your phone; the picture is not sent anywhere for it. The resulting outline is used only for the animation you are making and is not stored as personal data. Motionly does not detect or identify faces, and it never generates, animates or alters faces with a model.
  • What gets saved on your phone: finished videos go to Movies/Motionly and GIF, WebP and photos to Pictures/Motionly. Motionly keeps a list of recent exports with small thumbnails, your drafts (a copy of the photo you are animating plus its settings, in the app's private storage), your saved recipes and settings. Decoded video frames are kept in the app's cache only while you edit and are removed afterwards. Deleting a draft or uninstalling the app removes them.

Camera, gallery and storage

Motionly does not ask for the camera, microphone, location or contacts permission. Photos and videos are opened through Android's photo picker, which needs no storage permission. On Android 9 and older Motionly asks for permission to save to your gallery. Motionly uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Motionly is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or have removed ads. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home and result screens, never while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished export, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video appears only when you choose to watch one, for example to try a premium tool for 24 hours or to export one clip in 4K.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions, the lifetime purchase and the one-time ad removal are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of what you own, so it can unlock Premium on your devices.

Ratings

After you have exported a few creations, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service, Google Play Billing and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Your photos, videos and songs are never part of it.

Children

Motionly is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
