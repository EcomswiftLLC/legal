Numbloom Privacy Policy

Math Games for Kids: Numbloom, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Made for children

Numbloom is designed for children aged 3 to 8 and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not ask a child for any personal information and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account, no sign-in, no name or birthday entry, no photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location, no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no push notifications.

What stays on the device

Stars, stickers, levels played, time played each day, the buddy and nickname (picked from a fixed list) and settings are stored only on the device. They are never sent to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else. A grown-up can clear them with "Reset progress" in the grown-ups corner, and uninstalling the app removes them.

Ads

The version without Premium shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and as for users under the age of consent, and is limited to the G content rating. Ads are never personalised, interest-based or used for remarketing. The app does not access the advertising ID. As Google describes for child-directed requests, the ads SDK may collect a device or other identifier, an approximate location derived from the IP address, ad interactions and diagnostics, used to deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud. See how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Ads appear only between levels and on the home menu and grown-ups corner, never during a puzzle. Premium and Remove ads turn all ad requests off.

Purchases

One-time purchases (Premium and Remove ads) are handled by Google Play, behind a parent check. Billing is handled by Play; we never see payment details.

No analytics

Numbloom contains no analytics or crash reporting SDK.

Parents' rights

Because we hold no personal information about you or your child, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. For any question, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
