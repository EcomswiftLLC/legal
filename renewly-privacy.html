Dueleaf: Subscription Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Dueleaf (Subscription Tracker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it, and it never asks for a bank login. The subscriptions you add are stored on your phone and never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Dueleaf stores on your phone

  • Your list: the names, prices, currencies, renewal dates and cycles, categories, notes, optional account-page web addresses, share percentages, free trial details and reminder choices you type in.
  • Exchange rates: the rates you type in yourself. Dueleaf does not download rates.
  • Settings: base currency, reminder times, theme, accent and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Dueleaf does not read your bank, your messages, your email, your contacts or your notifications. It does not cancel anything for you. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Android's phone-to-phone transfer during setup of a new phone may carry the data over. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and the erase option in Settings removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: saving a backup file and exporting a CSV save or share a file to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker or share sheet. That place or app then handles the file under its own privacy policy. When you import a spreadsheet, Dueleaf reads only the file you pick in Android's file picker, on your phone, and nothing is uploaded. The popular services list, with names and logos, is part of the app itself and is not downloaded. The optional app lock uses your phone's own screen lock or fingerprint through Android; Dueleaf never sees or stores your PIN, pattern or fingerprint. If you type a web address for a service's account page, Dueleaf opens it in your browser only when you tap it.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for reminders before a charge, on the day, and before a free trial ends. Android asks at the moment you save a subscription with reminders on, and you can decline. Reminders arrive around the time you chose; Android may move them by a few minutes to save battery.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Dueleaf plans your reminders again.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Dueleaf asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission, and it does not use exact alarms.

Advertising

Dueleaf is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your subscription list, prices and notes are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on the main tabs, never on the add or edit screens. A full-screen ad may appear on the Home list after you add a subscription or export a CSV, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to add room for more subscriptions or to open a Premium tool for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a good moment, for example after you have set up a few subscriptions, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Dueleaf has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Money and accuracy

Dueleaf is a personal tracking tool, not a bank, a payment service or a financial adviser. Amounts, dates and totals are only what you typed in, plus the exchange rates you typed in.

Children

Dueleaf is made for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
