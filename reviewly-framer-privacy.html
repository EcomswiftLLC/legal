Privacy Policy - Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews is a product reviews service published by Ecom Swift LLC. A store owner ("the merchant") installs the Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews plugin in their Framer project, which adds one small script to the published site. Shoppers on that site can then read and write product reviews, with photos and short videos. The merchant can also import a list of customers (a CSV file) or send purchases to a private webhook so the service can mark real buyers. This policy explains what data the service handles, where it lives and why.

The Framer plugin. The Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews plugin for Framer works entirely inside the merchant's Framer project. The reviews the merchant types or imports are stored in the project's own code file (and, if the merchant asks, in a CMS collection of that project) and are published with the site. The plugin makes no network request, has no account and sends nothing to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else, so for the plugin we collect and process no personal data. Photos the merchant adds with the plugin's upload button go into the project's own Framer asset storage, handled by Framer under Framer's terms, not by us. Video links are stored only as addresses of YouTube or Vimeo videos. The rest of this policy describes the separate hosted service used on other platforms and through the web dashboard.
Short version. We store the merchant's account details, the reviews shoppers write (including any photos or short videos they add) and, if the merchant imports them, the customers used to mark verified buyers. We use no cookies, no advertising and no analytics trackers, and we never sell data. A shopper's IP address is turned into a salted one-way code that is used only to limit spam. The merchant decides what happens to the reviews on their store.

1. Who we are

The service is published by Ecom Swift LLC. Contact us at framer@ecomswiftllc.com. For the review content on a store, the merchant is the data controller and Ecom Swift LLC acts as their processor.

An email address typed into a review form is never shown publicly. It is visible only to the merchant. By default Reviewly: Photo, UGC reviews does not send emails to shoppers: the merchant sends review requests from their own tools. If the merchant switches on automatic review requests, the service sends a one-time email to each customer on the merchant's list through the merchant's own Brevo or Resend account (the merchant's sender address, the customer's email address and first name, the product name and the review link are passed to that provider, which has its own policy). Every such email has a one-click link that stops further requests for that address.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies. The review widget may keep a short-lived copy of review data in the shopper's browser session storage so a page does not reload the same data repeatedly. It is cleared when the browser tab is closed.

4. Where data lives and who sees it

Data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers and D1) on its global network. The merchant can see all reviews on their store, including shopper emails. Reviews are shown publicly on the merchant's store only after the merchant's moderation rules allow it. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers and we do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Reviews are kept until the merchant deletes them or deletes their account. Deleting an account in the dashboard permanently removes the account and all of its reviews. Salted IP hashes, photos and videos are removed together with the review they belong to. If the merchant clears the customer list, the imported customers are deleted; reviews stay. The merchant's admin key is shown once in the dashboard and never passes through the Framer plugin. The plugin only holds the public site key, which is also printed in the published page's code. The dashboard link the plugin opens is a plain address with no key or project data. Removing the plugin's code from the site stops the service from running there.

6. Your choices and rights

Merchants can export every review as CSV or JSON, edit or delete any review, clear their imported customer list, and delete their whole account at any time. A shopper who wants a review changed or removed should contact the store that published it, or write to us at the address above and we will help route or act on the request. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We honor such requests.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Admin keys are stored only as one-way hashes. Review text is displayed as plain text so it cannot run code on a store, and uploaded photos and videos are checked by their file contents and served only as images or video. We limit submissions per network and filter obvious spam, but no system is perfectly secure.

8. Children

The service is for store owners and their adult customers and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the dashboard.
