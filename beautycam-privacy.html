Beauty Camera: Aurette, Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Aurette (Beauty Camera: Aurette) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos, videos and face are processed on your phone and never leave it. We don't run a server that receives your pictures or clips, and we don't sell personal information.

Your face, photos and videos

  • Everything happens on your device: the live beauty effects, filters, makeup looks, background blur, photo capture, video recording and the touch-up screen all run on your phone.
  • Face detection, on device only: to place beauty effects on your face, Aurette uses Google ML Kit face detection and selfie segmentation, whose models are built into the app and run on your phone. They find the outline of a face, the eyes, lips and nose in each camera frame, and the area of a person for background blur. This information exists only in your phone's memory while a frame or a photo is being processed. It is not stored, not uploaded, not used to identify or recognise anyone, and Aurette does not estimate age, gender, ethnicity or emotion.
  • You choose what Aurette sees: to touch up an existing photo, the app uses Android's system Photo Picker, which gives it only the photo you select. It does not browse your gallery.
  • What is kept: for photos taken or touched up in Aurette, an untouched copy and the beauty settings used are kept inside the app, so you can touch the photo up again from the Studio. Your saved looks (My looks) and settings are kept too. Deleting a shot in the Studio removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of this. No face data is kept.
  • What gets saved: photos and videos are written to Pictures/Aurette (or DCIM/Aurette if you choose it in Settings). If you turn on "Also save the original", the untouched photo is saved there as well. If you tap Share, the file is handed to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Camera

Aurette asks for camera permission for its live beauty camera. The camera is used only while the camera screen is open and stops when you leave it or the app goes to the background. Camera frames are processed on your phone and are not sent anywhere.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while you record a video, so the clip has sound. Aurette asks for it the first time you record a video. It is optional: if you decline, videos are recorded without sound. Sound is stored only inside the video file on your phone. The app never listens in the background.

Location and other permissions

Aurette has no location permission and never adds location to your photos. It asks for no contacts or phone permission. On Android 9 and older it asks for permission to save to your Pictures folder, only when you save. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Aurette is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Banners appear on the Studio, photo viewer and Settings screens, never over the camera or while you touch up a photo. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a photo, limited in number and spacing, and never before you take a photo. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to use the Pro tools for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have saved a few photos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. Photos, videos, camera frames and face data are not part of this. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Aurette has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Aurette is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
