Pantry Tracker: Freshjar: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Freshjar (Pantry Tracker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your pantry, shopping lists and history are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your stock, and we don't sell personal information.

What Freshjar stores on your phone

  • Your stock and lists: item names, quantities, storage places, dates, prices you enter, notes, shopping rows, and the Used and Thrown away history that powers the Insights screen. They are kept in the app's private storage.
  • Products you teach it: when you scan a barcode and give it a name, Freshjar remembers the code, the name and the place on your phone so the next scan is one tap. There is no online product database.
  • Settings: theme, accent colour, reminder time and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and the Erase everything option in Settings removes it at any time.

Camera and barcode scanning

If you allow the camera, Freshjar reads barcodes on your phone using a model that ships inside the app, so it works without a network. The camera picture is analysed in memory and is not saved, uploaded or shared. You can also scan from a photo you pick or type the number by hand. Freshjar does not read your contacts, messages, location or microphone.

Dates are a guide

Freshjar shows the dates you enter and a rough guide for common foods. It is a reminder tool, not a food safety service. Always check the package and use your own judgement before eating anything.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: saving a backup file, restoring one, exporting a CSV file, sharing a snapshot or a shopping list, and sharing the app. These go to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker or share sheet, which then handles them under its own privacy policy. A snapshot or backup file contains your stock and lists as plain text in the Freshjar file format, so share it only with people you trust.

Permissions

  • Camera: only for scanning barcodes, and only if you allow it. Everything else works without it.
  • Notifications: for the optional daily summary. Android asks when you turn it on, and you can decline. Reminders may arrive a few minutes early or late.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Freshjar plans your reminder again.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Freshjar asks for no location, microphone, contacts or storage permission, and it does not use exact alarms.

Advertising

Freshjar is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your items, lists and history are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear only on the Today, Stock and Shopping screens. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish a task, limited in number and spacing, and never while you are typing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature for a day or two.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have used a few items before their date, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while you are working.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Freshjar has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Freshjar is a household tool for adults and is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
