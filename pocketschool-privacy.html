Privacy policy

Preschool Learning: Pebbleton (Android, com.ecomswiftllc.pocketschool). Last updated 23 November 2026. Ecom Swift LLC.

Made for children

Pebbleton is designed for children aged 3 to 8 and follows the Google Play Families policy, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the GDPR rules for children. We do not ask for, collect or store any personal information from children.

What stays on the device

Stars, stickers, the chosen picture and the chosen nickname (picked from a fixed list, never typed), play time for the daily limit and the settings are stored only on the device. We cannot see them. Use "Reset progress" in the parent corner or uninstall the app to erase them.

What we never collect

  • No account, login, name, email, birthday or phone number.
  • No photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location permission.
  • No analytics, crash reporting, social features, chat or user-generated content, and no push notifications.

Ads

The free version shows a small number of ads from Google AdMob, a Families self-certified ad network. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, with the maximum ad content rating G. Ads are never personalised, never use interest or remarketing data, and the advertising ID is not used (the permission is removed from the app). To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, the AdMob SDK may process limited device information such as the IP address (approximate location), an app-scoped device identifier, ad interactions and diagnostics, as described in Google's documentation for child-directed requests. Ads never appear in a game or on the first launch. If Premium or Remove ads is bought, no ad requests are made at all.

Purchases

Two optional one-time purchases (Premium and Remove ads) are offered only to adults behind a parent check. Payments are processed by Google Play; we never see payment details. Google Play keeps the purchase history.

Voices and content

All voices, pictures and games are built into the app and work offline. Nothing is downloaded or uploaded while playing.

Parents

Links that leave the app, purchases, the rewarded video for a bonus sticker pack, settings and rating are all behind the parent check. Contact us at support@ecomswiftllc.com with any question or request. We answer parents, and we will delete any message on request.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date above.
