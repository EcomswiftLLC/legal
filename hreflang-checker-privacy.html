Privacy Policy — Hreflang Checker

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Hreflang Checker lists and validates the hreflang annotations on the current page and, with Pro, confirms that each alternate page links back. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the current page's hreflang link tags (<link rel="alternate" hreflang>) from the active tab, only when you ask.
  • Validation runs locally in your browser.
  • The optional Pro "verify return links" check requests each alternate page's URL directly to read its hreflang tags and confirm it points back. Those requests go to the alternate pages themselves — never to us — and only the presence of a return link and the status code are shown.
  • Settings and any history are stored locally. CSV exports are saved by you, to your computer.

Permissions

  • Host access is required for the optional return-link check to request each alternate page. It is used only for pages you choose to check.
  • activeTab / scripting let the extension read the hreflang tags on the current page.
  • alarms is used only for the optional reminder described below.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you check, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
