Privacy Policy — SERP Snippet Preview & Editor

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

SERP Snippet Preview & Editor loads the current page's title, meta description and URL into an editable Google-result preview so you can optimize them. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the current page's title, meta description and URL from the active tab, only for the tab you are on and only when you open it, to pre-fill the editor.
  • The live preview, pixel and character measurements, the snippet checklist, the click-through score, variants and bulk preview all run locally in your browser. The page content is never sent to us or any third party.
  • Your settings, saved variants and recent history (Pro features) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you preview, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
