Privacy policy

Desktop Widgets & Weather: WidgetDeck for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • WidgetDeck has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive any of your data. Your widgets, notes, places, layouts and settings are stored only on your PC.

What WidgetDeck stores, and where

  • A local database in the app's data folder holds your widgets and their positions, note, to-do and habit text, saved weather places, layouts, settings and a cache of the last forecast, calendar and headlines.
  • Calendar files and pictures you add are copied into the same folder.
  • Automatic daily backups of the database are kept in the same folder for 14 days.
  • Uninstalling the app removes this folder.

When WidgetDeck uses the internet

These are all of the network connections the app makes.

  • MET Norway (api.met.no), for weather. This happens only while a weather widget is on screen, at most every 30 minutes per saved place, and when you press Refresh. The app sends the latitude and longitude of the saved place, rounded to 4 decimals, and a User-Agent that names the app and links to this policy. It never sends what you type into a search, your name or an identifier.
  • Calendar links (ICS or webcal) that you add. The app makes a normal web request to the address you entered when you add it, then every 30 minutes while WidgetDeck runs.
  • News feeds (RSS or Atom) that you add. The app makes a normal web request to the address you entered when you add it, then every 30 minutes while WidgetDeck runs.
  • Microsoft Store, to check your Premium licence, show prices, buy, restore purchases, check for updates and show the rating window. Windows and the Microsoft Store handle this under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. WidgetDeck does not receive your payment details.
  • Your web browser, only when you search from the search widget or open a headline or a quick launch link. The search or link opens in your browser and WidgetDeck does not keep it.

No other network connection is made.

City search

City search runs on your PC against a list of places from GeoNames that is bundled with the app. Nothing you type there is sent anywhere.

System information

The system monitor and battery widgets read CPU, memory, disk, network, GPU, battery and running program names from Windows on your PC to show them in the widget. These numbers are not stored long term and are not sent anywhere.

Children

WidgetDeck does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Data sources

Weather is based on data from MET Norway (CC BY 4.0 and NLOD 2.0). Places are from GeoNames (CC BY 4.0).
