Mojilens Face Filters: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 10 November 2026

Mojilens (Face Filters: Mojilens) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Faces are tracked on your phone, and your photos and videos never leave it unless you share them yourself. We don't run a server that receives your pictures, clips or face data, and we don't sell personal information.

Face tracking

  • On your device only: to place effects, Mojilens uses Google ML Kit face detection, a model that ships inside the app and runs on your phone. It finds the position of eyes, nose, mouth and face outline in the current camera frame or in a photo you pick, and estimates whether the eyes are open, the mouth is open or the person is smiling, so effects can follow the head and react to gestures.
  • Nothing is stored or sent: these face positions exist only in memory while a frame is drawn and are discarded right after. They are not saved, not uploaded, not shared, and not used to identify or recognise anyone. Mojilens does not estimate age, gender, ethnicity or emotion.
  • Mojilens has no face swap or likeness-transfer feature. Funhouse warps are cartoon effects applied to the picture you see.

Camera and microphone

Mojilens asks for camera permission for its live effects camera. The camera is used only while the camera screen is open and stops when you leave it or the app goes to the background. The microphone is optional and used only while you record a video, so the clip has sound; Mojilens asks for it before your first video. If you decline, videos are recorded without sound. Mojilens never listens in the background.

Your photos and videos

  • You choose what Mojilens sees: to add effects to an existing photo, the app uses Android's system Photo Picker, which gives it only the photo you select. It does not browse your gallery.
  • What gets saved: nothing is saved until you tap Save. Photos are written to Pictures/Mojilens and videos to Movies/Mojilens on your phone. The app keeps a list of the items you saved (their addresses on your phone, the effect name and the date) so it can show them under Your captures; deleting an item there removes it from your phone after you confirm.
  • Sharing: if you tap Share, the file is handed to the app you pick, and that app's privacy policy then applies.
  • Settings and favourites: your favourite and recent effects, settings and the answer to the first-run question ("What will you make most?") are stored on your phone only. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Other permissions

Mojilens asks for no contacts, location or phone permission. On Android 9 and older it asks for permission to save to your Pictures and Movies folders, only when you save. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Mojilens is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Banners appear on the home, captures, result and settings screens, never over the camera view or the photo you are editing. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a photo or video and return to the home screen, limited in number and spacing, and never after your first save. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to unlock Premium effects for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have saved a few photos or videos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. Camera frames, photos, videos and face data are not part of this. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Mojilens has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Mojilens is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
