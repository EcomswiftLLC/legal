Privacy policy

PCCO: PC Cleaner & Optimizer for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 6 November 2026.

In short

  • PCCO works on your PC. Scan results, your Health Score, live readings, clean history, settings and the lists of your startup items and drives stay on your PC. The app never uploads them, or any file, to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • The app itself makes no internet connections. The only online parts are Microsoft Store purchases, licence checks and update checks, which Windows and the Microsoft Store handle, and web pages that open in your browser only when you click a link.

What the app reads on your PC

To do its job, PCCO reads information on your PC, and only on your PC:

  • Smart Scan and junk cleanup read the names, sizes and dates of files in temporary, cache and log locations. They do not open or read the contents of those files.
  • The Storage Analyzer and the large file finder read the names, sizes and dates of files in the drives or folders you pick, to show what takes space.
  • The duplicate finder reads the contents of files in the folders you pick, to compare them. The comparison happens on your PC; file contents are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • The startup manager reads the startup entries Windows keeps (for example the Run registry keys, the Startup folders and sign-in scheduled tasks).
  • The live system health monitor and the Health Score read the readings Windows reports about your processor, memory, drives, network and, where available, temperatures, such as how busy they are and how much free space the drives have.
  • Privacy cleanup looks for the browser data and Windows traces you choose to review, such as cookies, browsing history lists and recent items. It lists them and changes nothing until you select them.

What the app changes, and only when you ask

  • Every clean starts with a review and an optional dry run that deletes nothing. A clean removes only the temporary, cache and log files you approved.
  • Your own files (for example duplicates and large files) are moved to the Recycle Bin, not deleted outright.
  • Privacy cleanup is opt-in per item: nothing is selected for you.
  • Switching a startup item off or on changes the same setting Windows Task Manager uses.
  • Scheduled auto-maintenance (Premium) runs only after you switch it on, and only does the tasks you chose in the maintenance plan.

What the app stores, and where

PCCO saves its settings, the clean history, the free plan's monthly cleanup counter and, with Premium, your Health Score history and alerts in its own app data folder on your PC. A saved licence status is kept there too, so Premium keeps working for a short time if the Microsoft Store cannot be reached. You can clear the history in the app. Uninstalling the app removes this folder.

Purchases

Premium is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The Microsoft Store handles payment, renewal, refunds and cancellation under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. The app never sees your payment details. To show your plan, the app asks Windows and the Microsoft Store for the add-on licences of your Microsoft account. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your account details.

Links

The app opens a web page, the Microsoft Store, a Windows Settings page or your email program only when you choose a link or button. Those pages open outside the app and are covered by their own policies.

Children

PCCO is a general utility. It does not collect personal information and has no communication features.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
