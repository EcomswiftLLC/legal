Offroad: Wild Summit: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Offroad: Wild Summit is a single-player off-road driving game made by Ecom Swift LLC. You do not need an account to play. We do not run a server that collects your game data, and we do not sell personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and settings: your credits, level, vehicles, upgrades, paint and livery choices, mission results, control and audio settings and graphics quality are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Settings, Privacy, Erase progress, removes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob: a banner in the garage and menus, a full-screen ad between missions, and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch for a bonus. Ads never appear while you are driving. Subscribers to the ad-free plan see no ads.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device and app information such as device model, operating system, language, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions) a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears first, and ads are requested only if you allow it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Privacy, Ad privacy choices (shown where required). You can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

The optional ad-free subscription and the Collector Livery Pack are handled by Google Play Billing. We receive only confirmation from Google Play that a purchase or subscription is active, so the game can unlock what you bought. We never see your card or payment details. Subscription terms are shown in the game's Store screen and in our Terms of Use.

Permissions

The game asks for internet access (to load ads and talk to Google Play), network state, billing, the advertising ID, and vibration (for optional haptics). It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Children

Offroad: Wild Summit is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

Because your game data lives only on your device, you control it: erase progress inside the game, or uninstall it. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
