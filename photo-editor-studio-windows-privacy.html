Photo Editor Studio

by Ecom Swift LLC

Privacy policy

Photo Editor Studio edits your photos on your own PC. We do not collect your photos or any personal data, and the app has no account, no analytics and no ads.

Effective date: 29 October 2026

Stays on your PC

  • Your photos, projects and exports
  • The AI background remover and its model
  • Your settings, saved looks and autosave copies

Goes online

  • Only the Microsoft Store license check that confirms Premium, and purchases you start, both handled by Windows and Microsoft Store

Who we are

Photo Editor Studio is made by Ecom Swift LLC, a company registered in Wyoming, USA. In this policy, "we" means Ecom Swift LLC and "the app" means Photo Editor Studio for Windows.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not send your photos, your edits, your files, your usage or any personal information to us or to anyone else. It contains no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting service, no advertising and no tracking of any kind. You do not need an account to use it.

Your photos and the AI model

Every edit, every export and the AI background remover run on your PC. The AI model is built into the app and runs locally, so a photo is never uploaded to remove its background or to select a subject. The app only opens the files and folders you choose.

Purchases and the Store license

Premium is sold as a Microsoft Store add-on. Microsoft handles the purchase and your payment details; we never see them. To know whether Premium is active, the app asks Windows for the app's Microsoft Store license. This check, and any purchase you start, is the only time the app goes online. These requests are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Files the app keeps on your PC

To work properly, the app stores a few files in its own local data folder on your PC:

  • your settings, such as theme, language and export choices, and the list of recent projects;
  • autosave copies of unsaved edits, so you can recover your work if the app closes unexpectedly (you can turn autosave off in Settings);
  • looks you save yourself;
  • a count of today's free AI background removals.

These files never leave your PC, and Windows removes them when you uninstall the app. Photos, projects and exports you save go only to the places you choose.

Photo metadata in exports

Exported files never contain camera data (EXIF) or the GPS location where a photo was taken. The app removes this information on every export, including batch exports, so you can share your pictures without revealing where they were made. Your original files are never changed.

Links that open outside the app

A few buttons, such as Manage subscription, Rate the app and the support email address, open your web browser, Microsoft Store or your email app. What happens there is covered by the privacy policy of that service.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children under 13 or under the age of digital consent where you live.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and its effective date before the change reaches the app.
