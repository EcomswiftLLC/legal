Privacy policy

Invoice Maker: Estimates & Receipts for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 1 November 2026.

In short

  • Invoice Maker creates your invoices, estimates, receipts and reports on your PC. Your documents, clients, items, prices and settings stay in a file on your computer. The app never uploads them to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • Invoice Maker itself sends no data over the internet. The only online parts are Microsoft Store purchases, licence checks and update checks, which Windows and the Microsoft Store handle, and pages that open in your browser or the Microsoft Store app when you click a link.

What the app reads

Invoice Maker reads the files you choose with the Windows file picker: a logo or signature image, a CSV file you import (clients, items or past invoices), and a backup file you restore. It reads them only to use them inside the app. Nothing it reads is sent anywhere.

What the app writes

Invoice Maker writes the files you ask for, where you choose to save them: PDF and PNG documents, CSV and Excel exports, e-invoice files (UBL and Factur-X) and backups. When you choose to email a document, the app opens a draft in your default email program with the details filled in. Invoice Maker does not send the email; you do, from your own email program.

What the app stores, and where

Invoice Maker keeps its data in its own app data folder on your PC: your business profiles (including any bank details and payment links you typed in), clients, items, documents, payments, settings, and automatic daily backups of that data (the last 14 are kept). A small file there records your Premium licence so the app can unlock Premium while you are offline, and a small setting records when the app last asked you to rate it. Uninstalling Invoice Maker removes this folder. If you use Windows backup or a sync service for your user files, these files may be included in it, under that service's terms. Protect them as you would any file with business information.

Purchases

Invoice Maker Premium is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons (a monthly or yearly subscription and a one-time lifetime purchase). The Microsoft Store handles payment, renewal, refunds and cancellation under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. Invoice Maker never sees your payment details. To check your plan, the app asks Windows' Store service for the licences of your Microsoft account; Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your account details.

Rating request

From time to time, after you have saved or exported a document, the app may offer to open its Microsoft Store page so you can rate it. The offer is the same for everyone and is shown at most once every 48 hours. It sends nothing to us. You can turn it off in Settings, under "Occasionally ask me to rate the app".

Children

Invoice Maker is a business tool, does not collect personal information and has no communication features.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
