Lumina Photo Editor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Lumina is a photo editor made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos are edited on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your pictures, and we don't sell personal information.

Your photos

  • You choose what Lumina sees: the app opens only the photos you pick with Android's photo picker, take with the camera app, or send to it from another app. It does not browse your gallery.
  • Everything happens on your device: filters, adjustments, cutouts, collages, text and stickers are processed locally. The person cutout uses an on-device model from Google ML Kit that ships inside the app; the picture is not sent anywhere for it.
  • What gets saved: when you tap Save, the finished picture is written to Pictures/Lumina on your phone. If you tap Share, it is handed to the app you pick. Lumina itself keeps only a short list of your recent edits so the home screen can show them, plus your settings (theme, export format and quality). Uninstalling the app removes them.

Permissions

Lumina asks for no camera, contacts, location or microphone permission. On Android 9 and older it asks for permission to save to your Pictures folder. It uses the internet only to load ads.

Advertising

Lumina is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home and save screens, never while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a photo, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video appears only when you choose to watch one to unlock the bonus filters and effects for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Ratings

After you have saved a few photos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Children

Lumina is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
