QR Code Scanner: Scanloom: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Scanloom (QR Code Scanner: Scanloom) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. The codes you scan and make are stored only on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data.

What Scanloom stores on your phone

  • Your scans: the text a code contained (for example a web address, a Wi-Fi name, a contact or a product number), its type, the time you scanned it, and any note, favorite mark or folder you add. They are kept in a database in the app's private storage. You can turn history off in Settings, delete it at any time, and remove single scans.
  • Codes you make: the text, the colours and the style, so you can open and edit them later.
  • Settings: sound, vibration, theme and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

Scanloom does not read your contacts, messages, location or microphone, and it keeps no pictures of what the camera sees.

The camera and pictures

Scanloom asks for the camera permission only to read codes. The picture from the camera is analysed on your phone by a barcode reader that is bundled inside the app (Google ML Kit, bundled model, and the open source ZXing library). Nothing is uploaded, and the camera picture is not saved. You can also choose a picture with Android's photo picker, or share a picture to Scanloom from another app. It is read on your phone and not kept, and it needs no storage or photo permission.

The link check

When a code holds a web address, Scanloom looks at the address on your phone for common warning signs, such as a shortened link, a name that imitates a well-known brand, a hidden address before an @ sign or an address that is not encrypted. The address is not sent anywhere to be checked, and Scanloom never opens a link by itself. The check cannot promise that a site is safe, so always read the address before you continue.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: sharing a spreadsheet (CSV), a list, or a code as a picture, SVG or PDF; saving a picture to your gallery; or tapping a button that hands a scanned item to another app, for example opening a link in your browser, a map location in your maps app, a phone number in your dialer, or a product page on Open Food Facts in your browser. These go to the app or folder you pick, and that app's own privacy policy applies from then on.

Joining a Wi-Fi network uses Android's own confirmation. Saving a contact or an event opens your contacts or calendar app with the details filled in, and you decide whether to save them.

Permissions

  • Camera: to read codes. Android asks when you first open the Scan tab. You can say no and still read codes from pictures and make codes.
  • Vibration: a light tap when a code is read. You can turn it off in Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Scanloom asks for no location, microphone, contacts, storage or notification permission.

Advertising

Scanloom is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Ads never appear over the camera. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). Ad requests do not include what you scanned.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after your fifth scan or your first exported code, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while the camera is open.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Scanloom has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Scanloom is a general-audience tool for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
