Install and use guide

From install to your first reviews

Five steps, in the order most stores do them. Each step says what to click and what you should see. The same steps are in the app under Get started.

Before you start

  • You need a Shopify store and permission to install apps.
  • Reviewly shows reviews with theme app blocks, which Shopify supports in Online Store 2.0 themes. If your theme cannot add app blocks to the product page, the blocks cannot be placed.
  • Verified buyer badges and review request e-mails read your orders. Shopify requires an approval called protected customer data access for that. Everything else works without it, and the Home page tells you when it is missing.
  • Reviewly is currently free to use.

The steps

  1. Install and connect

    Install Reviewly from the Shopify App Store. Shopify lists the permissions the app asks for and asks you to approve them. The reasons are explained on the privacy page.

    The app opens inside your Shopify admin. The first time, it prepares the places where reviews, questions and settings are kept in your own store, and connects your store to the part that receives reviews from your storefront. There is nothing to copy or paste.

    What you will see

    The Home page with a card called Finish setting up Reviewly, a progress bar and one row for each unfinished step. The first two rows, Review storage is set up in your store and Connected for storefront reviews and e-mails, change to Ready and Connected after a few seconds. If a row shows an error, press Check again.

  2. Add the reviews block to your theme

    Reviews appear on your store through blocks that you place in the theme editor.

    1. In Reviewly, open Get started (or Home) and press Add the reviews block. Your theme editor opens in a new tab.
    2. If it does not open on the product page, go to Online Store, Themes, Customize in your Shopify admin and choose the product template.
    3. Add the block named Product reviews (under Apps) below the product description, then press Save in the theme editor.
    4. Back in Reviewly, press Add star ratings and add Star rating near the product title.
    5. Press Mark as done on Home. Shopify does not let an app see which blocks a theme uses, so this one is your confirmation.

    Other blocks you can add the same way: Review carousel, Photo wall, All reviews (for a page of its own), Trust badge and Questions and answers.

    Two features are app embeds, which you switch on under App embeds in the theme editor’s left sidebar instead of placing them on a page: Product card stars, which adds a small rating to product cards on collection and search pages, and Review rich results, which prints the structured data search engines read. Whether a search engine shows stars is up to the search engine.

    What you will see

    In the theme editor, the block appears in the list on the left and a preview in the middle. Until the product has reviews, the block shows a short invitation to write the first one. On your live store, the same block shows the star summary, the reviews and a Write a review button.

  3. Set how the widget looks

    Open Widget settings.

    • Look: choose a layout, light, dark or automatic colors, and the colors themselves.
    • Features: switch photos, videos, helpful votes, search, sorting, filters, the verified badge, abuse reports, the review form and questions on or off.
    • Lists: how many reviews load per page, and which reviews the carousel, photo wall and all reviews page highlight.
    • Wording: replace any text shoppers see. An empty field keeps the default.

    The preview next to the form follows your choices before you save. When you change something, a save bar appears at the top of the admin with Save and Discard.

    What you will see

    A preview of a rating summary and one review, which changes as you edit. If you pick colors that are hard to read, a warning banner says which ones.

  4. Add or import reviews

    Import a file. Open Import. Choose a CSV or JSON export from another review app, or paste the text.

    1. Choose your export. Reviewly tells you which format it recognised. Files can be up to 25 MB and 20,000 rows.
    2. Check the columns. Confirm which column holds the rating, text, name, date and so on. You see a preview before anything is written. Only the rating is required.
    3. Match products. Reviews are matched to your products by id, handle or title. You can pick a product for the ones that do not match.
    4. Run the import. It runs in steps and you can pause it. Photo and video links are fetched by Shopify from the original addresses. A list of rows that could not be imported can be downloaded.

    Add one by hand. In Reviews, press Add a review, choose the product, and enter the rating, name and text. You can attach photos or videos. Only add words from customers who agreed to share them. Reviews added by hand never get the Verified buyer badge.

    What you will see

    Reviews lists every review with its status: Published, Pending, Hidden or Spam. Click a review to read it, reply publicly, publish, hide or delete it.

  5. Turn on review requests

    Review requests are off until you choose a mode. Open Review requests.

    • Send e-mails after orders are fulfilled: Reviewly e-mails the customer after the number of days you set (5 by default), with up to two reminders. Each e-mail lists the products of the order with a row of stars and a link that opens the review form. Every e-mail has an unsubscribe link.
    • No e-mails: Reviewly sends nothing. Shoppers can still write a review from the product page. Prompts on the Shopify thank-you page and in customer accounts are not available in this release.
    • Off: no requests.

    In the e-mail mode, set the sender name, the reply-to address, the subject and the introduction. Add a postal address for the footer, which e-mail law in many countries requires. By default e-mails go out through a shared sending service operated by Ecom Swift LLC with a limit per store per day (25 by default). You can instead paste your own Resend or Brevo key, which is stored encrypted. Use Send a test e-mail to see one first. You can skip orders with certain tags, for example wholesale.

    The first time you switch e-mails on, Reviewly looks at orders fulfilled in the last 14 days only. Orders older than 60 days never get a request. The check runs once an hour, so an e-mail goes out within about an hour of becoming due.

    Optionally you can give a discount code for reviews that include a photo or video. The settings page requires a disclosure to be shown next to the review form, and reviews are published whether they are positive or negative.

    What you will see

    A live preview of the e-mail on the right of the settings, using a sample product. On Home, the row Asking customers for reviews changes from Off to E-mail on.

Day to day

  • Reviews is your queue. Filter by status, rating, buyer, photos or product. Select several reviews to publish, hide or mark as spam together.
  • Moderation rules decide what is published automatically. By default everything is published unless it contains a link, an e-mail address or phone number, a blocked word, spam-like text or a duplicate. A review is never held because of its star rating. The Test a review box shows what a rule would do.
  • Questions lists questions shoppers asked on product pages. A question is shown on the product page after you answer and publish it.
  • Analytics shows rating over time, the star distribution, common topics and your reply times.
  • Home shows what needs attention and, under Maintenance, a button to recompute star ratings if a number ever looks wrong.
  • Optional AI help runs only when you press its button: moderation suggestions for pending reviews and reply drafts. Each store has a small number of AI uses per day.

If something is wrong

The questions and answers cover the common cases: reviews not showing, photos still processing, a missing verified badge and e-mails not arriving. If you are still stuck, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com with your store address and what you see.

Uninstalling

Remove the Reviewly blocks in the theme editor, then in your Shopify admin open Settings, Apps and sales channels, choose Reviewly and uninstall. Your reviews are stored in your own store. Shopify removes the app’s data definitions when an app is uninstalled and keeps the entries for a period it does not publish, so export anything you want to keep first. The details are in the privacy policy.
