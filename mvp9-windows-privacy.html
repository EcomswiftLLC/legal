Privacy policy

MVP9 Media Player for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC (Wyoming, USA). Effective 2 November 2026.

In short

  • MVP9 Media Player plays music and video files on your PC, and web addresses that you enter. Your files never leave your PC. Nothing is uploaded to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no telemetry, no crash reporting and no tracking. The app contains no third-party software that collects data.
  • Ecom Swift LLC does not collect any personal data through the app.

What the app stores, and where

Everything below is kept only in the app's own data folder on your PC:

  • Your settings.
  • Playlists and smart playlists.
  • Play counts, playback positions (so you can resume where you stopped) and the list of recent items.
  • The music and video library index: file paths and tags read from your files.
  • Cached album art and video preview frames.
  • A cache of audio files converted to FLAC. You can clear this cache in Settings.

Screenshots you take of a video are saved to your Pictures folder. After that they are ordinary files on your PC.

Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

What goes online

The app connects to the internet only in these cases:

  • Streams you open. When you enter a web address (http, https or an HLS stream), the request goes directly from your PC to that server. That server may log your IP address under its own policy. Ecom Swift LLC is not involved and receives nothing.
  • Microsoft Store. Licence checks, in-app purchases and the update check are handled by Windows and the Microsoft Store under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.
  • Links you click. A link in the app, such as the support email address or our website, opens in your default browser or email program. Those pages are covered by their own policies.

Nothing else is sent: no usage statistics, no crash reports and no third-party services.

Purchases

Premium is sold only through the Microsoft Store as add-ons: Premium Monthly (2.99 USD), Premium Yearly (19.99 USD) and Premium Lifetime (29.99 USD). The Microsoft Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy. Microsoft handles payment, renewal, refunds and cancellation. Ecom Swift LLC never sees your card or payment details.

Your rights (GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA and similar laws)

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR and the California Consumer Privacy Act give you rights to access, correct, delete or move personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing. Because Ecom Swift LLC collects no personal data through MVP9 Media Player, there is nothing on our side to access, correct, export or delete, and we do not sell or share any personal data.

The data the app keeps on your PC is under your control. You can clear the FLAC cache in Settings, delete playlists and history in the app, or uninstall the app to remove its data folder. For data handled by Microsoft, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. For data held by a stream server you open, contact that server's operator.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.
