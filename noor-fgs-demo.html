Noor — Media Playback Foreground Service

Ecom Swift LLC · demonstration for the Google Play foreground-service declaration

What the video shows

  1. The user opens Audio & Reciters and taps play on a surah (Al-Faatiha) to start audio recitation.
  2. A media-style notification appears with play/pause, previous, and a seek bar.
  3. The user leaves the app (Home). Recitation continues in the background and the media notification persists, with the seek bar advancing — this is the FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK service in use.

How to reproduce in the app

  1. Open Noor → bottom navigation More → Audio & Reciters.
  2. Tap the play icon next to any surah.
  3. Press Home; the recitation keeps playing with a notification and lock-screen controls.
