Using PCCO safely

You choose what is cleaned. Every scan shows what it found before anything is removed, and a dry run shows what a clean would do without deleting anything. Your own files are moved to the Recycle Bin. Even so, deleting files cannot always be undone: emptying the Recycle Bin, privacy cleanup and cache deletion are permanent. Review the list before you clean and keep backups of important files.

What PCCO does not promise

PCCO reports what Windows and your drives report, and the sizes it measured. The Health Score is an indicator built from those readings; it is not a guarantee about your PC. PCCO does not promise that your PC will become faster, and it does not include a registry cleaner or a memory booster. It is not an antivirus or security product.
