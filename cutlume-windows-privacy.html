Cutlume Privacy Policy

Cutlume (Photo Background Remover: Cutlume) for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Effective 2026-11-23.

Summary

Cutlume removes backgrounds from photos on your PC. Your photos never leave the computer: Cutlume does not upload, analyse or store them anywhere else. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your PC

  • Your settings, the list of recent photos (file path and a small thumbnail) and your saved work on each photo (the edit settings and the cut-out mask), in the app's data folder.
  • Background pictures you add, pictures you paste, and batch results waiting to be exported, in the same folder.
  • Files you export are saved only where you choose. Camera details (EXIF) are removed from exports unless you switch on "Keep camera details".

You can clear the recent photos and their saved work in the app at any time, and remove everything by uninstalling Cutlume.

How the cutout works

The background removal runs on your PC with an open source model that is installed with the app. The photo is processed in memory on your computer. No image, mask or result is sent over the network.

Network use

  • Microsoft Store: to check your purchases and export balance, show prices, process purchases and show the rating dialog, the app uses Microsoft Store services. Payments are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your card details.
  • Links you click (support email, this policy, the terms) open in your browser or mail app.
  • Nothing else: Cutlume sends no photos, files or usage data to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

Children

Cutlume collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
