Privacy Policy - Profit Compass

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Profit Compass is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that shows a Wix Stores merchant their net profit by combining their store orders and product costs with the fees, shipping, ad spend and expenses the merchant enters. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs the app (the "merchant") is the controller of their store data. Ecom Swift LLC provides the software. The app is a dashboard for the merchant only: it has no visitor-facing component and collects nothing from shoppers.

1. What the app processes

WhatWhy
Store orders and catalog, read from Wix eCommerce and Wix Stores while the merchant has the dashboard open: order totals, discounts, shipping, taxes, line items, product names and the product cost the merchant saved in Wix. Buyer names, e-mail addresses and addresses are not used or stored by the app.To calculate revenue, cost and profit per order and per product.
What the merchant types in: payment fee rates, shipping cost, default product cost, target margin, per-product cost overrides, ad spend entries and expenses.To calculate profit and show it back.

The app has no analytics or tracking, sets no cookies and reads no payment details or passwords.

2. Where data is stored

Merchant-entered data is stored in private data collections that belong to the app on the merchant's own Wix site (Wix Data). They can only be read and changed through the app's backend after it checks the caller is the site owner or a collaborator. Order and catalog data is read live from Wix and is not copied into any other database. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive this data.

3. Other service providers

  • Wix hosts the app and stores its data, provides orders and the product catalog, and identifies the site owner.

The app has no servers of its own and uses no AI model provider, analytics service, advertising network or e-mail service. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Costs, ad spend and expenses are kept until the merchant edits or deletes them in the app.
  • When the app is removed, Wix controls what stays reachable. The app's data collections may remain in the site's data until the merchant deletes them. Write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will help.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every write goes through a backend endpoint that first checks the caller is the site owner or a collaborator and validates all numbers and text. Raw technical errors are not shown to users.

6. Browser storage

The dashboard may keep a few small values in the browser (for example the selected period). They contain no personal information. Links to the Wix App Market, the Wix Experts Marketplace, a WhatsApp chat with Ecom Swift LLC and our support e-mail send information to those services only if clicked.

7. Your rights

Merchants can view, edit and delete everything the app stores for their site, and can ask us to help with any request. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data. We respond to requests within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

8. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify merchants in the app.
