Privacy Policy — Robots & Meta-Robots Inspector

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Robots & Meta-Robots Inspector tells you whether the current page is crawlable by reading its meta robots tag, its X-Robots-Tag header and the site's robots.txt. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the current page's meta robots tags from the active tab, only when you ask.
  • To read the X-Robots-Tag header and the crawl rules, your browser requests the current page and the site's /robots.txt directly. These requests go to the website being inspected — never to us — and are used only to show the header, the rules, and a crawlable/blocked verdict.
  • Rule matching and the optional path tester run locally in your browser. Settings and any history are stored locally. CSV exports are saved by you, to your computer.

Permissions

  • Host access is required so your browser can request the page's headers and its robots.txt. It is used only for sites you choose to inspect.
  • activeTab / scripting let the extension read the meta robots tag on the current page.
  • alarms is used only for the optional reminder described below.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you inspect, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
