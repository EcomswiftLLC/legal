1. The App

Pitchloom listens to an instrument through the microphone to show the note and how far it is from pitch, plays a metronome click, keeps a list of saved tempos and times your practice. Your data is stored on your device. We may add, change or remove features over time.

2. Accuracy and use

Pitch readings depend on your phone's microphone, the room and the instrument, and can be wrong in loud places or with very quiet or very low sounds. The metronome click depends on your phone's audio output, which can add a small delay on some devices. Pitchloom is a practice aid and not a measuring instrument. Keep the volume at a level that is safe for your hearing. Screen flash and beat lights can be turned off in Settings, and the screen flash is limited to three pulses a second.
