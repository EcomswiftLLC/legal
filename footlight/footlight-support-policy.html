Footlight support policy

How we help, how fast we reply, and what to send so we can fix things quickly.

How fast we reply

We reply to every message within 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, except public holidays in the United States. You can reach us by email at support@ecomswiftllc.com or through the Footlight support page.

A reply within 2 business days means we have read your request and answered it, or told you what we need to answer it. Some fixes take longer than a reply, and we will say so.

What support covers

  • Installing Footlight and publishing it.
  • Questions about how the theme works and how to use its settings, sections and blocks.
  • Bugs in the theme, such as broken layouts, controls that do not work, dead links and logic errors that appear in the theme as we shipped it.

We fix confirmed bugs in the theme and release the fixes as theme updates through the Shopify Theme Store. Problems that stop shoppers from buying are handled first.

What support does not cover

These are not part of the theme price. We offer them separately as paid services, and you can ask us for a quote.

  • Custom code changes and custom design work, including edits to the theme's files.
  • Installing, configuring or fixing third-party apps. If a problem comes from an app, contact the app's developer.
  • Adding your products, collections and content, or moving a store from another theme or platform.
  • Re-applying custom code after a theme update.
  • Problems that come from your Shopify plan or your domain provider.

Before you change theme code, duplicate your theme and work on the copy. Code changes are not supported, and they can make later updates harder to apply.

How to send a good request

A request that has the details below can usually be answered in one reply.

Store address
For example https://your-store.myshopify.com.
Example page
The full address of a page where the problem shows. For a product problem, include an example product address.
Theme name and version
Footlight, and the version number shown in the theme editor under Theme settings.
Browser and device
For example Safari on an iPhone, or Chrome on Windows.
What happened
What you expected, what you saw instead, and the steps that lead to it.
Screenshots
A screenshot or a short screen recording helps. You can attach files with the support form.

Store access and privacy

  • Do not send passwords, payment details or customer data in a message.
  • If we need to look inside your store, we will ask you to send a staff account request that you control. You can remove access when the work is done.
  • We use the details you send only to answer your request.

More help

Many questions are answered in the Footlight documentation, which describes every setting by the name shown in the theme editor.
