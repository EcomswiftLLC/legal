Gaugewell for Windows: privacy policy

Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com. Last updated 2026-11-23.

Gaugewell (PC Maintenance & Health: Gaugewell) reads information about your PC so it can show its health: drives and free space, disk health counters, battery capacity, startup entries, installed programs, Windows update and driver dates, and running processes while the Live page is open. It reads file names and sizes in your temporary, cache and personal folders to show what can be cleared.

All of this stays on your PC. Gaugewell has no account, no ads, no analytics, no telemetry and no crash reporting, and it does not send any of this information to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

Network use: the Microsoft Store checks your Premium licence, shows prices and handles purchases and ratings under Microsoft's own privacy statement. Windows Package Manager (winget), a part of Windows, checks its public catalogue for app updates and, if you choose Update, downloads the update from the app's publisher. Links you click open in your browser.

Files you remove from your own folders are kept in a quarantine inside the app's data folder for 30 days so you can restore them, then deleted. The activity log, settings and scan history are stored in the same folder and are removed when you uninstall Gaugewell.

Children: Gaugewell is not directed at children and collects no personal information.
