Phone & Storage Cleaner: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Your privacy is the foundation of this app. All scanning, analysis, compression and cleaning happen entirely on your device. We do not operate a server that receives your files, and we never upload your photos, videos, documents, file contents, contacts or other personal data.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Photos and videos (media permissions): to find large files, identical and similar copies, blurry photos, screenshots and old media, to search your files, and to compress photos and videos. Media is read on your device only.
  • Downloads and folders you open in the file browser (Storage Access Framework): only the folders you explicitly choose are accessed.
  • Usage access (optional): if you grant it, the app can show real per-app storage sizes and last-used times in Apps. The app works without it.
  • Notifications (optional): if you turn on Auto-clean, the app sends one quiet notification when a rule you chose fires, such as a weekly storage check or low storage. The check runs on your device and you can turn it off at any time.
  • Screen lock and biometrics (Private Vault): the app asks Android to confirm it is you. Android checks your fingerprint, face or screen lock. The app never receives or stores that data.

Private Vault

Files you hide are encrypted on your device with a key that stays in the Android Keystore, and are stored only in this app's private storage. They are never uploaded. The vault locks when you leave the app. Uninstalling the app removes the vault, so restore anything you want to keep first.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not upload or back up your files.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not claim to clean other apps' data or perform system-level optimizations that Android does not allow.
  • Nothing is deleted or replaced without your confirmation in Android's own dialog.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may use an advertising identifier to serve and measure ads. In regions where consent is required, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown. You can also choose to watch a short optional ad to try one Premium tool for 24 hours. Premium stops ad requests. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Premium is handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details.

Data we store

The app stores only small on-device preferences (such as your theme choice, a local list of your past cleanups, when a 24 hour trial ends, and your Auto-clean rules) and, if you use the Private Vault, the encrypted files you hid. Uninstalling the app removes them. Files you delete through the app are removed by Android with your confirmation, or moved to Android's Trash for 30 days if you turn that option on.

Children

The app is a general-purpose utility and is not directed at children.
