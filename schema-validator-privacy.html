Privacy Policy — Schema & JSON-LD Validator

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Schema & JSON-LD Validator detects and validates the structured data on the page you are viewing. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the current page's own structured data — its JSON-LD scripts, Microdata and RDFa — from the active tab. It does this only for the tab you are on, only when you ask.
  • All detection, pretty-printing, validation, rich-result checks and schema generation run locally in your browser. The page content is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Your settings and your saved scan history (a Pro feature) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you scan, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
