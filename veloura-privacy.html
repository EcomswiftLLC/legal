Velvessa Skin Care Routine: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Velvessa (Skin Care Routine) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your routines, products, diary and photos are stored on your phone and never leave it unless you choose to save or share a file. We don't run a server that receives your data, we have no analytics of our own, and we don't sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your care data: your quiz answers and profile, routines and steps, reminders, your products shelf (including ingredient lists, prices, opened dates, notes and verdicts), your wishlist, the care logs for hair and nails, water and sunscreen tracking, insights and settings are kept in a private database inside the app.
  • Your diary: daily check-ins about skin, hair, stress, sleep hours, water, weather, tags and notes, plus the optional cycle day field, are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to us, to advertisers or to anyone else.
  • Ingredient checks: the ingredient dictionary is built into the app and works offline. Ingredient lists you paste or type are checked on your phone and are not uploaded.
  • Android cloud backup is turned off for the app's data, so Android does not copy it to Google Drive or to a new phone. To move your data, use the app's own backup (see below).

Photos and camera

  • Private photos: diary photos and product photos are stored only inside the app's private storage. They are not added to your gallery and other apps cannot see them. You can delete one photo or all photos at any time.
  • Camera: Velvessa asks for camera permission only when you open the diary camera, and uses the camera only while that screen is open. The face guide and alignment overlay are drawn on screen; there is no face recognition or face analysis.
  • Gallery photos: to add an existing picture, the app uses Android's system Photo Picker, which gives it only the picture you select. It does not browse your gallery and asks for no storage permission.

Diary lock

You can lock the diary with a PIN. The PIN is never stored as typed: the app keeps only a salted hash of it on your phone. If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, Android handles the check. Velvessa never receives your fingerprint, face data or any biometric information; it only learns whether the unlock succeeded.

Notifications

Velvessa asks for notification permission when you turn on a reminder (routine, water, sunscreen, diary, product replace-by, nail care or weekly summary). Reminders are scheduled on your phone and planned again after a restart or a time zone change. No reminder content is sent anywhere.

Backups and exports

You can save a backup zip file (optionally with photos) and export CSV files of your routines, diary, products and care log. These files are created only when you ask, and you choose where they go, for example a folder on your phone or an app in the share sheet. Once you save or share a file, keeping it safe is up to you, and the privacy policy of the place you send it to applies.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data erases everything the app stores on your phone, including photos. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover deleted data.

Other permissions

Velvessa asks for no location, contacts, microphone or storage permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases, and it re-plans reminders after your phone restarts.

Advertising

Velvessa is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Ads never receive your routines, products, diary, photos or any other content from the app.

Banners appear on list screens (Today, Shelf, Insights, Discover and Settings). Ads never appear during a routine, a step timer, a diary entry or the camera. A full-screen ad may appear occasionally after you leave a finished routine, limited in number and spacing, and never after the first routine you finish. A rewarded video plays only when you tap the offer, for example to unlock one use of a Premium feature or one extra routine or product slot.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

The app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Velvessa has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Velvessa is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Cosmetic information only

Velvessa offers cosmetic guidance and general information, not medical advice. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose any condition. See our Terms of Use for details.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
