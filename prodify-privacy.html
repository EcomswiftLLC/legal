Privacy Policy - Prodify: Sticky Cart & Badges

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Prodify: Sticky Cart & Badges is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that adds trust badges, a sticky add-to-cart bar, size charts, product tabs, a free-shipping progress bar and benefit rows to a Wix Stores product page. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives and why.

Short version. The app stores the settings and content the site owner writes, plus anonymous daily counters. It does not collect names, emails, addresses, payment details or any other personal data about the site's visitors, and it sets no cookies. Wix hosts the app and keeps its data on the site owner's own Wix site. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive a copy of that data.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Ecom Swift LLC. You can reach us at wix@ecomswiftllc.com.

3. Where data is stored

The app has no database of its own. Its settings and counters live in two private data collections (Wix Data) on the site owner's own Wix site, in a space reserved for the app. Each site has its own collections, so one site's data is never mixed with another's. Only site administrators and the app's backend, after Wix has verified the caller, can read or change them. The app needs the Wix CMS (Wix Data) on the site for this.

The visitor's browser may keep a short-lived copy of the block configuration (a few kilobytes, not personal) in session storage for the length of the visit so the page loads faster. It is cleared when the browser session ends. The app sets no cookies and uses no local tracking identifiers.

4. Cookie consent

Anonymous counters are sent only when the site's cookie banner settings allow analytics for that visitor. If the visitor has not allowed analytics, the blocks still display normally and nothing is counted. Site owners remain responsible for their own cookie banner and privacy policy.

5. Rate limiting

To protect the service from abuse, the backend briefly keeps a short one-way hash of the network address of requests in the memory of the running server, to count requests per minute. It is not written to disk, not logged and not linked to any data above, and it disappears within minutes.

6. Service providers

  • Wix hosts the app and the data, provides the product and cart data, and identifies the site owner.

We do not sell personal data and do not share data for advertising. We receive no copy of the site's data and operate no other server that handles it.

When the app is removed, Wix handles what happens to its data collections according to Wix's app removal rules. Data that Wix itself holds (products, orders, contacts) is governed by Wix and the site owner's Wix account, not by this app.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every dashboard request is checked against a verified Wix identity before anything is read or written, and every input is validated on the server. Public endpoints return only the configuration the site owner chose to show visitors, and are rate limited. Merchant-written text is shown as plain text, never as HTML. No system is perfectly secure, and the data on the site is also protected by the site owner's own Wix account security.

9. International transfers

Wix operates globally, so data may be processed in countries other than where the site owner or the visitor lives. Wix applies its own transfer safeguards. We do not transfer personal data anywhere else.

10. Your rights

The app holds no personal data about site visitors. A site owner can view, export and delete everything the app stores for their site from the app's Settings, and can contact us at wix@ecomswiftllc.com with any privacy question. Visitors who have questions about their data on a store should contact the store first.

11. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age). It does not knowingly process children's data.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify site owners in the app.
