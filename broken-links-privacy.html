Privacy Policy — Broken Link Checker

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Broken Link Checker scans the links on the page you are viewing and reports each one's live HTTP status (such as 404 or 500) so you can find and fix dead links. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you press Scan, the extension reads the links (<a href>) on the active tab, only for the tab you are on and only when you ask.
  • To report each link's status, your browser then requests each of those link URLs directly (a normal HEAD or GET, exactly as if you clicked the link) and shows the status code it returns. These requests go to the websites the links point to — never to us. The responses are used only to show a status and are not stored or transmitted to Ecom Swift LLC.
  • Results, settings and any history are kept locally on your device via your browser's storage. CSV exports are saved by you, to your computer.

Permissions

  • Host access is required so your browser can request each link and read its status code. It is used only for the links on pages you choose to scan.
  • activeTab / scripting let the extension read the links on the current page when you open it.
  • alarms is used only for the optional reminder described below.

Reminders

  • If you are on the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours to show what Pro adds. Paying users are never shown it, and it includes a "Don't remind me again" option that turns it off for good. It opens a page that ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages or links you scan, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
