Gaugio: Car Log & Fuel Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Gaugio (Car Log & Fuel Tracker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it, and it never uses your location. The records you add are stored on your phone and never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Gaugio stores on your phone

  • Your records: vehicle names, make and model lines, years, plates, odometer readings, fill-ups (date, quantity, price, total, station, fuel grade, notes), services, expenses, reminders, tires and parts, and optional vehicle and receipt photos you pick.
  • Settings: units, currency, reminder times, theme, accent colour and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Photos you choose with Android's photo picker are copied into the app's private storage; Gaugio does not get access to the rest of your photos. Gaugio does not connect to your car, and it does not read your messages, your email, your contacts or your notifications. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Android's phone-to-phone transfer during setup of a new phone may carry the data over. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Delete all my data" in Settings removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: saving a backup file, exporting a spreadsheet (CSV) or sharing a PDF cost report saves or shares a file to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker or share sheet. That place or app then handles the file under its own privacy policy. Importing a file reads only the file you pick, on your phone.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for service reminders. Android asks at the moment you set one up, and you can decline. Reminders arrive around the time you chose; Android may move them by a few minutes to save battery.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Gaugio plans your reminders again.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Gaugio asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission, and it does not use exact alarms.

Advertising

Gaugio is free to start and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your records, prices and notes are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on list screens such as Garage, Log, Due, Stats and Settings, never while you add or edit a record. A full-screen ad may appear on the Garage screen after you save a record or export a file, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium tool for 24 hours or to add room for one more vehicle or three more reminders.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a good moment, for example after you have logged a few fill-ups, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Gaugio has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Accuracy and advice

Gaugio keeps manual records. Figures such as fuel economy, cost per distance and reminder dates are worked out from what you typed in. Gaugio does not diagnose your vehicle and does not give legal, tax or safety advice.

Children

Gaugio is made for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
