What Linelens does

Linelens reads text and gives an estimate of how likely it is to contain AI-generated writing, with reasons. It can also show what a picture file says about itself (camera, editing software, Content Credentials), without guessing from the pixels. It is a reading aid. It is not proof and it is not a verdict. It must not be the only evidence that a person used AI, and you must not use it to accuse, discipline, punish or make decisions about a person without other evidence and without hearing that person.

Results can be wrong

Results can be wrong in both directions, and are less steady on short, edited, formal or non-native writing and on languages other than English. The text check is a rough impression from an AI model combined with on-device signals: it can flag a person's own writing and it can miss AI-written text. We make no promise about accuracy and we do not state an accuracy figure. Missing picture details prove nothing, and details that are present can be added by anyone.

Daily checks, Premium and ads

  • Quick checks on your phone are free. Full checks are limited: free use includes 10 full checks a day for each install (text up to 4,000 characters), which reset at midnight UTC. When the shared daily capacity of the service is used up, a full check may pause until it resets.
  • Premium is a paid subscription sold through Google Play. It gives 100 AI requests a day, longer texts (up to 12,000 characters), priority when the service is busy, the whole picture report, PDF reports and compare, and removes ads. Prices and the billing period are shown in the app before you subscribe, as given by Google Play. There is no free trial.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel them in Google Play, in the Subscriptions section of your account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies.
