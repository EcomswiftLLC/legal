Blockmerge: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

Blockmerge is a number-merging puzzle game made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to play. We don't run a server that collects your game data, and we don't sell personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and settings: your scores, highest block, streak, daily challenge result, missions, the game in progress, theme and sound or haptic settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the app removes them.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Ads never appear during a game. Interstitial ads appear only between games, and rewarded ads appear only when you choose to watch one.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy options. Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Purchases (Merge Plus, Remove Ads and the Theme Pack) are handled by Google Play. We receive only confirmation that a purchase is active, so the game can unlock the features you bought. We never see your payment details.

Children

Blockmerge is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
