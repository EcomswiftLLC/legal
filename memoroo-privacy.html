Memoroo Privacy Policy

Memory Games for Kids: Memoroo, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Made for children

Memoroo is designed for children aged 3 to 8 and follows the Google Play Families Policy. We do not ask a child for personal information and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. There is no account, no sign-in, no name or birthday entry, no photos, camera, microphone, contacts or location, no chat, no social features, no user-generated content and no push notifications.

What stays on the device

Stars, levels played, stickers and their places on the sticker meadow, time played each day, the animal friend and nickname (picked from a fixed list, never typed) and the sound and play-time settings are saved only on the device. We cannot see them. A grown-up can delete them at any time in the Grown-ups corner (Delete all progress) or by uninstalling the app.

Advertising (free game only)

The free game shows ads from Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ad network, only on the home menu, in the Grown-ups corner and between rounds, never during play. Every ad request is marked as child-directed and under the age of consent, with a maximum ad content rating of G, so ads are not personalised and no interest-based advertising or remarketing is used. The app does not read the advertising ID (the permission is removed). As Google describes for child-directed requests, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process the IP address, app or device identifiers other than the advertising ID, and ad interaction and diagnostic data to deliver ads, limit how often they show, measure them and prevent fraud. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

The optional bonus sticker video is only offered to grown-ups inside the Grown-ups corner, behind a parent check. Buying Premium or Remove ads stops all ad requests.

Purchases

The two one-time purchases (Remove ads and Premium) are handled by Google Play. We never see payment details. Every purchase screen, Restore and every link that leaves the app is behind a parent check.

Voice and sounds

All voice clips, music and sounds are bundled in the app and play offline. Nothing the child does is recorded.

Your rights and contact

Because we hold no personal information, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Parents with questions can write to support@ecomswiftllc.com. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
