Privacy policy

Sticky Notes & Quick Notes: NoteDock for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • Your notes stay on your PC. NoteDock has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cloud sync. Nothing you write is sent to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

What NoteDock stores, and where

  • Notes, sticky note positions, tags, folders, reminders, pasted images and settings are stored in a database file in the app's own data folder on your PC.
  • Automatic daily copies of that database are kept in the same folder for 14 days.
  • Backups and exports are written only where you choose to save them.
  • Locked notes are encrypted on your PC with the password you choose (AES-256-GCM, key derived with scrypt). The password is never stored and cannot be recovered by anyone, including Ecom Swift LLC.

When NoteDock uses the internet

  • Microsoft Store, for Premium purchases, licence checks, prices and the rating window you open yourself. Microsoft handles payments and reviews under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. NoteDock does not receive your payment details.
  • Links inside your notes open in your web browser only when you click them. NoteDock never loads pictures from the web inside notes.

No other network connection is made.

Importing from Windows Sticky Notes

When you choose Import from Windows Sticky Notes, NoteDock reads a copy of that app's local database on your PC and copies the notes into NoteDock. The original is not changed, and nothing leaves your PC.

Children

NoteDock does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can export every note as Markdown at any time, delete notes and empty the Trash, or uninstall NoteDock, which removes its data folder. Backups you saved elsewhere stay where you put them.
