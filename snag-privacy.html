Snag: Image & Video Downloader. Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026. Snag is a project of Ecom Swift LLC (support@ecomswiftllc.com).

What Snag does with your data

Snag lists the images, videos and audio on the page you are viewing and saves the files you choose. This happens entirely on your device. We do not collect, store, sell or share the pages you visit, the files you find or the files you download.

What stays on your device

Settings, usage counters (for example how many files you saved, used for your usage report) and your download history are stored in the browser's local extension storage. They never leave your device and are removed when you uninstall Snag.

The only data sent to our server

If you buy Snag Pro, the extension checks your plan on our website. It sends the email address you paid with and the product name, and receives whether the plan is active. If you start checkout, a small script on our checkout page (ecomswiftllc.com only) reads the email you type there so Pro can unlock automatically. Payments are handled by Wix; we never see your card details.

Permissions

Snag asks to read all sites so it can find files on the page you open. It only reads the page; page content is not transmitted. Network requests are observed (never blocked or changed) to detect video and audio files.

Third parties

We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking. Checkout and subscriptions run on Wix.

Your choices

Uninstall Snag to remove all local data. To cancel a plan use Settings, Cancel plan, or email us. Questions: support@ecomswiftllc.com.
