Sticker & Meme Maker: Peelwise: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Peelwise (Sticker & Meme Maker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. The photos, stickers and packs you make are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. Cutting out a subject from a photo happens on your phone, with a model that ships inside the app. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Peelwise stores on your phone

  • Your photos: pictures you choose to turn into a sticker or meme. A copy (at most 1280 pixels on the long side) is kept in the app's private storage so you can edit the sticker later.
  • Your stickers and packs: the cut-out, outline, text, decorations, captions and the packs you group them in, with a small preview picture of each.
  • Settings: your theme, the author name you type for packs, how many exports you have left, and a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Photos are chosen with Android's photo picker, so Peelwise does not get access to the rest of your photos. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Android's phone-to-phone transfer during setup of a new phone may carry the data over. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and you can delete any sticker or pack inside the app at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to:

  • Add to WhatsApp: when you tap Add to WhatsApp, Peelwise builds the pack on your phone and WhatsApp (or WhatsApp Business) reads the sticker files, the pack icon, the pack name and your author name from Peelwise through Android's standard sticker content provider. Only WhatsApp apps that hold WhatsApp's sticker permission can read it. WhatsApp then handles the stickers under its own privacy policy.
  • Save and share: saving a sticker or pack file to Pictures/Peelwise, sharing a picture through Android's share sheet (for example to Telegram or Messenger), or saving a backup file to a place you pick. The app you share to handles the file under its own privacy policy.
  • Restore: reading a backup file you pick, on your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.
  • Billing: used for purchases through Google Play.

Peelwise asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification or storage permission. It checks whether WhatsApp is installed so it can show the Add to WhatsApp button, and reads nothing else from that app.

Advertising

Peelwise is free to start and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your photos, stickers and packs are never shared with the ad service. Banner ads appear on the Create screen and in the Save and share window, never while you edit. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to take one sticker or pack out of the app.

Where the law requires it, you can change your consent in the app under Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime), Remove ads and the small export pack are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have successfully saved or shared a few stickers, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Peelwise has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Your content

You decide which photos and words go into your stickers. Peelwise does not check them, and you are responsible for having the right to use them, including the likeness of other people.

Not affiliated with WhatsApp

Peelwise is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp, Meta, Telegram or Messenger. Those names belong to their owners and are used only to say which apps the stickers can be added to or shared with.

Children

Peelwise is made for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
