Decibel Meter: Auralyn: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Auralyn (Decibel Meter: Auralyn) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. It listens through your microphone only while you measure, turns sound into a level on your phone, and never records, stores or sends audio. The levels you save stay on your phone. We don't run a server that receives your measurements, and we don't sell personal information.

What Auralyn does with the microphone

When you tap Start measuring or Record session, Auralyn reads the microphone in short blocks of a fraction of a second, works out the sound level and the frequency bands in memory, and discards the block straight away. No recording, no voice or speech analysis, no audio file and no audio upload exists at any point. The microphone is used only while the meter is running on screen or while a recording session you started is running. Android shows its microphone indicator while the microphone is in use.

What Auralyn stores on your phone

  • Saved sessions: for each session you record, the start time, length, the name, place and note you type, the highest, lowest and average levels, one level for every second, and a short average spectrum. These are numbers only. They are kept in a database in the app's private storage.
  • Settings: weighting, response, calibration correction, alert limit, hearing standard, theme and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.
  • A recovery copy: while a long session records, a small file of the levels so far is saved every half minute, so a session survives if the app is closed by the system. It is deleted when the session ends.

Auralyn does not read your contacts, messages, photos, files or location. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Delete all my data" in Settings removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: sharing a spreadsheet (CSV) or a PDF report of a session, and sharing the app. These go to the app you pick in Android's share sheet, and that app handles them under its own privacy policy. A report contains the numbers of the session and the name, place and note you typed, so share it only with people you trust.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to measure sound. Android asks the first time you start measuring, after Auralyn explains why.
  • Foreground service (microphone): keeps a recording session you started running with the screen off. A notification with a Stop button is shown for as long as it runs.
  • Notifications: for the recording notification, a loud-sound alert while the app is in the background, and a note when a session is saved. You can decline.
  • Vibrate: for the loud-sound alert, which you can turn off in Settings.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Auralyn asks for no location, camera, contacts or storage permission.

Advertising

Auralyn is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your measurements, names and notes are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear only on the Sessions and Guide screens, never over the live meter, in a report or in a form. A full-screen ad may appear on those screens after you finish an export or save a session, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium tool for a day or to add room for more saved sessions.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have saved your second session or shared your first export, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while the meter is on screen.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Auralyn has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Accuracy and advice

Auralyn measures with the microphone in your phone, which is not a certified sound level meter. Readings are estimates and are not legal, medical or workplace-compliance advice.

Children

Auralyn is a general-audience tool. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
