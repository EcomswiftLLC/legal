Vyvora support

Stuck on a setting or found a bug in Vyvora? Tell us what happened and we will help you fix it.

2business days or less until we reply. Business days are Monday to Friday, except US public holidays.

Check the documentation

The Vyvora documentation covers every setting, section and block, using the labels you see in the theme editor. It also has a troubleshooting list for navigation, products, the cart and collections.

What to send us

With these details we can usually answer in one reply.

Store URL
For example https://your-store.myshopify.com.
Example page
The full address of a page where the problem shows, such as one product page.
Theme version
Listed in the theme editor under Theme settings.
Browser and device
For example Chrome on Windows, or Safari on an iPhone.
Screenshots
A screenshot or a short screen recording. Attach them to the form.

Never send passwords, payment details or customer data.
