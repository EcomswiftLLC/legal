Reply time

We reply to every support request within 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, except public holidays in the United States. Requests sent on a weekend or holiday count from the next business day.

A reply means we have answered your question, or told you what we need to answer it and what happens next. Some bug fixes take longer than a reply, and we say how long when that happens.

Reach us through the Vyvora support page or by email at support@ecomswiftllc.com.

Support is free

Support is free for everyone who bought Vyvora. It is never bundled into the theme price and is never sold as an add-on to the theme.

What we help with

  • Installation help: adding Vyvora to your theme library, previewing it, publishing it and updating to a new version.
  • Bugs: anything in Vyvora that does not work as the documentation describes, such as a broken layout, a control that does not respond or a page that shows an error.
  • Questions about theme settings: what a setting, section or block does and how to get the result you want with it.

When we confirm a bug, we fix it in Vyvora and release the fix as a theme update. Bugs that stop shoppers from buying come first.

What support does not include

  • Custom development: new features, design changes and edits to the theme's code, or restoring your own code changes after an update.
  • Third-party apps: installing, setting up or fixing apps. If an app causes a problem, its developer can help.
  • Store content: adding products and collections, writing text, preparing images or moving content from another theme or platform.

We can quote for custom work separately if you ask. Accepting or declining a quote never changes the support you get as a theme buyer.

Duplicate before you edit code. Changes to theme files are not covered and can make updates harder. Work on a copy of the theme.

What to include in a request

Store URL
For example https://your-store.myshopify.com.
Example page
The full address of a page where the problem shows.
Theme version
Listed in the theme editor under Theme settings.
Browser and device
For example Firefox on a Mac.
What happened
What you expected, what you saw, and the steps that lead to it.
Screenshots
A screenshot or short screen recording, if you have one.

Store access and your data

  • Never send passwords, payment details or customer data in a message.
  • If we need to see inside your store, we ask you to approve a collaborator or staff request that you control. Remove it once the issue is solved.
  • We use the details you send only to answer your request.

Documentation

The Vyvora documentation explains every setting by its label in the theme editor and lists fixes for common problems.
