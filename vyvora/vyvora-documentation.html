About Vyvora

Vyvora is a Shopify theme for streetwear, activewear and other apparel stores that sell in drops. Pages look like race-day signage: bold color fields, a heavy grotesk for headings, tall condensed numerals for prices and sizes, and statuses such as Live, Next and Gone.

This guide explains how to install Vyvora, how to set it up in the theme editor and what each setting does. Labels in bold are the exact labels you see in the editor, so you can search this page for a label to find its description.

What comes with the theme:

  • Three navigation pieces: the Rail on the left edge of large screens, the full-screen Index menu, and the Dock at the bottom of phone screens.
  • Five color schemes (Graphite, Bone, Volt, Cobalt and Tangerine). Every section picks its own, so a page reads as stacked color fields.
  • Fourteen sections on the default home page and eight more you can add, from the Hero frame to the Drop board and the Lookbook.
  • Product pages with three gallery layouts, color chips, size tiles, a size guide, tech sheet panels, the Build the fit bundle builder, trust badges, recommendations and recently viewed products.
  • Collection pages with filtering, sorting, five grid layouts and story inserts; a cart drawer or cart page; predictive search; and pages for blogs, contact, 404, password, gift cards and customer accounts.

Vyvora is an Online Store 2.0 theme. Every template is built from sections, and apps can add blocks to the product page and the Featured product section.

The starter content is an example

The text and images that come with the theme show how sections work. Replace them with your own before you publish. The launch checklist lists the places to check.

Install and update

Install Vyvora

  1. Open Vyvora in the Shopify Theme Store and add it to your store's theme library.

  2. In Shopify admin, go to Online Store, then Themes. Vyvora appears in your theme library, below the live theme.

  3. Back up your live theme first: open its actions menu and choose Duplicate.

  4. Select Customize on Vyvora to open the theme editor. While Vyvora is unpublished, your changes do not affect the live store.

  5. To check the theme on a phone or share it with a colleague, open the actions menu on Vyvora and choose Preview.

  6. When you are ready, select Publish. Vyvora replaces your live theme right away.

Update to a new version

When a new version of Vyvora is released, Shopify shows an update notice on the theme in your theme library. Updating adds a fresh copy of the new version to your library and carries over your settings and content from the theme editor. Your current theme stays untouched until you publish the updated copy.

  • Changes you made in the theme editor carry over.
  • Changes you made to the theme's code files do not carry over. Note them before you update so you can add them again.
  • Preview the updated copy before you publish it.

The theme name and version are listed in the theme editor under Theme settings. The changelog lists what changed in each version.

Theme editor basics

Use the page selector at the top of the editor to switch between templates such as Home page, Products and Collections. The left sidebar lists the sections on the page. Select a section to edit its settings, drag it to reorder it, use the eye icon to hide it, or add a new section with Add section.

The header area holds three sections: Announcement ticker, Header and Cart drawer. The footer area holds the Footer. They show on every page.

Settings most sections share

Color scheme
Which of your color schemes the section uses. See Colors and schemes.
Top padding and Bottom padding
Space above and below the section: None Compact Standard Roomy. Page templates such as the product, cart and blog pages call the same options Top spacing and Bottom spacing.

Text that is not in a section

Button labels, messages and other fixed text, such as "Add to bag" or "Bag's empty.", live in the theme's language file. To change them, go to Online Store, then Themes, open the actions menu on Vyvora and choose Edit default theme content.

Launch checklist

  1. Upload your logo and favicon in Theme settings, Logo and favicon.

  2. Check the five color schemes against your brand and keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher.

  3. Choose the Menu in the Header section, and add Menu image blocks for links that need their own picture.

  4. Replace the starter text and images on the home page, including the review notes in the Review wall and the Source note under them.

  5. Pick collections in every section that shows products: Category index, Season stack, Product bento grid, Ranked list and Product reel.

  6. Set Free shipping message threshold to match a real free shipping rate, or leave it at 0 to hide the message.

  7. Add your social media links and set up the footer menus.

  8. Write the text for the Shipping and returns block on the product page so it matches your policies.

  9. Place a test order with a test payment (opens in a new tab), then publish.

Theme settings

Open Theme settings from the gear icon in the editor's left bar. These settings apply to the whole store.

Colors and schemes

Vyvora uses color schemes. A scheme is a set of colors that work together: background, text, buttons and borders. Each section picks one scheme, so you change a color in one place and every section that uses that scheme follows.

Page color scheme
The scheme for the page background and for areas outside sections. Default: Bone.
Schemes
Edit a scheme to change its colors, or add a new one. Each scheme has Background, Background gradient, Text, Secondary text, Border, Accent, Button, Button label, Outline button label, Input background and Overlay.

The theme ships with these five schemes:

  • GraphiteButton

    Background #15161A, text #F1EFE8, secondary text #A9ABB3, Volt buttons with graphite labels. Default for the Rail, Dock and footer.

  • BoneButton

    Background #F1EFE8, text #15161A, secondary text #55575E, graphite buttons. Default page scheme.

  • VoltButton

    Background #C6F432, text #15161A at full strength, graphite buttons with volt labels. Default for the Hero frame and the Index menu.

  • CobaltButton

    Background #2342F5, text #FFFFFF, secondary text #E1E6FF, Volt buttons with graphite labels. Default for the Newsletter.

  • TangerineButton

    Background #FF6B1F, text #15161A, graphite buttons with bone labels. Default for the Tape band and Promo sticker.

Keep text dark on Tangerine

White text on #FF6B1F has a contrast of about 2.85:1, which fails accessibility guidelines. Graphite text (#15161A) on Tangerine passes at about 6.35:1. The editor shows the reminder "Keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher." under Colors.

Motion

Motion in Vyvora is fast and short: headlines rise word by word, cards arrive in a stagger, drawers slide in, and buttons lean toward the pointer. These settings control how much of it shoppers see.

Motion intensity

Off turns off reveals, kinetic headlines, marquees, scroll drift and transition animations. Content shows in place right away.

Subtle keeps all motion but makes it shorter and quicker.

Standard is the default timing.

Expressive uses longer reveals and slower drawer and panel arrivals, for a more dramatic feel.

Animate headlines
Headings rise into view one word at a time.
Enable magnetic buttons
Primary buttons, such as Add to bag, follow a mouse or trackpad pointer slightly. Not used on touch screens.
Show cursor labels on desktop
A small label such as "View", "Drag" or "Play" follows the pointer over images, reels and videos. The normal cursor stays visible.
Enable page transitions
Pages cross-fade when shoppers move between them, in browsers that support view transitions. Turned off when Motion intensity is Off.
Marquee loop time
How long the Announcement ticker and Tape band take to scroll once, from 10 to 90 seconds. A higher number is slower. Default: 40s.
Show reduce motion button in footer
Adds a Reduce motion button to the footer. A shopper's choice is remembered on their device. If the browser blocks storage, the button still works for the current page.

Reduced motion always wins

When a shopper's device is set to reduce motion, Vyvora shows the reduced version whatever you choose here: no reveals, no marquee scrolling, no scroll drift, no magnetic buttons and no cursor labels. The ticker shows its messages as static text, tilted stickers and tape sit straight, the Season stack panels stack normally, and the Lookbook becomes a swipe track with arrow buttons.

Cart

Show cart note
Adds an Add a note field to the cart drawer, for gift messages or delivery details.
Free shipping message threshold
Enter the cart value that matches a free shipping rate set up in Shopify admin, in your store's default currency. The free shipping message is text only. Set up the actual free shipping rate in Shopify admin under Settings, Shipping and delivery, and keep the threshold here at the same value.

Country and language

Lets shoppers pick a language. It appears only when your store has more than one published language.

How navigation works

Vyvora replaces the usual top header with three pieces that share one menu: the Rail on large screens, the Index menu that opens from it, and the Dock on phones.

All three are set in the Header section. Large screens are 1024 pixels wide and up; below that, shoppers see the Dock.

The Rail and quick links

The Rail is a narrow bar fixed to the left edge of the screen. From top to bottom it holds the Menu button, your logo, the name of the current page, and buttons for search, account and the bag with a live item count. Page content sits beside the Rail, never under it.

Rail and dock color scheme
The scheme for the Rail, the Dock and the phone logo strip. Default: Graphite.
Show current page name on the rail
Shows the name of the page the shopper is on, below the logo. Not shown on the home page.
Show quick links on desktop
Shows the first four main menu links at the top right. They hide while scrolling down and return when scrolling up.

The Index menu

The Menu button opens the Index: a full-screen menu with your top-level links set large. On large screens, hovering over or focusing a link shows a preview picture and that link's sub-links beside it; third-level links appear as plain text below them. On phones, each link with sub-links has a button that shows them. The Index works with the keyboard: Tab moves through links, Escape closes it, and focus returns to the Menu button.

Menu
The menu shown in the Index, the quick links and the Dock. Default: Main menu. Set it up in Shopify admin under Content, Menus. Up to three levels of links are shown.
Menu overlay color scheme
The scheme for the Index. Default: Volt.
Show collection images in the menu
Links to a collection or a product preview its featured image. Add a Menu image block to set your own.

Main menu link name

The Dock on phones

On screens under 1024 pixels wide, a bar fixed to the bottom of the screen gives shoppers five slots with visible labels: Menu, Search, a shortcut you choose, Account and Bag. The Account slot shows only when customer accounts are turned on. Your logo sits in a strip at the top of the page that scrolls away.

On product pages, once the main Add to bag button scrolls out of view, the Dock shows an Add to bag bar with the price. If no size is chosen yet, it opens a size sheet first.

Swatches and size tiles

The Variant picker chooses how to show each option from its name and setup in Shopify:

  • Color chips: options with swatches show as color chips. Set up swatches in Shopify admin under each product option.
  • Size tiles: options named Size show as tiles in the numerals font.
  • Sold-out values are crossed out but stay selectable, so shoppers can still see their details. A note explains what the crossing out means.
  • When a value's stock is at or under the Low stock label number, it reads "only 3 left" (with the real number) to screen readers and shows a low stock marker.

Without JavaScript, the picker falls back to links that load each variant.

Apps and app blocks

Apps that support Online Store 2.0 add their features to Vyvora as blocks or embeds, so you do not need to edit theme code.

  1. Install the app from the Shopify App Store and follow its setup steps.

  2. In the theme editor, open a product template and select Product information.

  3. Select Add block. App blocks are listed under Apps. Choose one, then drag it to where it should sit in the details panel.

  4. For apps that work across the whole store, such as chat widgets, turn them on under App embeds in the editor's left bar.

App blocks can be added to Product information and Featured product. For example, a bundle app such as Sleek Bundles can add its block to the product page; review, wishlist and size-recommendation apps work the same way. For apps that give you a code snippet instead, use a Custom Liquid section or block.

Apps are made and supported by their developers. If an app's block looks wrong or breaks the page, contact the app developer first.

Accessibility

Vyvora is built so that shoppers can use it with a keyboard, a screen reader or reduced motion. What is in the theme:

  • A Skip to content link is the first thing keyboard users reach.
  • Menus, search, the cart drawer, quick view, size guide, zoom and filters open as dialogs: focus moves into them, Escape closes them, and focus returns to the button that opened them.
  • Every focusable control shows a visible focus outline.
  • Everything that moves on its own has a way to stop: the ticker, the Tape band and background videos have pause buttons, and the footer can show a Reduce motion button.
  • Device settings for reduced motion are always respected. See Motion.
  • No content depends on hover alone. Previews that appear on hover also appear on keyboard focus, and every preview row is a normal link.
  • Prices include hidden labels such as "Sale price" and "Regular price" for screen readers. Add to bag results, live search results and filter results are announced.
  • Sold-out size tiles stay focusable and are described, instead of being hidden.
  • The five default schemes meet WCAG AA contrast for text.

What you control:

  • Write alt text for product and section images in Shopify admin, or in the Alt text field where a section offers one.
  • Keep text contrast at 4.5:1 or higher when you edit schemes. Never put white text on Tangerine.
  • Use one Heading 1 per page. The Hero frame's Heading level setting helps with this.
  • Give videos a Video description, and avoid flashing footage.
  • Write link and button labels that make sense on their own, such as "Shop night run" instead of "Click here".

Performance tips

Vyvora loads each section's styles and scripts only when that section is on the page, uses no animation library, and lazy-loads images below the first screen. To keep your store fast:

  • Upload images at the recommended sizes listed in each setting. Shopify serves smaller versions to smaller screens automatically.
  • Prefer a poster image and a Play button over background video. Use Play a muted loop behind the poster only where the film matters.
  • Keep the home page focused. Each extra section adds images to load.
  • Review your apps. Remove apps you no longer use, and check that their code was removed too.
  • Limit Custom Liquid sections that load scripts from other sites.
  • After big changes, check the web performance report in Shopify admin under Analytics.

FAQ

Can I change the fonts?

Yes. Pick any font from Shopify's library under Typography in theme settings. Uploading your own font file is not supported.

Does Vyvora show countdown timers or invented stock numbers?

No. The Drop board shows date text you write, with no countdown. Low stock labels show the real number left, and only when Shopify tracks inventory. A Sale badge shows only when the compare-at price is higher than the price.

Does Build the fit give a discount?

No. It adds products at their own prices. To offer a bundle discount, create an automatic discount in Shopify admin. The Discount note only describes it.

Can I turn off all the motion?

Yes. Set Motion intensity to Off. Shoppers who ask their device to reduce motion always get the reduced version.

Why is there no dropdown menu?

Vyvora uses the Index, a full-screen menu that works the same way with a mouse, a keyboard and touch. It shows up to three levels of links.

Can I show my Instagram feed?

The Image mosaic shows images you upload. Vyvora does not connect to social media accounts.

Are the reviews on the Review wall real?

Only if you add real ones. The starter notes are samples for layout. The section shows what you type and does not create ratings or review markup.

How do I change "Add to bag" to "Add to cart"?

Use Edit default theme content from the actions menu on Vyvora in your theme library, and change the text for the button.

Can I use Vyvora on more than one store?

A Theme Store purchase covers one store. Check the license terms on the Vyvora listing in the Shopify Theme Store.

Where do I find the theme version?

In the theme editor, open Theme settings. The theme name and version are listed there. Include the version when you write to support.
