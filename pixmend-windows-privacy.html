Pixmend Privacy Policy

Pixmend (AI Image Upscaler & Photo Enhancer: Pixmend) for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Effective 2026-11-23.

Summary

Pixmend upscales and cleans up photos on your PC. Your photos never leave the computer: Pixmend does not upload, analyse or store them anywhere else. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your PC

  • Your settings, the list of recent photos (file path and a small thumbnail) and the settings and crop you chose for each photo, in the app's data folder.
  • Pictures you paste, and batch results waiting to be exported, in the same folder.
  • A record of how fast the AI ran on your PC (milliseconds per megapixel), used only to show honest time estimates. It never leaves the PC.
  • Files you export are saved only where you choose. Camera details (EXIF, which can include a location) are removed from exports unless you switch on "Keep camera details". Exported files carry a short note in their file details that the picture was AI-enhanced with Pixmend.

You can clear the recent photos and their saved settings in the app at any time, and remove everything by uninstalling Pixmend.

How the AI works

The upscaling runs on your PC's processor with open source Real-ESRGAN models that are installed with the app. The photo is processed in memory on your computer. No image or result is sent over the network.

Network use

  • Microsoft Store: to check your purchases and export balance, show prices, process purchases and show the rating dialog, the app uses Microsoft Store services. Payments are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement; Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your card details.
  • Links you click (support email, this policy, the terms) open in your browser or mail app.
  • Nothing else: Pixmend sends no photos, files or usage data to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

Children

Pixmend collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
