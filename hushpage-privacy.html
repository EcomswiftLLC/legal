Veilpage: Diary with Lock: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Veilpage (Diary with Lock) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. The pages you write are stored only on your phone, encrypted, and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your writing, and we don't sell personal information.

What Veilpage stores on your phone

  • Your pages: the titles, text, dates, moods, tags and photos you add. Each page and each photo is saved as an encrypted file in the app's private storage.
  • Your lock: the encryption key for your diary is protected by your PIN. The PIN itself is not stored; a wrong PIN simply fails to open the key. If you turn on fingerprint or face unlock, a second copy of the key is protected by a key held in Android's secure hardware. Without a PIN, the key is protected by a key in Android's Keystore.
  • Settings: theme, paper colour, font, auto-lock time, reminder time and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

Veilpage does not read your contacts, messages, location, microphone or camera. Photos are only added through Android's photo picker, and only the ones you pick. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and the erase option in Settings removes it at any time.

Important limits

If you forget your PIN, we cannot recover your pages, because we never have your key. Keep a backup file and remember your PIN. Encryption protects the files on your phone. It does not protect against a phone that is unlocked and in someone else's hands, a rooted phone or malware.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: saving a backup file, restoring one, sharing a page, and sharing the app. A backup file is encrypted with a password you choose, and it is saved to the place you pick in Android's file picker. A page you share goes to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. That place or app then handles the file or text under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the optional daily reminder. The reminder text is neutral and never shows what you wrote. Android asks when you turn the reminder on, and you can decline. Reminders may arrive a few minutes early or late.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Veilpage plans your reminder again.
  • Fingerprint or face (biometric): used only to unlock the diary on your phone. Veilpage never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Veilpage asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage permission, and it does not use exact alarms.

Advertising

Veilpage is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your pages, titles, moods and tags are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear only on the main list screens, never on the lock screen or while you write. A full-screen ad may appear on the Journal list after you save a page, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature for a day or two.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Screen privacy

By default Veilpage hides its preview in the recent apps list and blocks screenshots. You can turn this off in Settings.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have written a few pages, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while you are writing.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Veilpage has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Not a health service

Veilpage is a private writing tool. It is not a medical device or a therapy service and gives no medical advice. If you are in crisis or worried about your health, please contact a professional or your local emergency number.

Children

Veilpage is made for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
