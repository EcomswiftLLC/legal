Privacy Policy - Reviewly: Photo, UGC Reviews

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Reviewly: Photo, UGC Reviews is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that lets a Wix Stores merchant collect, moderate and display product reviews with photos and videos, and optionally e-mail buyers one request to review what they bought. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs the app (the "merchant") decides how reviews and review requests are used and is the controller of their customers' data. Ecom Swift LLC acts as the merchant's processor and only handles that data to run the app. All of it stays inside the merchant's own Wix site.

1. What the app processes

The app does not read payment details or passwords, does not track visitors across sites and sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

2. Where data is stored

  • Reviews are stored in a private data collection that belongs to the app on the merchant's own Wix site (Wix Data, which needs Wix CMS on the site). Only the site's owner and collaborators and the app can read it. Visitors see only published reviews, and only the reviewer's name, rating, title, text, photos or videos, date and the merchant's reply. A reviewer's e-mail address is never shown publicly.
  • Photos and videos are uploaded straight to the merchant's Wix Media Manager. Uploaded files have public web addresses, so anyone with the exact link can open them.
  • Review requests (settings, request log and opt-out list) are kept in a second private data collection of the app on the same site.

Ecom Swift LLC has no database of its own for this data and does not copy it anywhere else.

3. Review-request e-mails

When an order is approved and the merchant has review requests switched on, the app reads that order, checks that it has a buyer e-mail address and live product pages, and records the order in the request log. It then sends one transactional e-mail to the buyer of that order, once the delay the merchant chose has passed, using the Wix Email Transmissions service. The e-mail comes from Wix's shared sender address with the store's name as the sender name, contains the buyer's first name, up to three purchased products and a "Write a review" link to the product page, and uses the merchant's own wording. The link carries no personal data. Canceled and refunded orders are skipped. Sending uses the merchant's Wix e-mail quota. The buyer's e-mail address is read from the order again when the message is sent and is not copied into the request log.

Every e-mail says why it was sent and how to opt out. A buyer who opts out is added to the opt-out list as a one-way hash of their address, and no further review requests are sent to that address. Merchants can switch the feature off at any time, which cancels requests that are still waiting. Merchants remain responsible for having a lawful basis to contact their customers under the rules that apply to them.

4. Other service providers

  • Wix hosts the app, stores its data (Wix Data and the Media Manager), provides orders and the product catalog, delivers the e-mails (Wix Email Transmissions) and identifies the site owner.

The app has no servers of its own and uses no AI model provider, analytics service, advertising network or third-party e-mail provider. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Reviews, uploaded media and the request log are kept until the merchant deletes them. Merchants can delete reviews one by one or in bulk from the app, including a reviewer's review when that reviewer asks.
  • The opt-out list is kept so that opt-outs continue to be honoured, and it holds hashes only.
  • The app does not delete anything automatically when it is removed from a site. The collections the app created stay in the site's data, and uploaded files stay in its Media Manager, until the merchant deletes them. To have this done, or for help with any deletion request, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com.

6. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stays in the merchant's own Wix site. The app's collections can be read and changed only by the site's owner and collaborators and by the app. Moderation and settings are limited to administrators, public methods return only published reviews without e-mail addresses, and uploads accept only photos and videos within size limits (25 MB for a photo, 200 MB for a video). No secrets are stored in the app's code.

7. Browser storage

The reviews widget on a product page writes one small value to the visitor's session storage (a timestamp) so that it checks for waiting review requests only about every ten minutes. It contains no personal information and ends with the browser session. The widget can also add search-engine structured data (product rating and review author names and text) to the page so that reviews can appear in search results. The merchant's dashboard keeps a few small preferences in the browser on the Wix dashboard (for example whether the "rate this app" prompt was answered, whether a help panel is collapsed, and the address last used for a test e-mail). The dashboard also has links to the Wix App Market, the Wix Experts Marketplace, a WhatsApp chat with Ecom Swift LLC and our support e-mail address, which send information to those services only if clicked.

8. Your rights

Reviewers and buyers can ask the site owner to access, correct or delete the data they gave or that was collected about them, or to stop review requests. Merchants can edit and delete reviews in the app and can ask us to help with any request. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data and to object to its use. We respond to requests relating to data we process within 30 days. Wix may process data in several countries under its own safeguards. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

9. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

10. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify merchants in the app.
