Linelens: AI Content Detector, Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Linelens (AI Content Detector: Linelens) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You do not need an account. The quick check runs on your phone. The full check sends what you submit to our server and to an AI provider so a model can write its reasoning. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • The quick check: the on-device signals for text are computed on your phone. Nothing is sent.
  • Picture details: camera data, editing software and Content Credentials are read from the file on your phone.
  • History: your saved checks, notes and small thumbnails are stored only on your phone. You can switch history off or clear it in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Settings and counters: your theme, and counters that pace the Premium offer and the rating request, stay on your phone.

What is sent when you run a full check

  • Text: the text you submit is sent to our server and to an AI provider, which generates the answer.
  • Pictures: a smaller copy of the picture is sent, re-encoded so it carries no metadata and no location.
  • Retention: our server does not keep what you send. It keeps only anonymous usage counters and a random install identifier so that free daily credits can be counted. The AI provider processes the request to answer it under its own terms.
  • No account, no sale: there is no sign-in, and we do not sell your data or use what you submit to build advertising profiles.

You are shown a plain-language notice before the first full check, and you can decline it. Please do not enter passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Reports

Every result has a Report action. If you send a report, we keep the reported result, the reason and your note. If you leave the box ticked, we also keep the text you checked, so that we can review it and make results safer. Pictures are not included in reports.

Files you open

Linelens asks for no storage, camera, contacts, location or microphone permission. It opens only the file or picture you choose with Android's own picker, or the text or picture you share into it from another app.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one. AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information to show and measure ads. Banners appear on the History and Settings screens. A full-screen ad may appear between results, with limits on how often. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one for extra free credits. Premium removes ads. You can change your consent in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app learns only which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play.

Ratings

After you have used the app a few times, it may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Results can be wrong

Linelens gives an estimate with reasons. It is not proof, and it must not be the only evidence that a person used AI. Detectors of any kind make mistakes, especially on short, edited, formal or non-native writing.

Children

Linelens is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
