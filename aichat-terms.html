Free credits, Premium and ads

  • A small number of requests are free each day and reset at midnight UTC. You may choose to watch a video for an extra free credit, up to a daily limit. A request that fails is not charged.
  • Premium is a paid subscription sold through Google Play. It raises the daily limit on messages (fair use applies), uses a stronger model, allows longer messages and a longer memory in a chat, adds voice conversation, saved chats and folders without the free limits, and export, and removes ads. Prices and billing period are shown in the app before you subscribe, as given by Google Play. A one-time Remove ads purchase is also available.
  • Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period until you cancel them in Google Play, in the Subscriptions section of your account. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies.
  • The free version keeps your latest 30 chats on your phone (pinned chats are kept). Premium keeps all of them.
