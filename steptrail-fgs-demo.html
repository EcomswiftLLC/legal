Steptrail: Health and Location Foreground Services

Ecom Swift LLC · demonstration for the Google Play foreground-service declaration

What the video shows

  1. In Settings the user turns on Keep counting. The app starts the step counting foreground service (type health) and a quiet notification with a Pause action appears in the shade.
  2. On the Walks tab the user turns on Record my route and taps Start walk. The walk starts only because the user tapped it. The screen shows live time, steps, distance, pace and the route drawn from GPS.
  3. The user presses Home. The walk keeps running in the background. The notification shade shows the Walk in progress notification with elapsed time, steps, distance and a Pause action, and the location indicator is visible in the status bar.
  4. The user returns through the notification, sees the route continuing, and holds the stop button. The walk is saved, the service stops and its notification disappears.

Why foreground services are required

Steptrail is a step counter and walk tracker. Counting steps all day and recording a walk with the phone in a pocket or with the screen off must continue when the user is not looking at the app, so the work runs in two foreground services. CountingService uses type health (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH): it keeps the step sensor registered so steps are counted on phones that stop background apps, and it exists only while the user has switched on Keep counting. WalkService uses types health and location (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION): it records the steps, time and, only if the user turned on Record my route, the GPS route of a walk the user started. Location is used only in the foreground service while a walk is running, never in the background otherwise. Both services always show a notification with a Pause action, stop when the user stops the walk or turns the setting off, and all data stays on the device.

How to reproduce in the app

  1. Open Steptrail, go to Settings and switch on Keep counting. Pull down the notification shade to see the counting notification.
  2. Open the Walks tab, switch on Record my route, choose Walk and tap Start walk (allow location when asked).
  3. Press Home and open the notification shade to see the walk notification, then return to the app and hold the stop button to finish.
