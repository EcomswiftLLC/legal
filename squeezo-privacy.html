Shrinkwise Video and Photo Compressor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Shrinkwise is a video compressor and photo compressor made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your videos and photos are compressed on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your files, we don't build profiles of people, and we don't sell personal information.

Your videos and photos

  • You choose what Shrinkwise sees: the app opens only the videos and photos you pick with Android's photo picker, or that you share to it or open with it from another app. It does not browse your gallery.
  • Everything happens on your device: video compression, trimming, rotation, frame rate changes, photo compression, resizing and format conversion all run on your phone, using its own video encoder and image codecs (AndroidX Media3). Nothing is sent anywhere to be processed.
  • Photo details (metadata): by default Shrinkwise removes the location (GPS) from the photos it saves and keeps the date and camera details. You can change both in the app. Compressed videos are written as new files and do not carry over location data.
  • What gets saved on your phone: compressed videos go to Movies/Shrinkwise and photos to Pictures/Shrinkwise. The app keeps a History list (file names, sizes, dates, settings and the address of each result) and your settings in its private storage. Working files are kept in the app's cache only while a job runs and are removed when it ends. Uninstalling the app removes the History and settings; your saved results stay in your gallery.
  • Your originals: Shrinkwise never changes or deletes your original files on its own. If you choose to delete originals after compressing, Android shows its own confirmation and nothing is deleted unless you approve it there.

Permissions

Shrinkwise does not ask for the camera, microphone, location or contacts permission, and it does not need storage permission on Android 10 and newer. It asks for notification permission (Android 13 and newer) only when you start your first job, so it can show progress and tell you when files are ready; you can say no and the app still works. A foreground service keeps a running job going while you use other apps. On Android 9 and older the app asks for permission to save to your gallery. The internet is used only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Shrinkwise is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or have removed ads. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home, history and result screens, never while you set up or run a job. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished job, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video appears only when you choose to watch one, to use a PRO tool once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions, the lifetime purchase and the one-time ad removal are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of what you own, so it can unlock Premium on your devices.

Ratings

After you have compressed a few files, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service, Google Play Billing and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Your videos and photos are never part of it.

Children

Shrinkwise is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
