Mooncot: Media Playback Foreground Service

Ecom Swift LLC · demonstration for the Google Play foreground-service declaration

What the video shows

  1. The user opens Soothing sounds and taps White noise. Playback starts only because the user tapped it, with a sleep timer (30 minutes) already selected.
  2. The user leaves the app and locks the screen. The sound keeps playing with the screen off, which is the purpose of the feature: a parent settling a baby does not keep the phone awake.
  3. The notification shade shows the White noise notification with the time it plays until and a Stop action.
  4. The user taps Stop. Playback and the notification end immediately.

Why a foreground service is required

Mooncot plays white noise, shushing, heartbeat and lullaby sounds that are generated on the phone. They must keep playing while the screen is off and the app is in the background, otherwise the sound would stop as soon as the phone locks. It therefore runs as a foreground service of type mediaPlayback (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK). It is always started by the user tapping a sound, always shows a notification with Stop, and ends when the sleep timer ends or the user stops it. Nothing is downloaded or uploaded.

How to reproduce in the app

  1. Open Mooncot, finish the short setup and tap the Soothing sounds icon on Today.
  2. Tap White noise, press Home or lock the screen.
  3. Open the notification shade, see the playing notification and tap Stop.
