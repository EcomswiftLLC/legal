Ledgerloom: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Ledgerloom (Invoice Maker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. The business details, clients, documents and payments you add are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Ledgerloom stores on your phone

  • Your business profiles: business name, address, phone, email, website, tax or registration number, payment details you type (for example bank details or a payment link), your logo and signature pictures, and your document numbering and look.
  • Your clients: names, company names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses and tax numbers you enter.
  • Your documents: invoices, estimates, receipts, purchase orders and credit notes with their items, taxes, notes, terms, payments and up to three pictures you attach.
  • Saved items and settings: your catalog, formats, theme and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Pictures you choose with Android's photo picker are copied into the app's private storage; Ledgerloom does not get access to the rest of your photos. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Android's phone-to-phone transfer during setup of a new phone may carry the data over. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Erase all my data" in Settings removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: saving a PDF, a spreadsheet (CSV) or a backup file, sharing or printing a document, or sending a reminder or document by email or another app. The file or message goes to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker, share sheet or print dialog, and that place or app then handles it under its own privacy policy. Ledgerloom itself never sends a document or a message to your clients. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick, on your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.
  • Billing: used for purchases through Google Play.

Ledgerloom asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification or storage permission.

Advertising

Ledgerloom is free to start and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your business details, clients, documents and amounts are never shared with the ad service. Banner ads appear on the Home screen and on the screen where you save or send a document, never while you edit a document. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to take one document out of the app.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime), Remove ads and the small export pack are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have successfully saved or shared a few documents, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Ledgerloom has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Accuracy and advice

Ledgerloom helps you make business documents from what you type in. Totals, taxes and due dates are worked out from your entries. Ledgerloom layouts are not legal or tax advice, and you are responsible for what your documents say and for the invoicing rules that apply to your business.

Children

Ledgerloom is made for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
