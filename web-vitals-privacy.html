Privacy Policy — Core Web Vitals Snapshot

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Core Web Vitals Snapshot reads the performance metrics your browser already measured for the current page load and rates them. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the page's own Performance API data (LCP, CLS, INP/FID, FCP, TTFB, resource weight, DOM size) from the active tab, only when you ask.
  • Rating, history, comparison and CSV export run locally in your browser. No page content or metric is sent to us or any third party.
  • Saved snapshots and settings are stored locally; CSV export is produced on your device.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — to read the current page's performance metrics when you open the popup.
  • storage — to save your settings and (Pro) snapshot history locally.
  • alarms — only for the optional reminder below.
  • host access (ecomswiftllc.com) — only to verify a Pro purchase (license check). No other hosts.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you measure, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
