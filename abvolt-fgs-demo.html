Abvolt: Workout Foreground Service

Ecom Swift LLC · demonstration for the Google Play foreground-service declaration

What the video shows

  1. On the Today screen the user taps Start day 1, then Start now after the short countdown. The workout begins only because the user tapped it.
  2. The workout player shows the animated exercise, the rep target and the Done button. The app now runs the workout foreground service (type health).
  3. The screen is turned off and on again. The workout keeps running, and the notification shade shows the Abvolt Abs · Day 1: Upper abs notification with the current exercise, a progress bar and Pause and Done actions.
  4. The user returns to the app and ends the workout. The service stops and its notification disappears.

Why a foreground service is required

A guided workout is a timed sequence of exercises with spoken cues. It has to keep running when the user locks the phone or switches app, otherwise the timer stops and the coach goes silent in the middle of an exercise, and it cannot be paused and resumed by the system without breaking the session. The work runs in WorkoutService, a foreground service of type health (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH). It is started only when the user taps Start on a workout day, always shows a notification with Pause and Done, and stops when the workout ends or the user ends it. The service reads no sensors and sends no data anywhere; everything stays on the device.

How to reproduce in the app

  1. Open Abvolt and tap Start day 1 on the Today screen, then Start now.
  2. Lock the screen or press Home, then pull down the notification shade to see the workout notification.
  3. Return to the app and tap the close button, then End workout, to stop the service.
