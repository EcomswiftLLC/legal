Tapewave Voice Recorder & Audio Editor: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Tapewave (Voice Recorder & Audio Editor) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your recordings and audio files are recorded, edited and stored on your phone and never leave it unless you share them. We don't run a server that receives your audio, and we don't sell personal information.

Your recordings and audio files

  • Everything happens on your device: recording, the waveform, the audio editor (trim, cut, split, merge, volume, normalise, fade, speed and reverse), the ringtone maker, video to audio and the audio converter all run locally on your phone. Tapewave uses no speech recognition, no transcription and no artificial intelligence model.
  • Where files are kept: recordings and edited files are stored inside the app's private storage. If the setting "Copy new files to Recordings/Tapewave" is on (it is on by default), a copy of each new file is also saved to the shared Recordings folder (Music folder on Android 11 and older), where other apps on your phone can see it. Ringtones, notification sounds and alarms you make are saved to the system Ringtones, Notifications or Alarms folder.
  • Backups: Android's automatic cloud backup of the app includes only your settings, not your recordings.
  • Trash: files you delete in the app go to Trash and are deleted for good after 30 days, or earlier if you empty Trash. Copies already saved to the shared Recordings folder are not deleted by the app.
  • You choose what Tapewave opens: audio files are opened through Android's file picker, videos through the system Photo Picker, or when you use "Open with" or Share from another app. Tapewave only receives the files you pick. It does not browse your storage and asks for no storage permission on Android 10 and newer.
  • Sharing: if you tap Share, the file is handed to the app you pick, and that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Microphone

Tapewave asks for microphone permission the first time you tap Record. The microphone is used only while a recording you started is running, including in the background with the screen off. During that time Android shows a notification with Pause, Mark and Stop buttons and the system microphone indicator. The app never records on its own, and the sound is stored only in the recording file on your phone. If you choose a Bluetooth headset as the microphone, Android routes the headset's microphone to the app for that recording.

Notifications

On Android 13 and newer, Tapewave asks to show notifications before your first recording, so you can see the timer and control the recording from the notification. Recording works either way.

Ringtones and contacts

To make a tone your phone's default ringtone, notification sound or alarm, Android asks you to allow Tapewave to modify system settings. The app uses this only to set the default sound you chose. To give one contact their own ringtone, Tapewave asks for contacts permission and opens the system contact picker; it reads the contact you pick and writes the ringtone setting to that contact only. Contact information is not stored by the app and is never sent anywhere.

Other permissions

Tapewave asks for no location or phone permission. To pause a recording during calls it relies on Android's audio focus and microphone status, not on your call log or phone state. On Android 9 and older it asks for storage permission only to save copies to your Music folder. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Tapewave is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Banners appear on the Home and Library screens, never on the recorder or the editors. A full-screen ad may appear after a save, limited in number and spacing, and never while you record or edit. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one to use a Premium tool once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have saved a few files, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Tapewave has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Tapewave is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
