Privacy policy

CCWasher: PC Cleaner & Optimizer for Windows (package name CCWasher), by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 2 November 2026.

In short

  • CCWasher works on your PC. Scan results, clean logs, settings and the lists of your apps, startup items and drives stay on your PC. The app never uploads them, or any file, to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • The app itself makes no internet connections. The only online parts are Microsoft Store purchases, licence checks and update checks, which Windows and the Microsoft Store handle, and web pages that open in your browser only when you click a link.

What the app reads on your PC

To do its job, CCWasher reads information on your PC, and only on your PC:

  • Smart Scan reads the names, sizes and dates of files in a fixed list of temporary, cache and log folders (for example your temporary folder, browser and app cache folders, crash dumps and the Recycle Bin). It does not open or read the contents of these files.
  • The Storage Analyzer reads the names, sizes and dates of files in the drive or folder you pick, to show what takes space.
  • The Duplicate Finder reads the contents of files in the folders you pick, to compare them. The comparison happens on your PC; file contents are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • Apps reads the list of installed programs and Microsoft Store apps that Windows keeps, and Windows' record of when programs were last started from Start or File Explorer.
  • Startup reads the startup entries Windows keeps (Run registry keys, the Startup folders and sign-in scheduled tasks).
  • Disk Health reads the drive status Windows reports (health, temperature and wear where Windows allows it, TRIM and optimization settings).

What the app changes, and only when you ask

  • A clean deletes only the cache, temporary and log files you approved in the review. Your own files (duplicates, large files, old files) are moved to the Recycle Bin, never deleted outright.
  • Privacy cleaning (cookies, browsing history lists, recent files list, clipboard, DNS cache) is off by default and runs only when you show and tick those items.
  • Uninstalling an app starts that app's own uninstaller. Switching a startup item off or on changes the same setting Task Manager uses.
  • The automatic weekly clean (Premium) runs only after you switch it on, and only cleans the safe categories listed in the app.

What the app stores, and where

CCWasher saves its settings, the clean history, the file logs of each clean, the free plan's monthly allowance counter and saved disk health readings in its own app data folder on your PC. The location is shown in Settings, under About. A saved licence status is kept there too, so Premium keeps working for a short time if the Microsoft Store cannot be reached. Clear history in the app removes the history and logs. Uninstalling the app removes this folder.

Purchases

Premium is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The Microsoft Store handles payment, renewal, refunds and cancellation under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. The app never sees your payment details. To show your plan, the app asks Windows and the Microsoft Store for the add-on licences of your Microsoft account. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your account details.

Rating requests

After a clean that freed space, the app may ask once in a while whether you would like to rate it. The request is a window inside the app, shown at most once every 48 hours and never while you type. If you choose Rate in Microsoft Store, the Microsoft Store opens its review page and the app stops asking. The app does not see your rating or your review. You can turn the requests off in Settings.

Links

The app opens a web page, the Microsoft Store, a Windows Settings page or your email program only when you choose a link or button. Those pages open outside the app and are covered by their own policies.

Children

CCWasher is a general utility. It does not collect personal information and has no communication features.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
