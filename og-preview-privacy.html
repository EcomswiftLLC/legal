Privacy Policy — Open Graph & Social Preview

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Open Graph & Social Preview reads the current page's social-sharing tags and shows how its link will look on Facebook, X and LinkedIn. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the current page's Open Graph and Twitter-card meta tags (title, description, image URL, type, etc.) from the active tab, only for the tab you are on and only when you open it.
  • The card previews, the checklist, the tag generator and the image check all run locally in your browser. The page content is never sent to us or any third party. The share-image preview is loaded directly by your browser from the image URL in the page, the same way any web page loads an image.
  • Your settings and recent history are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you preview, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
