AI Rewriter: Wordlark: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 November 2026

Wordlark (AI Rewriter: Wordlark) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. It rewrites text you already have: it can paraphrase it, change its tone, shorten or expand it, fix grammar and simplify it. You don't need an account. This page explains, in plain words, what stays on your phone, what is sent to our server and to Cloudflare Workers AI, and what we keep.

The short version

  • The text you ask Wordlark to rewrite is sent to our server and on to Cloudflare Workers AI, so that the rewrite can be made. The app tells you this before your first rewrite and asks you to accept it.
  • Our server keeps no copy of your text and no copy of the result. It keeps only anonymous counters so that the daily limit works.
  • If you report a result, the reported result is kept so we can review it. Nothing else you wrote is kept.
  • Your history, favourites and writing samples stay on your phone. We don't have them.
  • We do not sell your data, and there is no account.

What Wordlark stores on your phone

  • History and favourites: the text you rewrote, the results, the mode you used and the time, in a database in the app's private storage. History keeps up to 200 items, and favourites are kept until you remove them.
  • Writing samples: if you add samples of your own writing so that results can sound more like you, they are stored only on your phone. When that feature is used, a short summary of your style and a few short excerpts are included in the request, as described below.
  • Settings and counters: theme, language, last mode, how many results you copied or exported (used to space out the rating request and ads), and your remaining daily rewrites.
  • An anonymous install ID, a random number made when the app first starts. It is not linked to your name, email, phone or Google account.

You can remove everything on your phone at any time in Settings, under Delete everything on this phone. Wordlark turns off Android backup for its data, so history is not copied to the cloud by Android.

What is sent to our server and to Cloudflare Workers AI

When you tap Rewrite, Wordlark sends the text you chose, the mode and tone you picked and, where it applies, your language choice. If you use your writing style, the style summary and short excerpts are sent too. Everything travels over an encrypted connection to our server, which adds safety rules and passes the request to Cloudflare Workers AI, an AI model service run by Cloudflare, Inc., to produce the rewrite. The result comes back the same way.

Please don't rewrite anything you don't want to share with an AI service, such as passwords, card numbers or health details.

  • Retention: our server does not store the text of requests or results. It stores the anonymous install ID, the number of rewrites used today, so that the daily limit works, and counters used to keep the service running and to stop abuse.
  • Cloudflare Workers AI processes the request to make the rewrite. According to Cloudflare's data usage page for Workers AI, it does not use your text to train its models and does not keep it. Cloudflare's own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing. No other AI provider receives your text.
  • Safety checks: requests and results are checked automatically by rules on your phone and on our server. Requests that break the rules are refused and not charged.

Other ways text reaches Wordlark

  • Rewrite in the selection menu: when you select text in another app and choose Rewrite, that selected text is handed to Wordlark. It is sent to be rewritten only when you tap Rewrite in the Wordlark sheet. If you tap Replace, the result goes back to the app you came from.
  • Share to Wordlark: text you share to Wordlark fills the text box. Nothing is sent until you tap Rewrite.
  • Quick tile and Paste: Wordlark reads the clipboard only when you tap the Rewrite clipboard tile or the Paste button. It never reads the clipboard in the background.

Reporting a result

Every result has a Report button. If you send a report, we receive the reported result, the reason you picked, your optional note and, if you leave the box ticked, the text that produced it. We keep reports so we can improve the safety rules, and we delete them when we no longer need them. You can untick the box before you send.

Ads

Wordlark shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Banners appear only on the Library and Settings screens. A full-screen ad can appear after you finish copying, sharing or exporting a result, and never while a rewrite is being made. Wordlark does not offer rewarded videos. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). Ad requests do not include what you wrote.

You can change your ad consent in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves.

Purchases

Premium (weekly, monthly or yearly) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you copy a result you like, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and Google Play handles the review. The app asks rarely, at least 30 days apart, and never after an error or a limit.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to make rewrites, and for ads and Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described above.
  • Billing: for Google Play purchases.

Wordlark asks for no camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or notification permission. When you share a result, your own sharing menu opens and you decide where it goes. Wordlark never sends anything for you.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Wordlark has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Who Wordlark is for

Wordlark is for adults, aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Rewrites are made by an AI and can change meaning or contain mistakes, so read them before you use them. Wordlark does not give legal, medical, tax or financial advice.

Your choices and rights

Because we keep no profile of you and no copy of your text, there is little to delete on our side. You can delete everything on your phone in Settings. To ask about a report you sent, or any question about your data, write to us and include the approximate date. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data about you, and we will answer such requests within the time the law requires.
