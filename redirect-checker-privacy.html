Privacy Policy — Redirect & Canonical Checker

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Redirect & Canonical Checker shows where the current page's URL ultimately lands, whether its http/https and www/non-www versions resolve consistently, and whether the canonical tag matches. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • The extension reads the current tab's URL and its canonical / meta-refresh tag from the active page, only when you open the popup.
  • To find the final destination, your browser requests the page URL and its standard address variants (http/https, www/non-www) directly and follows their redirects. These requests go to the website being checked — never to us — and only the final URL and status code are shown.
  • Settings and any history are stored locally on your device. CSV exports are saved by you, to your computer.

Permissions

  • Host access is required so your browser can request the URL and its variants and follow their redirects. It is used only for pages you choose to check.
  • activeTab / scripting let the extension read the current URL and its canonical tag.
  • alarms is used only for the optional reminder described below.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours to show what Pro adds. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the URLs you check, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
