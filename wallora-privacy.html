Privacy policy

4K Live Wallpaper Studio: Wallora for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • Wallora makes and sets wallpapers on your PC. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Wallora does not send your pictures, videos, wallpapers or settings anywhere.

What stays on your PC

  • Your library (wallpaper recipes, collections, favorites, picture adjustments, settings) is kept in a local database, with daily local backup copies.
  • Pictures you import are copied into Wallora's library folder. Videos you add are used where they are; Wallora does not copy or change them.
  • Wallpapers you set are saved as image files in your Pictures folder (Pictures, Wallora, Wallpapers) so Windows can display them. If your Pictures folder is synced by a cloud service you use (for example OneDrive), that service may sync these files; Wallora itself does not upload them.
  • The day and night feature uses the city or latitude and longitude you type. Sunrise and sunset are calculated on your PC. No location service is used.

Network use

Wallora makes no network requests of its own. There is no online wallpaper catalog in this version. The only network traffic comes from Microsoft Store when you buy Premium, restore purchases, read add-on prices, or rate the app. Microsoft handles that traffic under the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Purchases

Premium is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive or store your payment details. Wallora stores only whether Premium is active, as reported by Microsoft Store.

Desktop integration

To set wallpapers and show live wallpapers, Wallora uses Windows' own interfaces (the desktop wallpaper interface and the desktop window layer). To pause live wallpapers it checks whether a full-screen app is open, whether a window is maximized, the battery status and whether the screen is locked. To confirm a wallpaper is visible it reads the pixels of the desktop layer once after setting it. None of this information is stored beyond the current check or sent anywhere.

Children

Wallora collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

You can export or restore your library at any time (Settings, Backup) and remove all data by uninstalling Wallora and deleting the Pictures, Wallora folder.
