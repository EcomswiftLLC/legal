Bloomtide Period & Ovulation Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Bloomtide is a period, ovulation and pregnancy tracker made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your health data stays on your phone. We don't run a server that receives your logs, we have no access to them, and we never sell, rent or share them.

Your health data stays on your device

  • What you log: period dates, flow, symptoms, moods, pain, energy, sleep, sexual activity and protection, discharge, ovulation and pregnancy test results, medication and pills taken, water, weight, basal body temperature, notes, pregnancy dates, reminders and your settings (including the birth year you entered at setup).
  • Where it is stored: in a private database inside the app on your phone. Other apps cannot read it.
  • No cloud backup: Bloomtide switches off Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer for its data, so your logs are not copied to Google Drive or another phone without you.
  • Predictions and insights (next period, fertile window, ovulation, phases, charts and the doctor report) are calculated on your phone.
  • Ads never receive health data. Nothing you log is sent with ad requests or used to choose ads.
  • We have no analytics or tracking of our own inside the app.

Files you choose to export

You can create a doctor report (PDF), a spreadsheet of your logs (CSV) or a backup file. These are created on your phone and only leave the app when you share them or save them to a folder you pick. Plain backups and CSV files can be read by anyone who has the file, so keep them somewhere safe. Encrypted backups are protected with AES-256 and a passphrase you choose; we cannot recover a forgotten passphrase. Temporary export files are deleted from the app's cache after about an hour.

App lock

If you turn on the app lock, your PIN is stored only as a salted one-way hash on your phone. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by Android; Bloomtide never receives biometric data. When the lock is on, the app's preview in the Recents screen is hidden.

Notifications and widgets

Reminders are planned and shown by your phone. Notifications are hidden on the lock screen until you unlock your phone, and discreet mode replaces words like "period" and "fertile" with neutral wording in notifications and widgets. Bloomtide asks for notification permission only when you turn on a reminder.

Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all data erases everything on your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes it. Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Advertising

Bloomtide is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show and measure ads. Banners appear only on the Insights, Learn and Settings screens, never on Today, the calendar or the daily log. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish an export or an article, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, for example to use a PRO feature once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), and reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment; we never see your card details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. This never includes your cycle or health logs. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Permissions

Internet and network state (ads and purchases only), notifications (reminders, asked in context), biometrics (only if you turn on fingerprint or face unlock) and the advertising ID (ads). No contacts, location, camera, microphone or storage permission is used.

Health information

Bloomtide is for general information. It is not a medical device, not contraception and does not diagnose or treat any condition.

Children

Bloomtide is for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
