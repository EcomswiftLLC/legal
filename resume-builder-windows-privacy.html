Privacy policy

Resume Builder: CV & Cover Letter for Windows (package name Resume Builder & CV Maker), by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 1 November 2026.

In short

  • Resume Builder works on your PC. Your resumes, cover letters and job list stay on your PC. The app never uploads them, or any other content, to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.
  • No account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
  • The app itself makes no internet connections. The only online parts are Microsoft Store purchases, licence checks and update checks, which Windows and the Microsoft Store handle, and web pages that open in your browser only when you click a link.

What the app stores, and where

Resume Builder saves your resumes, tailored versions, cover letters, job tracker entries, optional profile photo and settings in its own app data folder on your PC. The exact location is shown in Settings, under Your data. The app also keeps up to 14 daily backup copies there. A saved licence status is kept there too, so Premium keeps working for a short time if the Microsoft Store cannot be reached. Uninstalling the app removes this folder.

You decide what leaves the app. A PDF, Word file, text file, web page, image or backup file is saved where you choose. After that it is an ordinary file on your PC.

What the app reads

The app reads a file only when you choose it: a resume to import (PDF, Word, text, JSON Resume, Reactive Resume or LinkedIn data export), a photo for your resume, or a backup file. It also reads job description text that you paste for the keyword match. All of this is analysed on your PC. The ATS check, writing help and keyword match do not use any online service or artificial intelligence service.

Purchases

Premium is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons: a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. The Microsoft Store handles payment, renewal, refunds and cancellation under Microsoft's terms and privacy statement. The app never sees your payment details. To show your plan, the app asks Windows and the Microsoft Store for the add-on licences of your Microsoft account. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your account details.

Spelling checks

If Check spelling while I type is on, the app uses the spell checker and dictionaries on your PC. Nothing is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in Settings.

Rating requests

After you export a file, the app may ask once in a while whether you would like to rate it. The request is a window inside the app, shown at most once every 48 hours and never while you type. If you choose Rate in Microsoft Store, the Microsoft Store opens its review page and the app stops asking. The app does not see your rating or your review. You can turn the requests off in Settings.

Links

The app opens a web page, the Microsoft Store or your email program only when you choose a link or button, for example the support email address or Manage subscription. Those pages open outside the app and are covered by their own policies.

Deleting your data

Use Erase all data in Settings to delete every resume, letter and job in the app. Uninstalling the app removes the app and its data folder. Files you exported or backed up stay where you saved them until you delete them.

Children

Resume Builder is a general writing tool. It does not collect personal information and has no communication features.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
