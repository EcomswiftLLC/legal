Nail Art Try On: Lacquerie: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Lacquerie is a nail art try-on app made by Ecom Swift LLC. It paints nail polish, nail art and nail shapes on a photo of your own hand. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos are processed on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your pictures, and we don't sell personal information.

Your photos

  • You choose what Lacquerie sees: the app opens only the photos you pick with Android's photo picker, take with your phone's camera app, or share to it from another app. It does not browse your gallery. You can also try the three sample hand photos that come with the app.
  • Everything happens on your device: finding your nails, painting, shading, the salon card and saving are all processed locally.
  • On-device hand detection: to place the nails, Lacquerie uses a MediaPipe hand landmark model from Google that ships inside the app. It finds the joints and fingertips of a hand in the photo. It runs on your phone; the picture is not sent anywhere for it, nothing is used to recognise or identify anyone, and Lacquerie does not estimate age, gender, ethnicity or any other personal trait. The hand points are kept in memory while you edit and are not saved.
  • Skin colour and light: to shade the polish like the light on your hand, the app reads the brightness and colour of the skin around each nail. These values are calculated on the phone for the picture you are editing and are not stored.
  • What gets saved: when you tap Save, the try-on picture or the salon card is written to Pictures/Lacquerie on your phone. If you tap Share, it is handed to the app you pick. To let you continue a try-on later, Lacquerie keeps a working copy of your last photo and where you placed each nail in its private storage; it is removed when you discard the try-on or go back home after saving. Your favourite shades and saved sets (shape, length and polish choices, no photos) are stored in the app. Uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

Lacquerie asks for no camera, contacts, location, microphone or storage permission on current Android versions. The Take a photo button opens your phone's own camera app, and the photo it takes is handed to Lacquerie. On Android 9 and older Lacquerie asks for permission to save to your Pictures folder. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Lacquerie is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Banners appear on the home and save screens, never over your photo while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a picture, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one to use Premium features for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Lacquerie Premium (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) and the one-time Remove ads purchase are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them. Premium removes all ads.

Ratings

After you have saved a few pictures, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service, Google Play Billing and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Children

Lacquerie is made for a general adult audience. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
