Privacy Policy - SEO AUDIT: Rank in AEO, GEO

Last updated: October 7, 2026

SEO AUDIT: Rank in AEO, GEO is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that checks a website's SEO and how ready it is for AI answer engines (AEO and GEO), including crawlability, structured data and llms.txt, and produces a report with plain-language fixes. You type a website address, the app reads that website's public pages, and the result is shown in your Wix dashboard. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

In short. The app keeps no database, no account and no copy of your audits. It does not read your Wix contacts, orders, customers, members or store catalog. An audit exists only while it runs and in your browser afterwards.

1. What the app processes

Whose dataWhatWhy
Merchant (the person using the app)The website address they type and whether they chose a full-site or single-page audit.To know what to audit.
MerchantThe interface language of the Wix dashboard (for example "en" or "es").To write the report in the same language.
The audited websiteIts public pages as any visitor would see them: the page HTML, plus robots.txt, llms.txt, sitemap.xml and the favicon, and for a full-site audit the pages listed in its sitemap (up to 500 pages). Also the HTTP status and response time, and the measurements returned by Google PageSpeed Insights (see section 4).To run the checks and score the site.

Page content is analysed in memory to produce findings. For things like a contact e-mail address, a phone number or a sign-up form, the app only records whether they are present, not the personal details themselves. If a public page happens to contain personal data, it is processed only for the duration of the audit and is not stored. The app does not read your site's private data and sets no advertising or tracking cookies.

2. Where data is stored

Nowhere on our side. The audit runs on the app's backend hosted by Wix, the result is sent straight back to your dashboard, and the report is shown in your browser. If you download the PDF report, it is created in your browser and saved to your own device. Ecom Swift LLC does not keep audit results, website addresses or fetched page content in any database, and the app does not create data collections on your site. Wix, as the host, may keep its own standard technical logs under its own policies.

3. How the audited website sees the app

When the app reads a page it makes ordinary web requests from Wix's cloud servers, not from your computer. It identifies itself with the user agent "AuditifyBot/1.0 (+https://ecomswiftllc.com)", so the audited website can see these requests in its own server logs.

4. Other service providers

  • Wix hosts the app and its backend, provides the dashboard, and identifies the site owner or collaborator who makes each request.
  • Google PageSpeed Insights measures page performance on Google's servers. When a PageSpeed Insights key is configured for the app, the address of the page being audited (and whether to test as mobile or desktop) is sent to Google together with the app's own API key, and Google returns measurements used for the performance measurements that feed the SEO findings. Google handles that request under its own terms and privacy policy. In a full-site audit this is done for a small sample of pages (up to three). When no key is configured, nothing is sent to Google, the report says the Core Web Vitals were not measured, and estimates based on the page's HTML and HTTP responses are used instead.

The app uses no AI model provider, no analytics service, no advertising network and no e-mail service. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

Because the app stores nothing, there is nothing to delete when you remove it from your site. A report you downloaded is your own file and stays on your device until you delete it. If you want confirmation of what we hold, or want us to act on a request, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will reply within 30 days.

6. Security

Requests from the dashboard to the app's backend are made through Wix's authenticated channel. The app accepts only web addresses in a valid format, limits how long each page request may take, limits the number of pages per audit, and returns a generic message instead of internal error details. The PageSpeed Insights key is kept as a server-side setting and is not shipped to your browser.

7. Browser storage and links

The app sets no cookies. The dashboard page keeps one small value in your browser's local storage on the Wix dashboard, which records that you have answered or dismissed the "rate this app" prompt. It contains no personal information and can be cleared by clearing site data. The dashboard loads a font file from Wix's own static servers. The page also has links (to the Wix App Market, the Wix Experts Marketplace, a WhatsApp chat with Ecom Swift LLC and our support e-mail address) that send information to those services only if you click them.

8. International transfers and your rights

Wix and Google operate infrastructure in several countries, so the data described above may be processed outside your own country under their safeguards. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data and to object to its use. We hold no personal data about you or your audits beyond ordinary support correspondence, but you can ask us about any of it at the address below and we respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA. If you run a website and have a question about requests from the user agent named above, contact us and we will help.

9. Children

The app is built for website owners and businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.
