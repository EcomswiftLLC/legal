Glam Makeup Photo Editor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Glam is a makeup photo editor made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos are edited on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your pictures, and we don't sell personal information.

Your photos

  • You choose what Glam sees: the app opens only the photos you pick with Android's photo picker, take with your camera app, or send to it from another app. It does not browse your gallery.
  • Everything happens on your device: face makeup, looks, skin tools, body glam, henna designs, filters, crop, text and stickers are processed locally.
  • On-device models: to place makeup, Glam uses Google ML Kit models that ship inside the app: face mesh and face detection (to find the eyes, brows, lips and face outline), pose detection (to find shoulders, arms and legs) and selfie segmentation (to separate the person from the background). They run on your phone; the picture is not sent anywhere for them, and nothing is used to recognise or identify anyone. The face points are used only to draw makeup on the photo you are editing.
  • What gets saved: when you tap Save, the finished picture is written to Pictures/Glam on your phone. If you tap Share, it is handed to the app you pick. Glam keeps your unfinished edit (a copy of the photo, your edit settings and the detected face and body points) in its private storage so you can continue later, a short list of recent saves, looks you saved, and your settings. Finishing or discarding an edit deletes the draft; uninstalling the app removes everything.

Permissions

Glam asks for no camera, contacts, location or microphone permission. The Camera button opens your phone's own camera app, and the photo it takes is handed to Glam. On Android 9 and older Glam asks for permission to save to your Pictures folder. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Glam is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home and save screens, never while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a photo, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one to unlock a PRO look or tool for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Glam Premium (a monthly or yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase) and the one-time Remove ads purchase are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them. Premium removes all ads.

Ratings

After you have saved a few photos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service, Google Play Billing and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Children

Glam is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
