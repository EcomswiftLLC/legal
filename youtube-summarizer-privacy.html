Privacy Policy — AI YouTube Summarizer

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

AI YouTube Summarizer turns the video you are watching into a short summary, chapter outline, or answer. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you press Summarize, the extension reads the current YouTube video's public transcript (captions) and basic details (title, channel, length) from the page you are on. It does this only for the active tab, only when you ask.
  • Your settings, your API keys, your summary language, and your recent history are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • With the built-in on-device model ("Gemini"), the summary is generated entirely on your device and nothing leaves it.

If you add your own API key (optional)

  • If you choose to add a free API key (e.g. Google Gemini or Groq), the transcript and your request are sent directly to that provider under your own key to generate the summary. We never receive them. Your key is stored only on your device and is used only to call that provider.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, your watch history, the videos you view, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
