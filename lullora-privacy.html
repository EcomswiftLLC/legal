Sleep Sounds: Lullora: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Lullora (Sleep Sounds: Lullora) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Every sound is generated on your phone, and your mixes, settings and sleep log stay on your phone. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Lullora stores on your phone

  • Mixes and settings: the sounds and volumes in your current and saved mixes, mix names you type, timer, fade and appearance choices, your bedtime reminder time and days, and the reason you picked when you first opened the app (for example "Falling asleep").
  • Sleep log: for each night you log, the bedtime and wake time you enter, how rested you felt, an optional note and the mix you played. This is what you write down. Lullora does not measure your sleep.
  • Listening history: start and end times of recent listening sessions and the mix name, used to suggest a bedtime in the sleep log and to show simple totals such as hours of sound played. Only the last 120 sessions are kept.
  • Counters: a few numbers used to space out ads, the rating request and Premium notes.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Delete all data" in Settings removes it at any time.

What Lullora does not use

Lullora does not use the microphone, camera, location, contacts, photos or files. It does not record you and does not track sleep with sensors.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: exporting your sleep log as a spreadsheet (CSV, a Premium feature), contacting support by email, and sharing the app. These go to the app you pick in Android's share sheet or mail app, and that app handles them under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Foreground service (media playback): keeps the sound you started playing with the screen off or after you swipe the app away. A notification with Pause and Stop is shown while it plays.
  • Notifications: for the bedtime reminder. Android asks only when you turn the reminder on. You can decline.
  • Run at startup: to set the bedtime reminder again after your phone restarts.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases. Sounds work offline.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Advertising

Lullora is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your mixes and sleep log are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear only on the Sounds, Mixes, Sleep log and Settings screens, never on the mixer, the night clock or the baby lock. A full-screen ad may appear in the daytime after you finish something, such as saving a mix, limited in number and spacing, and never while sound is playing or between 20:00 and 08:00. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to use a Premium feature for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have saved a mix or logged a few nights, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play. The app asks rarely and never while sound is playing.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Lullora has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health

Lullora plays sounds for relaxation, focus and sleep, and keeps a log you write yourself. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose or prevent any condition. If you have ongoing trouble sleeping, talk to a doctor.

Children

Lullora is a general-audience app for adults, including parents who play sounds for a baby. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
