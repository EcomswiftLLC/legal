Privacy policy

Pomodoro Timer, To-Do & Time Tracker for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 29 October 2026.

In short

  • Your tasks, focus sessions, time entries, habits, plans and settings are stored only on your PC.
  • The app has no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
  • The app does not send your data to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

What the app stores, and where

Everything you enter is saved in a file in the app's own data folder on your PC (for the Microsoft Store version, inside the app's private package storage). That includes tasks, notes, projects, tags, time blocks, focus sessions, time entries, habits, reminders you have received, your weekly reviews and your settings. A backup copy of the previous save is kept next to it.

You can export everything (a JSON backup, CSV files and calendar files) and delete everything at any time in Settings > Your data. Uninstalling the app removes its data folder.

Focus Guard

Focus Guard is off until you turn it on. When it is on and active (during a focus session, a "Guard now" period, or a schedule you set), the app checks the names of running programs on your PC and asks the ones on your list to close. It does not change any Windows setting and does not look at what you do in other programs. The app counts how many programs it closed each day, to show you that number. Program names are not stored beyond your list and are never sent anywhere.

Auto-Plan

Auto-Plan arranges your tasks into time blocks on your PC, using your tasks, your time blocks, your working hours and your past focus sessions. The calculation runs entirely on your PC. Nothing is sent to any server or AI service.

Microsoft Store and Premium

Premium is sold through Microsoft Store. To know whether you have Premium, the app asks Windows for the licence of your Microsoft Store purchases for this app. Payment, renewals, cancellations and refunds are handled by Microsoft under Microsoft's privacy statement. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your payment details.

If you choose to rate the app, the Microsoft Store page opens. Ratings and reviews are handled by Microsoft.

Notifications, sounds and system features

Reminders and end-of-session alerts are shown as local Windows notifications. Sounds are generated on your PC. The app reads how long the PC has been idle only to ask whether to keep away time while you track time, and it reads the Windows display language and theme to match them. If you turn on "Start with Windows", Windows starts the app when you sign in.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.
