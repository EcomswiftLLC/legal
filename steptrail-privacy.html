Steptrail Step Counter: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Steptrail (Step Counter & Pedometer) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your steps, walks and other health and fitness entries are stored on your phone and never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Steptrail stores on your phone

  • Steps: your step counts per hour, read from your phone's step sensor (or, on phones without one, counted from the motion sensor while Keep counting is on). Steptrail also keeps the goal that applied on each day, your badges and streaks.
  • Profile: the sex, birth year, height, weight, units and stride length you enter. They are optional and are used only to estimate distance, calories and active time on your phone.
  • Tracked walks: time, steps, distance, pace and calories of walks you start. If you turn on "Record my route" for a walk, the GPS points of that walk are stored with it.
  • Water and weight: the entries you add.
  • Settings: goals, reminders, quiet hours, theme and similar choices.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own backup (to your Google account, if you have turned it on in Android settings) may include it; that backup is controlled by you and by Google. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and Settings > Your data > Delete all data removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: "Back up to a file" and "Export CSV" save or share a file to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker or share sheet, and "Share" sends a summary image to the app you pick. That place or app then handles the file under its own privacy policy. Steptrail does not connect to Health Connect, Google Fit or any other health service.

Permissions

  • Physical activity: lets Steptrail read the step sensor. It is asked during setup or from the Today screen, with an explanation, and you can decline.
  • Notifications: for the goal reached note, reminders you turn on, and the notifications shown while Keep counting or a tracked walk runs.
  • Location: asked only when you start a walk with "Record my route" switched on, and used only while that walk runs (no background location). Route points stay on your phone and are not sent to us or to any map service. Routes are drawn by the app itself, without downloading map tiles.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Steptrail reads the step sensor once so the steps since the restart are kept.

Steptrail asks for no contacts, photos, microphone or storage permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Steptrail is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your steps, walks, routes, weight and other health entries are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on the history and settings screens, never over the daily ring or during a walk. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished walk, limited in number and spacing, and never during a walk. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a good moment, for example the first time you reach your daily goal or a seven day streak, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Steptrail has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health information

Steptrail is a wellness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Step counts, distance, calories and active time are estimates.

Children

Steptrail is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
