Privacy Policy - Spinly: Popups & Spin Wheel

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Spinly: Popups & Spin Wheel is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that lets a site owner show popups, coupon wheels and other messages to visitors and collect email addresses. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives and why. Two groups of people are involved: the site owner who installs the app, and the visitors of that site.

Short version. The app stores the popups and settings the site owner writes, the details a visitor chooses to submit in a popup (an email address and, only if the site owner asks for them, a name and phone number), the coupon issued, and anonymous daily counters. Submitted contacts are also added to the site's own Wix Contacts. Wix hosts the app and keeps this data on the site owner's own Wix site. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive a copy of it, does not sell it and uses no advertising or analytics providers.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Ecom Swift LLC. You can reach us at wix@ecomswiftllc.com. The site owner is the controller of the data collected from their visitors. Ecom Swift LLC provides the software and acts on the site owner's instructions.

3. Contacts, consent and labels

When a visitor submits a popup, the app creates or updates a contact for that email address in the site's Wix Contacts and tags it with the label the site owner chose. If the visitor ticked the marketing consent box, the email address is also recorded in Wix as subscribed to marketing email for that site. Without the tick the contact is saved without a marketing subscription. The site owner is responsible for the content of the popup text, the consent wording and for how they use the contacts afterwards.

4. Where data is stored

The app has no database of its own. Its data lives in private data collections (Wix Data) on the site owner's own Wix site, in a space reserved for the app, and in Wix Contacts and Wix Coupons. Each site has its own collections, so one site's data is never mixed with another's. Only the app's backend, after Wix has verified the caller, and site administrators can read or change them. The app needs the Wix CMS (Wix Data) on the site for this.

The visitor's browser keeps one small item in local storage on the site's own domain, only when the site's cookie consent policy allows functional storage. It holds a random visitor code, counters and times that tell the popup how often it was shown or closed, and the A/B variant it assigned. It never contains an email address or any submitted detail. The app sets no cookies.

5. Abuse protection

Submissions are checked on the server. A hidden field and a minimum time on the form stop most bots, repeated submissions from the same address or network are slowed down, and each email address can receive a popup's reward only once. The network address is held in the memory of the server only for the time window of the limit and is never written to the data collections.

6. Service providers

  • Wix hosts the app and the data, provides contacts, coupons and orders, and identifies the site owner.

We do not sell personal data and do not share data for advertising. We receive no copy of the site's data and operate no other server that handles it.

When the app is removed, Wix handles what happens to its data collections according to Wix's app removal rules.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every dashboard request is checked against a verified Wix identity before anything is read or written, every input is validated on the server, prize chances never leave the server, and coupon codes are generated with a secure random source and are single use. Merchant-written text is shown as plain text, never as HTML, and exports are protected against spreadsheet formula injection. No system is perfectly secure, and the data on the site is also protected by the site owner's own Wix account security.

9. International transfers

Wix operates globally, so data may be processed in countries other than where the site owner or the visitor lives. Wix applies its own transfer safeguards. We do not transfer personal data anywhere else.

10. Visitor rights

Visitors who want to access, correct or delete the data they submitted, or withdraw marketing consent, should contact the site they visited, because the site owner controls that data. Site owners can find, export and delete leads in the app and manage contacts in Wix. We will help a site owner with any request at wix@ecomswiftllc.com.

11. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age). Site owners should not target popups at children.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify site owners in the app.
