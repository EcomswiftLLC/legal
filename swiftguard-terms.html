What SwiftGuard is

SwiftGuard is a security companion that works beside Microsoft Defender. It asks Microsoft Defender Antivirus, which is part of Windows, to run scans and shows the results, your Windows security settings and your PC's health. It does not replace Microsoft Defender or any other antivirus program, it is not registered as an antivirus provider in Windows Security, and it does not guarantee that your PC is free of malware or other threats. Real-time protection on your PC is provided by Microsoft Defender or the antivirus you installed.

Your use

You may install and use SwiftGuard on Windows PCs as the Microsoft Store allows for your Microsoft account. Do not reverse engineer it, resell it or use it to scan systems you are not allowed to scan. Quarantine, restore, delete, cleanup and startup changes act on your PC only at your request (or through an automation rule you turned on and confirmed); keep backups of files that matter to you, because files deleted by PC Cleanup or deleted from quarantine cannot be restored by SwiftGuard.
