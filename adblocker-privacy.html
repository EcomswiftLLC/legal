Privacy Policy — Ad Blocker, Popup Blocker & Malware Protection

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

This extension blocks ads, pop-ups, trackers and known-malware requests on the websites you visit. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • Blocking happens locally in your browser using Chrome's declarativeNetRequest engine and bundled filter rules. We do not see the pages you visit.
  • Your settings, your per-site whitelist, and your blocked-count statistics are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.
  • On-page cleanup (hiding ad containers, cookie-consent pop-ups, the optional element picker) runs in your browser and does not send page content anywhere.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, page content, or personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or mine cryptocurrency. There are no third-party analytics.

The only network calls

  • Malware host list: the extension periodically downloads a public list of known-malware hosts so it can block them. This is a download only; no information about you is sent.
  • Pro subscription (optional): if you choose to unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, and re-check it periodically, and to cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.
