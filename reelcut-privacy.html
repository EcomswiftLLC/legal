ReelCut Video Editor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

ReelCut (Video Editor & Maker) is a video editor made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your videos, photos and recordings are edited on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your media, and we don't sell personal information.

Your videos, photos and sound

  • You choose what ReelCut sees: the app opens only the videos and photos you pick with Android's photo picker, the audio files you pick with the system file picker, and files another app shares with it ("Edit with ReelCut"). It does not browse your gallery or your music library.
  • Everything happens on your device: trimming, splitting, speed, filters, transitions, text, stickers, picture in picture, the audio mix and the export are processed locally with Android's own video codecs and the open-source Media3 libraries. Nothing is sent anywhere for editing.
  • Drafts: your projects are saved inside the app's private storage as small project files that point to the media you picked, so you can continue later. Freeze frames and voice-overs you record are stored inside the project. Deleting a project or uninstalling the app removes them.
  • What gets saved: when an export finishes, the video is written to Movies/ReelCut on your phone (GIFs to Pictures/ReelCut, extracted sound to Music/ReelCut). If you tap Share, the file is handed to the app you pick.

Permissions

  • Microphone: asked only when you tap the voice-over record button. The recording stays in your project on your phone. If you decline, every other feature keeps working.
  • Storage: on Android 9 and older the app asks for permission to save to your Movies and Pictures folders. Newer versions need no storage permission.
  • ReelCut asks for no camera, contacts or location permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

ReelCut is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home and export screens, never over your video while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished export, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video appears only when you choose to watch one to unlock a premium tool for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and "Remove ads" are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns from Google Play which products you own, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

After you have exported a few videos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics, purchase history and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Children

ReelCut is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
