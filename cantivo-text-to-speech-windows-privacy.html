Cantivo Privacy Policy

Text to Speech Voiceover Maker: Cantivo for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Effective 2026-11-23.

Summary

Cantivo works on your PC. Speech is made on your computer by voices inside the app and the voices built into Windows. Your scripts, documents and audio are never uploaded by Cantivo. There is no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Data stored on your PC

  • Your projects (script text and settings), your pronunciation list, voice styles and app settings, in the app's data folder.
  • A cache of the speech made for each line, so it does not have to be made again. You can clear it in Settings; it is trimmed by itself above 2 GB.
  • Files you export are saved only where you choose.
  • Uninstalling Cantivo removes its data folder.

Network use

The only network traffic comes from Microsoft Store, used to check your purchases, to buy an Export pack or Premium, to show prices in your currency and to open the rating window when you ask. Microsoft handles those payments; Cantivo never sees your payment details. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Voices

The voices are synthetic. Cantivo does not record your voice, has no microphone access and cannot clone voices. Windows voices are provided by Microsoft Windows on your PC.

Children

Cantivo does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact
