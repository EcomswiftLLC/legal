What ClipStack is

ClipStack is a clipboard manager for Windows. It keeps a history of what you copy on your PC, lets you search it and paste from it with a shortcut, and adds snippets, text transforms and privacy rules. It works next to Windows clipboard history (Win+V) and does not replace or turn it off.

Your use

You may install and use ClipStack on Windows PCs as the Microsoft Store allows for your Microsoft account. Do not reverse engineer it, resell it, or use it to collect what other people copy without their permission. You decide what ClipStack keeps: turn capture off or pause it, ignore apps, and delete clips at any time. ClipStack pastes only when you ask it to. Windows does not let any app type into programs running as administrator; in that case ClipStack leaves the clip on the clipboard for you to paste with Ctrl+V.

Sensitive-data detection is a pattern check and cannot recognise every secret. Do not rely on it as your only protection for passwords or other secrets; use a password manager that marks its copies as private, or add the app to ClipStack's ignore list.

Free and Pro

The free version includes clipboard history of text, formatted text, links, file lists and colours (the newest 200 clips, kept for up to 30 days), the quick-paste popup, the history window, 10 pinned clips, 10 snippets, basic transforms, privacy rules and encryption on disk. ClipStack Pro adds the features shown in the app and in the Microsoft Store listing, including unlimited history, image history and copying text from images, collections and tags, snippet placeholders and abbreviations, all transforms, the paste queue, backups and insights.

Pro is sold only through the Microsoft Store as an add-on: a monthly or yearly subscription that renews until you cancel it in your Microsoft account, or a one-time lifetime purchase that does not renew and stays linked to your Microsoft account for as long as ClipStack is available to you through the Microsoft Store. Prices, taxes, refunds and cancellation follow the Microsoft Store terms. If the Microsoft Store cannot be reached, a subscription keeps working for up to 7 days after its last confirmation.

If Pro ends, nothing is deleted: clips beyond the free limits are hidden until you get Pro again or delete them on the Trust and privacy page.
