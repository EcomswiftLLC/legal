Privacy Policy — SEO Inspector

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

SEO Inspector audits the web page you are viewing and shows you an SEO score, a Google search preview, and exactly what to fix. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup and run an audit, the extension reads the current page's own content — its title, meta tags, headings, links, images and structured data — from the active tab. It does this only for the tab you are on, only when you ask.
  • The entire audit, the score, and the SERP preview are computed locally in your browser. The page content is never sent to us or to any third party.
  • Your settings and your saved audit history (a Pro feature) are stored locally on your device. They are not sent to us.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email address you used at checkout. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification, we do not sell or share it, and the free features never send it.

Reminders

  • If you are on the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours to show what Pro adds. Paying users are never shown it, and it includes a "Don't remind me again" option that turns it off for good. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved. (This uses the alarms permission.)

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you audit, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
