Privacy Policy - Proofly: Social Proof Popups

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Proofly: Social Proof Popups is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that shows visitors small popups built from real activity on the site, such as a recent purchase, booking or sign-up, how many people are viewing a page, how many units sold recently and how few are left in stock. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs the app (the "merchant") decides what the popups show and is the controller of their customers' data. Ecom Swift LLC acts as the merchant's processor and only handles that data to run the app. All of it stays inside the merchant's own Wix site.

1. What the app processes

The app does not read payment details or passwords, does not track visitors across sites, does not fingerprint browsers and sets no advertising cookies. It never invents or estimates activity: every notification comes from an event that really happened on the site.

2. Where data is stored

  • All of the above is stored in private data collections of the app on the merchant's own Wix site (Wix Data, which needs Wix CMS on the site): settings, recent activity, sales ledger, product cache, stock levels, live presence and daily metrics. Only the site's owner and collaborators and the app can read them. Visitors never read the collections: their browser only receives the notifications the server has already decided to show.
  • Ecom Swift LLC has no database of its own for this data and does not copy it anywhere else.

3. What visitors' browsers receive

The popup script asks the app's server for the notifications that are due on the current page. The server applies the merchant's privacy choices first: names and places are already reduced, events wait their configured delay, and a live count is sent only when it is at or above the merchant's minimum. If the merchant turns on "Show counts instead of people", visitors see totals only and no names or places at all. The script writes small values to the visitor's browser, listed in section 6.

4. Other service providers

  • Wix hosts the app, stores its data (Wix Data), provides orders, bookings, events, pricing plans, forms, the product catalog and the visitor analytics consent signal, and identifies the site owner.

The app has no servers of its own and uses no AI model provider, analytics service, advertising network or e-mail provider. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

5. Retention and deletion

  • Recent activity is kept for the number of days the merchant chooses (from 1 to 90 days, 30 by default) and older records are deleted automatically. The sales ledger keeps about 8 days. Live presence rows are deleted within minutes. Daily metrics hold only totals.
  • From the Data and privacy tab the merchant can erase all stored activity at any time.
  • When the app is removed from a site, it deletes the stored recent activity, the sales ledger and the live presence rows. Settings, the product cache and daily totals contain no customer data and may remain in the site's data collections until the merchant deletes them. Wix, not the app, controls what is still reachable after removal, so if anything remains, or for help with any deletion request, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com.

6. Browser storage

On the storefront the popup script writes to the visitor's browser: a random per-tab token and the popups already shown or closed in this visit (session storage, cleared when the tab closes), and the time a visitor closed a popup, only when the merchant has set a "stay quiet for N days" option (local storage). None of it contains personal information. The popup script is registered as a functional script. The separate analytics script, which only counts visits for the Analytics tab, is registered as an analytics script and runs only for visitors who accepted analytics. The merchant's dashboard keeps a few small preferences in the browser on the Wix dashboard (for example whether the "rate this app" prompt was answered). The dashboard also has links to the Wix App Market, the Wix Experts Marketplace, a WhatsApp chat with Ecom Swift LLC and our support e-mail address, which send information to those services only if clicked.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stays in the merchant's own Wix site. The app's collections can be read and changed only by the site's owner and collaborators and by the app. Dashboard actions are limited to administrators, inputs are validated on the server, the public endpoints are rate limited, and logs contain no personal data. No secrets are stored in the app's code.

8. Merchant responsibilities

Showing a customer's first name and town to other visitors may need to be mentioned in the merchant's own privacy policy and, in some places, may need consent. Proofly offers anonymous and counts-only modes, initials, region or country instead of city, and extra delays for this reason. The merchant decides which to use.

9. Your rights

Customers and visitors can ask the site owner to access, correct or delete the data collected about them. Merchants can erase stored activity in the app and can ask us to help with any request. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data and to object to its use. We respond to requests relating to data we process within 30 days. Wix may process data in several countries under its own safeguards. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

10. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

11. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify merchants in the app.
