Privacy Policy - Products Bundles & Upsells

Last updated: October 7, 2026

Products Bundles & Upsells is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that lets a Wix Stores merchant create quantity-discount bundles, add-on and combo offers, buy-one-get-one deals, mix-and-match sets and spend-and-unlock rewards, shows them on product pages and in the cart, and applies the discounts through Wix eCommerce. This policy explains what data the app handles and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs the app (the "merchant") decides how offers are used and is the controller of their shoppers' data. Ecom Swift LLC provides the software. The app does not collect personal information from shoppers.

1. What the app processes

Whose dataWhatWhy
MerchantThe bundle settings they enter: the product, its name, address, image, price and currency, the tiers, add-ons or reward rules, and the look of the offer (colours, button text, layout). The app also reads the merchant's Wix Stores catalog (product names, images, prices).To run, show and price the offers, and to let the merchant pick products.
Merchant (suggestions)When the merchant uses "Smart suggestions", the app reads up to 100 store orders inside the dashboard and counts only product IDs and quantities, to find best-sellers and products bought together. Buyer names, e-mail addresses and addresses are not used or stored.To propose ready-made bundles.
Shoppers (site visitors)While a shopper views an offer, the app reads the contents of that shopper's current Wix cart (products, quantities and prices) in their browser and, when the shopper chooses, adds or removes items using Wix's cart. The app collects no name, e-mail address, payment detail or identifier.To show which offers are selected, show progress toward a reward, and add the offer's products to the cart.

The app has no analytics or tracking, sets no cookies and reads no payment details or passwords.

2. Where data is stored

Bundle settings are stored in a data collection that belongs to the app on the merchant's own Wix site (Wix Data), and the discounts each bundle needs are created as discount rules in the merchant's own Wix eCommerce. The collection can be read publicly because the storefront must be able to show the active offer for the product a visitor is viewing, so it holds only offer and product information and never personal data. Only the app and the site's owner and collaborators can change it. Ecom Swift LLC does not copy this data into any other database.

3. Other service providers

  • Wix hosts the app and stores its data, provides the Wix Stores catalog, the shopper's cart, the discount rules and checkout, and identifies the site owner.

The app has no servers of its own and uses no AI model provider, analytics service, advertising network or e-mail service. We do not sell personal data and we do not share it for advertising.

4. Retention and deletion

  • A bundle is kept until the merchant deletes it. Deleting a bundle in the app removes its record and the discount rules the app created for it.
  • When the app is removed from a site, it pauses all bundles and deletes the discount rules it created, so no offer keeps discounting after removal. The app's data collection may stay in the site's data until the merchant deletes it. Wix controls what is still reachable after removal, so if a rule or record remains, delete it in the site's dashboard or write to support@ecomswiftllc.com and we will help.
  • Shopper carts and orders belong to Wix and the merchant. The app keeps no copy of them.

5. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Changes to bundles and discount rules go through a backend endpoint that first checks that the caller is the site owner, a collaborator or the app itself, so shoppers cannot change anything. An offer is validated before any discount is created, and if saving fails the discount rules just created are rolled back so that no untracked discount is left live. No secrets are stored in the app's code.

6. Browser storage

The shopper-facing offer does not set cookies and does not write to the visitor's browser storage. The merchant's dashboard keeps a few small values in the browser on the Wix dashboard: whether the "rate this app" prompt was answered, which layout version the merchant prefers, and a flag that a one-time maintenance check has run in the current session. They contain no personal information and can be cleared by clearing site data. The dashboard also has links to the Wix App Market, the Wix Experts Marketplace, a WhatsApp chat with Ecom Swift LLC and our support e-mail address, which send information to those services only if clicked.

7. Your rights

Because the app collects no personal data from shoppers, requests from shoppers about their orders or carts should go to the site owner, who holds that data in Wix. Merchants can view, edit and delete everything the app holds for their site, in the app and in their site's data, and can ask us to help with any request. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data. We respond to requests relating to data we process within 30 days. Wix may process data in several countries under its own safeguards. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

8. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify merchants in the app.
