Privacy Policy - Arrivo: Delivery Date Tracking

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Arrivo: Delivery Date Tracking is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that shows estimated delivery dates on a Wix Stores site and provides a public order tracking page. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives and why.

Short version. The app stores the delivery rules and settings the site owner writes, plus anonymous daily counters. To answer a tracking lookup it reads the matching order from the site's own Wix Stores data at that moment and returns only a small set of fields to the person who entered the matching order number and email address. It does not copy orders, does not receive payment details, and sets no cookies. Wix hosts the app and keeps its data on the site owner's own Wix site. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive a copy of that data.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Ecom Swift LLC. You can reach us at wix@ecomswiftllc.com.

3. Where data is stored

The app has no database of its own. Its settings, counters and rate-limit entries live in private data collections (Wix Data) on the site owner's own Wix site, in a space reserved for the app. Each site has its own collections, so one site's data is never mixed with another's. Only the app's backend, after Wix has verified the caller, and site administrators can read or change them. The app needs the Wix CMS (Wix Data) on the site for this.

The visitor's browser keeps two small items in local storage on the store's own domain: the country the visitor chose for delivery estimates, and a flag that tells the page whether delivery rules exist so it can reserve space and avoid layout shifts. Neither identifies the visitor. The app sets no cookies.

4. Tracking page protections

A tracking lookup succeeds only when the order number and the email address both match the same order. A wrong order number and a wrong email address produce the same answer, and the answer always takes about the same time, so the page cannot be used to find out which order numbers or email addresses exist. Lookups are limited per order, per network address and per site.

5. Rate limiting

To protect the service from abuse, the backend keeps a one-way hash of the order number and of the network address, a request count and a timestamp, in a private collection on the site. Entries expire within about an hour after they stop being used, and expired entries are deleted as the app runs. They are not linked to any other data.

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every dashboard request is checked against a verified Wix identity before anything is read or written, and every input is validated on the server. Public endpoints return only the fields described above and are rate limited. Merchant-written text is shown as plain text, never as HTML. No system is perfectly secure, and the data on the site is also protected by the site owner's own Wix account security.

9. International transfers

Wix operates globally, so data may be processed in countries other than where the site owner or the visitor lives. Wix applies its own transfer safeguards. We do not transfer personal data anywhere else.

10. Your rights

The app keeps no personal data about site visitors beyond the short-lived rate-limit hashes described above. A site owner can view and change everything the app stores for their site from the app's dashboard, and can contact us at wix@ecomswiftllc.com with any privacy question. Visitors who have questions about their order data should contact the store first.

11. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age). It does not knowingly process children's data.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify site owners in the app.
