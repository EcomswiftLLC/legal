Before you buy

Do I need a Framer account?

No. If you do not have one, we set it up for you for free. Publishing on your own domain needs a Framer site plan, which Framer sells separately.

What is the licence?

One purchase covers one website, for you or for one client. You can change everything. You cannot resell or share the theme itself.

Is the demo content real?

No. Business names, prices, phone numbers and sample projects are placeholders. The setup guide lists what to replace.

Need a hand?

Setup questions are answered through our contact page. Customising a theme or building one from scratch is quoted separately.
