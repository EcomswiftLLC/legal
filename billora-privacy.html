Privacy Policy: InvoiceMaker Invoices and Estimates

Last updated: November 6, 2026

InvoiceMaker: Invoices & Estimates ("InvoiceMaker") is a Wix app published by Ecom Swift LLC. It lets a Wix site owner create invoices, estimates, receipts and credit notes, keep a client book and an item list, send documents by email, share an online view of a document, and optionally create documents automatically for purchases made on the site. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it is kept and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs InvoiceMaker (the "merchant") decides whom to bill and what to store, and is the controller of that data. Ecom Swift LLC provides the software and acts as the merchant's processor. We do not use billing data for our own purposes, and we never sell it.

InvoiceMaker does not process payments, does not collect card or bank account numbers from clients, and does not place cookies or trackers on the merchant's site. The online document view only shows the one document that matches its private link.

2. Where the data is stored

All business data lives in Wix Data collections created on the merchant's own Wix site (namespace @ecomswiftllc/billora). The app's backend runs on Wix's app hosting infrastructure and reads or writes that data only to perform an action the merchant requested, or to process a purchase event when automation is on. Ecom Swift LLC does not copy the data to its own servers or databases.

3. Emails

When the merchant sends a document, a reminder, or an automatic invoice, the email is sent through Wix's email service on behalf of the site, with the merchant's business name as sender and the merchant's business email as reply address. The email contains the document and, if the merchant set up an invoice page, a private link to view it online. Emails count toward the site's Wix email quota.

4. Access and security

  • Only the site owner and the site's collaborators, through the Wix dashboard, can use the app's management functions. Every request is checked against the identity Wix attaches to it.
  • Document links use a random 32-character token that cannot be guessed; a draft is never shown publicly, and the merchant can replace a link at any time.
  • All traffic uses HTTPS. Inputs are validated on the server. Logs never contain client names, emails or document contents.

5. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the merchant's site until the merchant deletes it. Documents moved to the trash are deleted for good after 30 days. Merchants can export documents, clients and items as CSV at any time. When the app is removed from a site, Wix may remove the app's collections with it, so we recommend exporting first. Buyers and clients who want their data changed or deleted should contact the merchant who billed them.

6. Wix permissions requested

InvoiceMaker asks Wix for the permissions it needs to do the above: manage its own data collections, read the app instance (site name, currency, language), send emails on behalf of the site, read contacts (only for the merchant's import), read Wix Stores products, Bookings services and Pricing Plans (only for the merchant's item import), and receive order events from Wix eCommerce, Pricing Plans and Events (only acted on when automation is on).

7. Children

InvoiceMaker is a business tool for site owners and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we update the date above. Material changes are announced in the app.
