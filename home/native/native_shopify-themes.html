How buying works

Pay at checkout

Click Buy, then pay securely at checkout. Prices are in US dollars.

Download the zip

A download link appears on the confirmation page and arrives by email. The zip holds the theme file, an install guide and the licence.

Upload it to Shopify

In your Shopify admin open Online Store, Themes, Add theme, then Upload zip file and choose the theme file from the zip.

Customize and publish

Add your products and photos in the theme editor. Work on it unpublished first, so your live store stays untouched until you publish.
