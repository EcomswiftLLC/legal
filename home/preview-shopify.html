Frequently asked questions about our Shopify services

Plain answers about what we build for Shopify stores, how we work and how to start.

What Shopify services does Ecom Swift LLC offer?

We build and redesign Shopify stores, improve conversion rate, speed and SEO, customize themes, build custom apps, set up bundles and upsells, migrate stores to Shopify, build on Shopify Plus, manage stores day to day and audit existing stores.

How much does a Shopify project cost?

The price depends on the scope, so we send a written quote in US dollars after a short brief. Work starts once you approve the quote. If you already have a store, we begin with a free audit so you can see what to fix before you commit.

Can you improve the conversion rate of my existing Shopify store?

Yes. Our Shopify CRO service improves product pages, the cart and the checkout flow, and we measure the results. We start with a free audit that shows what to fix first.

Can you move my store to Shopify without losing search rankings?

Yes. We move your products, customers and content from platforms such as WooCommerce, Magento, BigCommerce and Wix, and set up redirects so you keep your search traffic.

Do you build custom Shopify apps and themes?

Yes. We build custom public and private Shopify apps, themes made for one brand, and changes to the theme you already use. We also offer ready-made themes you can edit without code.

Do you offer support after launch?

Yes. Store management and ongoing development are available after launch, so fixes, updates and new features continue without starting over.
