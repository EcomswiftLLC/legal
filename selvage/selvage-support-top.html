What to include in your message

  1. Your store address, such as your-store.myshopify.com.
  2. The page address where the problem happens.
  3. What you expected, and what happened.
  4. A screenshot or a short screen recording. You can attach it with the form.
  5. Your device and browser.

Please do not send passwords or customer data.
