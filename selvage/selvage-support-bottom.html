Our support policy

  • We reply to every message within 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, except public holidays in the United States.
  • We help with questions about how Selvage works and how to use its settings.
  • We fix bugs in the theme, such as broken layouts, dead links and logic errors. Critical bugs are fixed as soon as we can, and fixes ship in theme updates.
  • Support does not include paid services. These are not part of the theme price: custom design or code changes, installing or configuring apps, adding your products and content, moving a store from another theme or platform, and applying theme updates to a store that has code changes.
  • If a problem comes from an app, your Shopify plan or your domain provider, contact that provider.
  • Customizing theme code is not supported. Before you edit code, duplicate your theme and work on the copy. For code changes, we suggest hiring a Shopify Partner from the Shopify Experts marketplace. You can also ask us for a quote.

Updates

We publish updates in the Shopify Theme Store. Release notes list what changed in each version.
