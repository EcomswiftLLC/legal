Selvage theme for Shopify

Selvage documentation

The merchant guide for the Selvage theme, version 1.0.0.

This guide explains how to set up and run a store with the Selvage theme. It is written from the theme's own settings and code for version 1.0.0. If a setting in your theme editor does not match what you read here, check your theme version under Theme settings and contact support.

Contact Selvage support

Words used in this guide

  • Theme editor: the customizer opened from Online Store, Themes, Customize.
  • Theme settings: the settings that apply to the whole store, found in the theme editor sidebar. In Selvage they are grouped as Colors, Typography, Layout, Product cards, Animation, Cart, Search, Logo and favicon, and Social media.
  • Section: a full-width part of a page, such as a slideshow or a featured collection.
  • Block: a smaller piece inside a section, such as a single price or a single menu promotion.
  • Color scheme: a saved set of colors. Each section picks one.

Chapter 1

Getting started

Selvage is an Online Store 2.0 theme for women's fashion stores. Every page is built from sections you can add, remove and reorder in the theme editor.

Before you open the theme editor

Prepare these in your Shopify admin first. The theme reads them, and several sections look empty without them.

  1. Products with photos. Use the same aspect ratio for every product photo when you can. The theme crops photos to a fixed frame on collection pages, and the frame is set in Theme settings under Product cards.
  2. Product options. Name your color option Color and your size option Size. The theme looks for these names to show color dots and size chips on product cards. Other names still work on the product page, but cards will not show them.
  3. Collections. Create the collections you want in the menu, for example Dresses and Knitwear.
  4. Menus. In Content, Menus, check that you have a menu with the handle main-menu (your main navigation) and one with the handle footer. The header and footer use them by default.
  5. Policies. In Settings, Policies, add your refund and shipping policies. Several blocks link to them when they exist.
  6. Payments. Turn on the payment methods you accept. The payment icons in the footer and on product pages come from this list.

Choose a preset

Selvage includes three presets: Selvage (editorial luxury), Toile (airy and romantic) and Bias (modern and bold). The preset you add from the Theme Store comes with its own starting home, product and collection page layouts, header and footer. Switching presets later in the theme editor changes colors, fonts, page width, product cards and motion only. It does not change your page layouts, products or content. Pick the preset closest to your brand when you add the theme, and then adjust.

Add your brand

Open Online Store, Themes, Customize, then Theme settings.

  1. Logo and favicon. Upload your logo and favicon. The favicon works best as a 48 x 48 px .png. The logo can be a wide wordmark or a square or portrait mark. Portrait and square logos are limited to half the logo width setting in height.
  2. Colors. Review the six color schemes. See Color schemes and fonts.
  3. Typography. Choose a heading font and a body font. See the same page.
  4. Social media. Paste the full address of each profile you use. Leave the rest empty. Empty fields show nothing.
  5. Sharing image. This image appears when someone shares a page that has no image of its own. A 1200 x 628 px .jpg is recommended.

Build the home page

Select Home page in the theme editor, then add, remove and reorder sections. Home page sections lists every section and what it does.

Replace the starter text. Defaults in sections are written to show you how to use them, not to be published as they are.

Set up the shopping experience

  • Cart: choose between a drawer and a cart page. See Cart drawer.
  • Collection filters: install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app and choose your filters. See Collection page and filters.
  • Product page: add the blocks you need and set the optional product fields. See Product page and blocks.
  • Languages and countries: see Localization.

Check before you launch

Work through this list on a phone and on a computer.

  1. Open the home page, a collection, a product with options and a product without options.
  2. Choose a color and a size, add the product to the cart, then change the quantity and remove the item.
  3. Go to checkout and check that your payment methods appear.
  4. Search for a product and for a word that returns nothing.
  5. Open the contact page and send a test message.
  6. Use only the keyboard on the home page and a product page. You should always see where the focus is.
  7. Turn on your device's reduced motion setting and reload. Animations should be calm or off.

What the theme does not do

The theme stays within Shopify's built-in features. It does not include a wishlist, countdown timers that restart, fake stock counts or visitor counts, discount code fields in the cart, or an Instagram feed. Product reviews, subscriptions and bundle discounts need Shopify features or apps. See App compatibility.

Chapter 2

Color schemes and fonts

Color schemes

A color scheme is a saved set of colors. Every section has a Color scheme setting, so you can give each section its own look without changing the rest of the store.

Open Theme settings, then Colors. Selvage includes six schemes. They are named Scheme 1 to Scheme 6 in the editor, and each one has these colors:

Color Used for
Background The section background.
Background gradient Replaces the background color when you set it.
Text Body text.
Heading Headings.
Muted text Captions, secondary text.
Lines and borders Hairline rules and outlines.
Solid button background and Solid button label Filled buttons.
Outline button Outline buttons.
Accent Links and small highlights.
Badge background and Badge text Product badges.

Presets

Selvage includes three presets: Selvage, Toile and Bias. Each preset sets its own six color schemes, fonts, page width, product card style and motion level. Applying a preset changes presentation settings only, not your products, pages or section content. The descriptions below are for the default Selvage preset.

The default schemes in the Selvage preset are:

  • Scheme 1: ivory background with espresso text. Used for most sections.
  • Scheme 2: a slightly deeper cream. Used for the editorial story and the newsletter.
  • Scheme 3: espresso background with light text. Used for the footer, the announcement bar and text over images.
  • Scheme 4: champagne. Scheme 5: dusty rose. Scheme 6: charcoal with light text. Use them for campaigns and promotions.

Tips:

  • Choose a text color that is clearly different from the background. Aim for a contrast ratio of at least 4.5 to 1 for body text and 3 to 1 for large text, buttons and borders. Free contrast checkers on the web will calculate it.
  • When a section has text over an image, it also has a "Color scheme over image" setting. That scheme sets the text, button and overlay colors on top of the picture.
  • Checkout colors are not part of the theme. Change them in Settings, Checkout and accounts, Customize.
  • Shop Pay and other accelerated checkout buttons keep their own brand colors. The theme does not recolor them.

Fonts

Open Theme settings, then Typography.

Setting What it changes
Heading font All headings. Default: Cormorant.
Body font Paragraphs and most interface text. Default: Jost.
Navigation and button font Menu links, buttons and small labels. It starts as the same font as the body font.

You pick fonts from Shopify's font library. Custom font files are not supported, and you do not need an app for fonts.

Fine-tune the type with these settings:

  • Heading size: scales every heading between 80% and 140%.
  • Heading letter spacing and Heading line height.
  • Heading case: Normal or Uppercase.
  • Body text size and Body line height.
  • Navigation and button letter spacing, case and weight.

Selvage loads bold, italic and bold italic styles of your chosen fonts when the font family offers them. If a font has no italic, browsers create a slanted version.

Choosing a very wide font, a very large heading size or Uppercase heading case can make long product names wrap onto many lines. Check a collection page and a product with a long title after you change them.

Layout, corners and lines

Under Theme settings, Layout:

  • Page width: Narrow (1280 px), Standard (1520 px), Wide (1760 px) or Full width.
  • Side margin: 12 to 64 px.
  • Space between sections: Compact, Standard or Airy.
  • Button corner radius, Input corner radius: 0 gives square corners, which is the default look.
  • Line thickness: 1 to 3 px for hairlines.

Header and mega menu

The header lives in the header group, together with the announcement bar. Open any page in the theme editor and select Header or Announcement bar at the top of the page.

The account button is Shopify's account component. It shows on both phones and computers.

The search button opens a search drawer. The cart button opens the cart drawer or goes to the cart page, depending on your Cart setting.

Transparent header

Turn it on only when the first section of your home page is an image or video. Text over an image can be hard to read, so check the contrast. If your first section is plain, leave it off.

Menus with dropdowns

Selvage supports three levels:

  1. Top-level links appear in the main bar.
  2. Their child links appear in a dropdown.
  3. Grandchild links appear under each child link.

Build your menu in Content, Menus. Dropdowns open when you hover, tap or move keyboard focus into them. With the keyboard, the Down arrow on a top-level link opens its panel and moves into it, the Left and Right arrows move between top-level links, and Escape closes the panel and returns focus to its link.

A top-level item with the link # becomes a button that only opens its panel.

Mega menu

The mega menu is a full-width panel with several columns. It is built from two things: your menu, and a Mega menu block.

  1. Build a menu where a top-level item has child links. Each child link becomes a column heading, and its own child links are listed under it.
  2. In the theme editor, select Header, then Add block, then Mega menu.
  3. In Menu item, type the name of the top-level item exactly as it appears in your menu, for example Clothing. Capital letters and spaces around the name do not matter.
  4. Optionally add a promotion: an image, a heading, a description, a button label and a link.
  5. Optionally pick a featured Product, or a Collection (used only when no product is selected).

If the name does not match, the item shows as a normal dropdown. Add one Mega menu block for each top-level item that needs one.

Mobile menu

On small screens the menu opens in a drawer. Links with child links open a second panel with a Back button. The drawer also shows the optional promotion and the country and language selectors.

Tips

  • Keep top-level names short so they fit in one line.
  • Use a square or landscape promotion image of at least 800 px wide.
  • Check the header with the longest menu you plan to use at tablet width.

Chapter 4

Home page sections

Open Online Store, Themes, Customize, then choose Home page. Select Add section to see every section you can use. Most of them also work on other pages that support sections.

Every content section has these shared settings, so they are not repeated below:

  • Color scheme: the saved set of colors the section uses.
  • Top padding and Bottom padding: 0 uses the store's standard section spacing (set in Theme settings, Layout). Any other value overrides it in pixels.

The starting home page

The default home page contains these sections in order: Hero, Trust and service, Featured collections, Featured collection, Editorial story, Shop by category, Lookbook, Image with text, Promotional campaign, Testimonials, Brand values, Journal, Newsletter and Recently viewed. Remove what you do not need.

Recently viewed shows nothing until a visitor has opened a product. Leave it in place, it hides itself.

Promotional countdown

The Promotional campaign section can show a countdown. It counts down to the end date and time that you enter, in your store's time zone.

  • End date uses the format YYYY-MM-DD, for example 2026-12-31.
  • End time uses 24-hour time, for example 18:00. If you leave it empty, the countdown ends at midnight.
  • When the date has passed, the countdown disappears and never restarts. You can show a short text after the end date, or hide the whole section.
  • Leave the date empty to hide the countdown.

Because the countdown follows a real date, it is not an evergreen timer. Do not set fake dates.

Images

  • Always upload the largest sensible original. The theme creates smaller versions for each screen.
  • Use the focal point tool in the image picker so the important part stays in view when the image is cropped.
  • Hero: 2400 x 1600 px or larger for the desktop image and 1080 x 1350 px for the mobile image.
  • Add alt text to important images in your Shopify admin under Content, Files.

Page structure tips

  • Each page needs one main heading (h1). On the home page, the first section provides it when it is a Hero, Slideshow or Image banner. Other sections use h2 headings.
  • Alternate color schemes between neighboring sections to make a clear rhythm.
  • Aim for no more than one animated or moving section in view at a time.

Banner

Setting What it does
Show collection image Shows the image you set on the collection in your Shopify admin.
Banner layout Split (image beside the title) or Wide (full-width image).
Show collection description Shows the collection description.
Show product count Shows the number of products.

Set the collection image and description in Products, Collections.

Product grid

Setting What it does
Layout Classic (even grid), Editorial rhythm, Large image or Compact.
Feature product frequency With Editorial rhythm, every product at this position (3 to 15) becomes a double-width campaign product. The default is 7.
Products per row on desktop 2 to 5.
Products per row on mobile 1 or 2.
Products per page 8 to 48, in steps of 4.
Pagination Numbers, or a Load more button.

Product card appearance is set once for the whole store in Theme settings, Product cards:

  • Image ratio: Portrait, Tall portrait, Square or Landscape.
  • Image fit: Fill the frame, or Show the whole image.
  • Show second image on hover.
  • Show quick add button.
  • Show vendor.
  • Show color swatches and Show sizes on hover.
  • Badge position: top left, top right or bottom left.
  • Days a product counts as new: products created within this many days show a New badge. Use 0 to turn the badge off.

Product badges

A card shows up to two corner tabs. The theme picks them in this order:

  1. Sold out, when the product is not available.
  2. Your own text, from the product metafield custom.badge.
  3. Sale, when the compare-at price is higher than the price.
  4. New, for products created within the number of days you set.

To use your own badge text, add a product metafield with the namespace and key custom.badge, type Single line text, then fill it in on each product that needs it. Examples: Limited edition, Back in stock.

Color dots and size chips

If a product has an option named Color (or Colour), or a first option with swatches, the card shows color dots. If it has an option whose name contains Size, the card shows size chips on hover and on keyboard focus. Name your options that way if you want them to appear.

Quick add

On cards, the quick add tab adds a single-variant product straight to the cart. A product with options opens a quick view window to choose options. A product that requires a subscription plan links to its page instead.

Filtering and sorting

Filtering and sorting come from Shopify. The theme does not invent any filters. Turn them on or off with Enable filtering and Enable sorting.

Setting What it does
Enable filtering Shows the filters Shopify provides for the collection.
Filter layout on desktop Sidebar or Top.
Enable sorting Shows the sort menu.

On phones, filters open in a drawer. Filters apply as soon as a shopper changes them, and the product count is announced to screen readers. If JavaScript is off, filters work as a normal form with an Apply button.

Set up filters with the Search and Discovery app

  1. In your Shopify admin, open Apps and install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app.
  2. Open the app and choose Filters.
  3. Add the filters you want. Shopify offers availability, price, product type, vendor, product options (such as Color and Size), tags and metafields.
  4. Drag filters into the order you want. Save.

Changes can take a few minutes to appear on your store.

Tips:

  • Filters show as swatches or images when Search & Discovery presents them that way. Swatches come from your product options. See Product page and blocks.
  • Keep the filter list short. Four to six filters fit a fashion store well.
  • If the app has no filters for a collection, the filter area does not appear.

Search page

The search results page uses the same filters and sorting. Results can include products, articles and pages. Pages and articles show as separate result types. Turn article images on or off with "Show article images".

Empty collections and sold-out products

An empty collection shows a message and a link to all products. If filters match nothing, shoppers see a message and a Clear all button. Sold-out products stay in the grid with a Sold out badge.

Collection list page

The collection list page (Collections list section) shows every collection. You can sort them (alphabetical, by product count, or by date), set the image ratio, choose 1 or 2 per row on mobile, and show or hide the product count. It uses each collection's image.

Chapter 6

Product page and blocks

Open Online Store, Themes, Customize, then choose Products and select any product to preview. The product page is the Product information section followed by Related products.

Everything in the information column is a block. You can add, remove and reorder blocks, and each block is explained below. The default order is: Vendor, Heading, Rating, Price, Variant picker, Size guide, Quantity selector, Inventory status, Buy buttons, Pickup availability, Trust badges, Description, Bundle, SKU, Share and Sticky add to bag.

Gallery and layout

Settings on the Product information section:

Setting What it does
Show breadcrumbs Home, collection and product trail above the product.
Desktop layout Mosaic (one full-width image, then pairs) or Thumbnails (a main image with a thumbnail rail). On mobile the images are always a swipeable carousel with a counter.
Thumbnail position Left or Bottom (Thumbnails layout only).
Offset paired images Offsets the second image of each pair in the Mosaic layout.
Image ratio Natural, Portrait, Tall portrait or Square.
Image fit Fill the frame, or Show the whole image.
Open images in fullscreen with zoom Opens a fullscreen viewer.
Loop videos Loops hosted and YouTube or Vimeo videos.
Desktop media width 50, 58 or 66 percent of the page.
Sticky column on desktop Keeps the information column, the media column or neither in view while scrolling.

The gallery shows images, uploaded video, YouTube and Vimeo video, and 3D models. When a shopper picks a variant that has its own image, that image moves to the front.

Upload images of the same aspect ratio for the best result. Mixed ratios still work, but the Natural ratio setting will make the page height jump between products.

Product options and swatches

The Variant picker block shows one group of controls for each product option.

Setting What it does
Color options Swatch, Buttons or Dropdown. Applies to options named Color, Colour, Shade, Tone or Print, and to options that have swatches.
Other options Buttons or Dropdown. Used for Size and everything else.

Unavailable and sold-out values stay selectable so shoppers can see them. The Add to bag button is disabled or replaced for a combination that does not exist or is sold out.

Set up color swatches

  1. Name the option Color.
  2. In your Shopify admin, give each option value a color or an image using Shopify's swatch feature for product options. The exact steps change over time, so search the Shopify Help Center for "swatches".
  3. Save the product and check the product page.

If an option value has no swatch, the theme tries the last word of the value name as a color. "Navy blue" becomes blue. A name that is not a color name shows an empty ring with the name as its text label. Add real swatches for names such as Oatmeal or Espresso.

Price, quantity and buy buttons

  • Price: shows the price, the compare-at price with sale styling, and the unit price when the product has one. Options: Price size (regular or large), Show tax and shipping note, and Show Shop Pay Installments, which appears only when installments are turned on in your payment settings. A tax note appears when your store's prices include tax.
  • Quantity selector: minimum, maximum and step follow the quantity rules you set for each variant.
  • Buy buttons: Add to bag, plus accelerated checkout buttons (such as Shop Pay) when you leave Show dynamic checkout buttons on. Choose a solid or outline Add to bag style. Subscription products show their purchase options here.
  • Pickup availability: shows whether local pickup is available for the selected variant. It needs pickup turned on for at least one location in your shipping settings.

Gift cards

For a gift card product, the Buy buttons block can show a recipient form: recipient email, name, message and a send date. Turn it off with "Show recipient form for gift cards". Dynamic checkout buttons are hidden when the recipient form is on.

Size guide

Add the Size guide block. It opens a panel from a link next to the size options. The content comes from the first of these that exists:

  1. The product metafield custom.size_guide (type Page). This lets one product use a different guide from another.
  2. The Size guide page set in the block.
  3. The size chart typed into the block.

To set up the product metafield:

  1. In Settings, Custom data, Products, choose Add definition.
  2. Name it Size guide. Set the namespace and key to custom.size_guide.
  3. Choose the type Page.
  4. Create a page for each guide in Content, Pages. For example, Dresses size guide.
  5. On each product, pick the right page in the Size guide metafield.

To type a size chart in the block instead:

  • Write one row per line, with values separated by commas.
  • The first row is the heading row. The first column is the size.
  • Ranges such as 84-88 work.
  • Example:
Size,Bust,Waist,Hips
XS,80-84,62-66,88-92
S,84-88,66-70,92-96
  • Choose whether your numbers are in centimeters or inches. Shoppers can switch between the two, and the theme converts the measurement columns.
  • You can add measuring notes and a measuring image. The image is shown below the chart or page.

If the product has no guide at all, the block does not show on the live store. In the theme editor it shows a short reminder.

Bundles

The Bundle block ("Complete the set") shows the current product with up to four other products. Shoppers can tick which items to include, pick variants, see a live total and add the whole set in one step.

The theme does not create discounts. Savings appear only when the items have compare-at prices. To give a real bundle discount, create an automatic discount in Shopify, or use a bundles app. You can write a note under the total to say so.

Choose the products in one of two ways:

  • Per product: add a product metafield with the namespace and key custom.bundle_products, type List of products, and pick the products on each product. This overrides the block's list.
  • For all products: pick the Products in the block settings.

Without JavaScript the block shows plain links to each product.

Complete the look (complementary products)

The Complete the look block shows Shopify's complementary product recommendations, with a list of fallback products you choose in the block. Each item has an image, price, variant picker and an include checkbox, and one button adds the selected items.

To set up complementary products:

  1. Install Shopify's free Search & Discovery app.
  2. Open it, choose Recommendations, then Complementary products.
  3. Pick the product and the products that go with it.

Related products at the bottom of the page work without setup. Shopify chooses them from your orders and product data, and they improve over time.

Trust badges

The Trust badges block shows up to five badges with line icons or your own icon images.

Return window in days Used in the returns text. Match your refund policy.
Show description Shows or hides the short line under each badge heading.
Link shipping and returns badges to store policies Links to your policy pages when they exist.

Only show promises your store keeps. If you do not offer free returns for 30 days, change the number of days or pick a different set.

Other blocks

  • Heading, Vendor, SKU, Description (open or collapsible), Text.
  • Collapsible row: for materials and care, shipping or returns. It uses a native details element, so it works without JavaScript. Connect a product metafield to the Content setting to show different text on each product. Presets: Materials and care, Shipping, Returns.
  • Icon with text: a short point such as Fit, with an icon or your own icon image.
  • Shipping and returns: a delivery estimate and up to three lines with icons, plus links to your shipping and refund policies.
  • Guarantee seal: a round seal with your return window.
  • Inventory status: uses real inventory data only. It shows a sold out message, a backorder message, or a low stock message when tracked stock is at or below the threshold you choose. The exact number left shows only for inventory that Shopify tracks, and you can turn it off. An In stock message is optional.
  • Rating: shows a rating from the reviews.rating product metafield, which many reviews apps fill in. You can also add a reviews app block inside it. The theme never invents a rating.
  • Payment icons: the payment methods enabled in your store.
  • Share: Pinterest, Facebook and X links, and a copy link button.
  • Custom Liquid: for code from an app or developer.

Sticky add to bag

The Sticky add to bag block shows a bar at the bottom of the screen after the main buy buttons scroll out of view. It follows the variant chosen in the main form. Options: Show quantity selector, Show on (all screens or mobile only), and a color scheme. The bar is hidden while a drawer is open.

Reviews

What the drawer shows

  • Each line item with its image, linked title, options, selling plan and any line properties. Properties whose names start with an underscore are hidden.
  • The unit price when the product has one, the original and final price, and any line discounts.
  • A quantity stepper that follows the quantity rules you set for the variant, and a remove link.

  • The subtotal, cart-level discounts with their names and amounts, the estimated total, and a note about tax and shipping.

Discounts

The cart does not have a discount code field. Codes are entered at checkout. Automatic discounts and any code already applied appear in the cart summary. To run a promotion, create an automatic discount in Shopify.

The progress message compares the cart total with the number you entered. It shows only when the shopper's cart uses your store currency. If you sell in other currencies, shoppers in those currencies do not see the progress message, because the threshold is not converted. Check your shipping rates match the threshold you publish. Select the Cart page in the theme editor, then Cart:

  • Heading for the recommendations.
  • Collection for an empty bag: products shown when the cart is empty. Leave it empty to show all products.
  • Color scheme and padding.

With items in the cart, the recommendations come from Shopify's related product data for the cart's products.

Tips

  • Keep the free shipping threshold just above your average order value, and state the same amount in your shipping policy.
  • Do not use the cart note for required information such as gift messages. Use a product field or gift card recipient form for that.

Chapter 8

Gift card page

Sell gift cards

  1. Create a gift card product in your Shopify admin and add the amounts as variants. The exact steps are in the Shopify Help Center under "digital gift cards".
  2. Add the product to a collection and show it in your menu.
  3. On the product page, keep the Buy buttons block. For a gift card product it can show a recipient form (see Product page and blocks).

The recipient form

When "Show recipient form for gift cards" is on, buyers can send the gift card to someone else.

  • Recipient email (required to send to someone else).
  • Recipient name (optional).
  • Message (optional, up to 200 characters).
  • Send on (optional). Leave it empty to send right away. Buyers can schedule up to 90 days ahead.

If the form is turned off, Shopify sends the gift card to the buyer.

If Shopify rejects a form entry and the page reloads, the theme keeps what the buyer typed and shows Shopify's error message.

The gift card page

When a customer receives a gift card, they open a page that Shopify renders with the file templates/gift_card.liquid. This page is not edited in the theme editor sections list. It shows:

  • Your logo, or your store name when no logo is set in Theme settings.
  • The balance and the original value.
  • The gift card code, with a Copy code button.
  • The expiry date, when the gift card has one.
  • A QR code and a short instruction for using the code at checkout, or in person.
  • An Add to Apple Wallet button, when Shopify provides a pass for the card.
  • A Print button and a Start shopping button.
  • A message when the gift card has expired or is no longer active. The QR code is hidden in that case.

The page uses the first color scheme (Scheme 1) and your fonts. Change the logo in Theme settings, Logo and favicon.

The QR code is drawn in the browser with a small, MIT-licensed library included in the theme, and it uses Shopify's own gift card identifier. If the QR code does not show, the code and the Copy button still work.

Change the wording

The gift card page text comes from the theme's language file. To change it, open the theme editor, choose the language menu, then Edit default theme content, and search for "gift card".

Test it

Create a test gift card in Products, Gift cards, send it to your own email and open the link. Check the page on a phone, and print it once.

Selvage works with Shopify Markets and Shopify's language tools. It does not translate your content by itself.

Add countries and regions in Settings, Markets. When your store has more than one country or region, a selector appears. If it has only one, nothing is shown.

Chapter 10

Animation settings and reduced motion

Selvage uses motion sparingly: content fades or slides up as it enters the screen, images can reveal with a wipe, large images can drift slowly as you scroll, and pages fade into each other. All of it is optional.

Animation settings

Open Theme settings, then Animation.

Setting What it does
Animation intensity Off, Subtle, Standard or Expressive. Subtle uses shorter, smaller movements. Expressive uses longer, larger ones. Off turns movement off.
Reveal content on scroll Content fades up as it enters the screen.
Reveal images with a wipe Images uncover with a curtain effect.
Zoom images on hover A slight zoom on product and collection images when the pointer is over them.
Fade between pages A short cross-fade between pages, in browsers that support it.
Enable parallax on large images Large images move slightly slower than the page. Each section that supports it has its own "Enable parallax" checkbox too.

Content is visible without animation. If JavaScript does not run, nothing stays hidden.

Reduced motion

Visitors can ask their device to reduce motion: in system settings on Windows, macOS, iOS and Android, there is a setting called Reduce motion or Show animations. Selvage respects it everywhere, without any setting from you:

  • Scroll reveals, image reveals, page fades and parallax are switched off, and content shows at once.
  • Animations and transitions finish almost instantly.
  • The slideshow does not advance automatically, even if you turned autoplay on.
  • The announcement bar does not rotate messages.
  • The hero background video does not play. The image stays as the cover. The same happens when the visitor has asked for reduced data use.
  • Smooth scrolling is turned off.

You cannot turn these protections off, and you should not try to work around them with custom code.

Pausing moving content

Moving content that starts by itself must be pausable. In Selvage:

  • Slideshow autoplay is off by default. When you turn it on, it pauses on hover and keyboard focus and shows a pause button.
  • The announcement bar rotation pauses on hover and focus.
  • Countdowns show the end date as text as well as the ticking numbers. The ticking numbers are not read out every second.

Tips

  • Pick one moment of motion per section. A hero with a video, parallax, wipe reveal and animated heading is too much.
  • Avoid autoplay when you can. A still image with a clear heading often converts as well.
  • If your customers are older, or you sell to a wide audience, start with Subtle.
  • Test on a real phone. Motion that feels smooth on a computer can stutter on an older phone.

App compatibility

Selvage follows Shopify's Online Store 2.0 rules, so apps that use theme app extensions work without editing theme code.

App blocks

An app block is a block that an app provides. You add it in the theme editor like any other block.

Selvage accepts app blocks in:

  • The Product information section on the product page.
  • The Featured product section.
  • The Rating block, so a reviews app can sit inside it.

How to add one:

  1. Install the app and follow its setup steps.
  2. Open the theme editor and go to the template you want, for example Products.
  3. Select Add block in the section, then pick the app under Apps.
  4. Drag the block into place and save.

Apps often add their own blocks to the Bundle block's section too. They sit next to the Bundle block.

App embeds

An app embed runs across the whole page, for example a chat widget, a loyalty badge or a cookie banner. Turn it on under Theme settings, App embeds. App embeds do not depend on any theme section. Selvage does not change the page head that Shopify fills in, so app embeds and scripts load as the app expects.

Custom Liquid

Use the Custom liquid section, the Custom Liquid block on the product page, or the Custom Liquid block in the footer when an app asks you to paste a snippet.

  • Only paste code from sources you trust.
  • Duplicate your theme before you paste anything.
  • Scripts that you add yourself can slow down the store. See Performance tips.

You want How

Chapter 12

Accessibility notes

Selvage is built to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. This page lists what the theme does, what stays in your hands, and what has not been verified yet.

Status

Selvage 1.0.0 was checked with source-level scripts during development. These checks look for missing alt text, unlabeled fields, unnamed buttons, removed focus outlines and missing reduced-motion rules. They found no errors on the last run.

A full test on a live store with a screen reader, keyboard-only use, browser zoom and a contrast tool has not been completed. If you find a barrier, please tell us (see Support).

What the theme provides

  • A "Skip to content" link as the first focusable element.
  • Landmarks: header, main content, footer and navigation regions.
  • One main heading (h1) per page. Heading levels are visually distinct.
  • Keyboard use for menus, drawers, dialogs, carousels, the size guide and the quantity controls. Escape closes drawers and menus, and focus returns to the control that opened them.
  • Visible focus outlines on links, buttons and fields.
  • Drawers and dialogs use the native dialog element, so focus is kept inside while they are open.
  • Messages for things that change without a page load, such as adding to the cart or applying filters, are announced to screen readers.
  • Form fields have labels, error lists link to the fields that need attention, and forms work as plain HTML forms without JavaScript.
  • Images use alt text from your product and media settings. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Reduced motion is respected everywhere. See Animation settings and reduced motion.
  • Slideshows and rotating messages can be paused.
  • Color swatches have a text name for screen readers.
  • Primary controls are designed with large touch targets, and small controls are designed to keep at least 24 by 24 pixels of tappable area. This has not been measured on a rendered store.
  • The page language is set from the visitor's language.

What you need to do

  • Write alt text for every important image in Content, Files, and for product media. Describe what the picture shows, not "image of". Leave decorative images empty.
  • Choose color schemes with enough contrast: at least 4.5 to 1 for text, and 3 to 1 for large text, buttons and borders. Test text over images, especially with the transparent header.
  • Use descriptive link and button labels. "Shop dresses" is better than "Click here".
  • Keep heading order logical. Use heading levels to structure the page, not to change the size.
  • Do not put text inside images. Use the Heading and Body text settings, so the text can be read, translated and zoomed.
  • Add a description to every video, and use the "Slideshow description" setting.
  • Keep Animation intensity modest, and avoid autoplay.
  • Preview every page at 200 percent browser zoom and on a narrow phone.

Known limits

  • The error and success message colors in forms are fixed colors. They have good contrast on the light color schemes but not on the darkest ones. If you use a dark scheme for a section with a form, check the message colors.
  • Third-party app blocks and embeds have their own accessibility. Test them before you publish.
  • Right-to-left layouts have not been tested.
  • The QR code on the gift card page has a text alternative, and the gift card code is shown as text.

Chapter 13

Performance tips

How fast your store feels depends on your images, your apps and your page design as much as on the theme. This page lists what the theme does, and what you can do.

Selvage has not been measured with Lighthouse on a live store yet, so this guide gives no scores. Measure your own store with the online store speed report in your Shopify admin and with PageSpeed Insights.

What the theme does

  • Scripts are deferred or loaded as modules, so they do not block the page.
  • Scripts and styles for a feature load only on pages that use it. For example, the QR code script loads only on the gift card page, and the filter script only on collection and search pages.
  • Fonts come from Shopify's font service, with the heading and body fonts preloaded, and use a fallback font while they load.
  • Images use responsive sizes. Apart from the logo, only the first image in the first section is loaded eagerly and at high priority. All other images load as the visitor scrolls.
  • Image frames reserve their space, so the page does not jump while images load.
  • Animations use transforms and opacity wherever possible.
  • There is no jQuery and no third-party script requested from outside Shopify. The only bundled third-party code is a small QR code library used on the gift card page. The only outside embeds are YouTube and Vimeo videos that you choose, and they load after a visitor presses play.
  • The video section loads its player only after a visitor presses play.

What you can do

Images

  • Upload images at the size you need, not the largest file you have. A hero image of 2400 px wide is enough. Photos from a camera at 6000 px are not.
  • Use JPEG for photographs. Shopify serves modern formats to browsers that support them.
  • Put the most important image first. Make it the one that looks good before it finishes loading.
  • Use the focal point tool, so you do not need extra-wide crops.

Page design

  • Keep the home page to the sections you need. Ten to twelve sections is plenty.
  • Avoid more than one autoplay video on a page. Use a cover image and let visitors press play.
  • Use the Video section instead of embedding a video with code.
  • Set "Products to show" in Featured collection to what a visitor will really look at. Eight is a good start.
  • Turn on Load more instead of showing 48 products per page on collection pages, or lower Products per page.

Apps

  • Every app can add scripts and styles. Remove apps you no longer use, and turn off their app embeds.
  • Remove an app's leftover code snippets from Custom Liquid sections.
  • Add apps one at a time and measure your store after each one.

Fonts

  • Use two fonts at most: one for headings and one for body text. A third font for navigation adds a download.
  • Avoid very large font families with many weights.

Animation

  • Choose Subtle or Off in Theme settings, Animation if your store feels slow on older phones.

Test before you publish

  1. Open PageSpeed Insights for the home page, a collection page and a product page, on mobile.
  2. Fix large images first, then apps.
  3. Test again after every change.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Why does my product card not show color dots or size chips?

The theme looks for options named Color (or Colour) and Size. Rename your options in the product, and check Theme settings, Product cards that "Show color swatches" and "Show sizes on hover" are on.

Why are my color swatches empty?

An option value without a swatch uses the last word of its name as a color. Names such as Oatmeal do not work this way. Give each value a color or image swatch in your product options. See Product page and blocks.

Why do my collection filters not appear?

Filtering uses the filters you set up in Shopify's Search & Discovery app. Install it, add filters, and make sure Enable filtering is on in the Collection section. See Collection page and filters.

Why does the mega menu not show?

The Mega menu block's "Menu item" must match the name of a top-level item in the header menu. Check the spelling. See Header and mega menu.

Why does my size guide not show?

Add the Size guide block, then set one of: the product metafield custom.size_guide (type Page), a page in the block, or a size chart in the block. See Product page and blocks.

Why is the free shipping message missing from the cart?

The message needs a number in Theme settings, Cart, Free shipping threshold, and it shows only for carts in your store currency. See Cart drawer.

Why does the cart open as a page and not as a drawer?

Check Theme settings, Cart, Cart type. Also, the cart page always shows the full cart even when the type is Drawer.

Why is the Sale price not showing?

A product shows a sale when its compare-at price is higher than its price. Set the compare-at price on the variant.

Why does my new badge text not appear?

Check the product metafield custom.badge (single line text) is filled in. A product also shows Sold out and Sale badges first, and a card shows at most two badges.

How do I add product reviews?

Install a reviews app that supports app blocks and add its block in the Rating block. See App compatibility.

Does the theme have a wishlist or an Instagram feed?

No. Use an app if you need them. See App compatibility.

Animations bother me or my customers. How do I switch them off?

Set Theme settings, Animation, Animation intensity to Off. Visitors whose devices ask for reduced motion never see the movement. See Animation settings and reduced motion.

Does the theme work in other languages?

The theme includes English text. Use Shopify's language tools to translate everything else. See Localization.

My text is hard to read over an image. What can I do?

Raise the Overlay opacity in that section, or choose a Color scheme over image with stronger contrast.
