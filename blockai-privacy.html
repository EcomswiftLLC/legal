Privacy Policy — BlockAI

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: November 2026.

BlockAI hides AI-labeled posts and blurs suspected AI text, images and video on the websites you visit. It is built to keep your data on your device. We have no account system, no analytics and no tracking.

What happens on your device

  • Detection runs locally in your browser. The text, images and video on the pages you visit are analysed on your device and are not sent to us.
  • To check an image for AI-generator metadata (for example C2PA, EXIF or XMP tags), the extension may request the first and last few kilobytes of that image directly from the site that hosts it, the same way your browser loads the image. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Your settings, trusted and always-hide creators, your own domain rules, the scan cache, the daily free-limit count and your usage numbers (items hidden, blurred and left alone, pages scanned, sites where it helped) are stored locally in your browser. They are never sent to us.

Why the extension asks for its permissions

  • Access to all sites: needed to find and hide or blur AI content on any site you visit. A small script also runs on www.ecomswiftllc.com, only after you press a Pro plan button, to read the email you type at checkout so Pro can switch on automatically.
  • storage: to keep your settings, counters and license status on your device.
  • contextMenus: the right-click options to mark a post as human or AI, or to trust or always hide a creator.
  • alarms: to refresh your optional domain list daily, re-check a Pro plan daily, check whether your performance report is due, and, for up to 30 minutes after you start a purchase, check whether the payment went through.
  • offscreen: to run the optional on-device AI model without slowing the page.
  • activeTab: so the toolbar popup can show the counters for the tab you are looking at.

Your performance report

About 48 hours after you install, and then about once a week, Free users see a report page inside the extension. It shows numbers measured on your device and an option to upgrade. It contains no third-party content, and nothing about you is sent anywhere to build it. You can switch it off in Settings. Pro members do not see it.

The Review link

The “Review extension” link only opens this extension's page on the Chrome Web Store. Nothing is offered in exchange for a review.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or transfer your browsing history, page content or personal data.
  • We do not use data for advertising, for creditworthiness or lending, or for anything unrelated to detecting and hiding AI content.
  • There are no third-party analytics or trackers.

The only network calls the extension makes

  • Image checks: described above, directly to the site hosting the image.
  • Optional domain list (off until you add a link): if you enter a link to your own list of sites, the extension downloads that list about once a day. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Optional on-device AI model (Pro, off by default): if you turn it on, the model files are downloaded once from huggingface.co and then run on your device. Your content is not sent anywhere for this.
  • Optional detection API (Pro, off by default): if you enter your own API key and agree, text or image links the extension is unsure about are sent to the detection service you configured, so it can give a second opinion. That service handles the data under its own terms. Nothing is sent to us.
  • Pro plan (optional): if you buy Pro, checkout happens on our website (ecomswiftllc.com) and you may check out as a guest. The extension can read the email you typed at checkout so it can unlock Pro for you. That email, with the product name, is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification. We do not sell or share it, and the Free features never send it. Payments are handled by Wix Payments; the extension never sees card details.

Accuracy

No tool can prove who wrote something. BlockAI estimates from patterns and can be wrong; when in doubt it keeps content visible.
