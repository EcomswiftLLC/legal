Abvolt Six Pack Abs Workout: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Abvolt (Six Pack Abs Workout) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your workouts, measurements and progress photos are stored on your phone and never leave it. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Abvolt keeps on your phone

  • Your answers and training records: the goals and minutes per day you choose, your level test result, workout history, program progress, plank holds and personal bests, daily challenge days, water log, waist measurements, badges, custom workouts and settings. They are stored in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Optional body details: if you enter a waist measurement, it is used only on your phone, for your charts and weekly report.
  • Progress photos: photos you take with your camera app or pick with Android's Photo Picker are copied into Abvolt's private storage. They are not added to your gallery, are never uploaded, and are excluded from Android's cloud backup and from Abvolt's own backup file. You can lock them with a PIN (only a scrambled, salted fingerprint of the PIN is stored) and delete them at any time.
  • Backups: if you choose Export a backup, Abvolt writes a file to the place you pick. It contains your records and settings but never your photos. What you do with that file is up to you.
  • Deleting: Settings, Erase all my data, deletes every record and photo on your phone. Uninstalling the app also removes everything.

Health information

Abvolt is a general fitness app, not a medical device. Fitness information you record (workouts, holds, measurements and photos) stays on your device and is not shared with us or with advertisers. Abvolt does not connect to Health Connect or Google Fit.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you turn on a reminder, and used for the workout, streak, missed-day and water reminders you choose, and for the controls of a running workout. Reminders are scheduled on your phone.
  • Foreground service: while a workout you started is running, Abvolt keeps a notification so the timer and voice coach keep working with the screen off. It reads no sensors and records no location.
  • Camera: Abvolt does not ask for camera permission. When you tap Take photo, your own camera app takes the picture and hands it to Abvolt.
  • Abvolt asks for no location, contacts, microphone or storage permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Abvolt is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Banners appear on the Today, Progress and Settings screens only. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished workout, limited in number and spacing. Ads never appear during a workout, a rest, a plank hold or while you enter health information. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to unlock one Premium workout, one photo comparison, one weekly report or one extra custom workout.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After a finished workout, a new plank personal best or a 7-day streak, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Abvolt has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Abvolt is intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
