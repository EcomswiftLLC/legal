Widgets: Home Screen & Icons | Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Widgets: Home Screen & Icons ("the app") is made by Ecom Swift LLC. The app works on your device. It has no account or sign-in, and we do not operate any server that receives your widgets, photos, notes or calendar events. We never upload, collect, sell or share that content.

What the app accesses, and why

  • Calendar (only for the Calendar widget): if you add a Calendar widget and allow calendar access, the app reads your upcoming events (title, time, color and location) from your phone's calendar so it can draw them in the widget. Events are read on your device only, are kept only as a small cache on your device for the widget, and are never sent anywhere. If you don't allow access, the widget simply shows a reminder instead of events. You can turn access off at any time in Android settings.
  • Photos you choose (Photo widget): Android's photo picker lets you choose specific images. Only the images you pick are read, and a resized copy is stored inside the app on your device to display in the widget. The app does not request access to your photo library and never uploads your photos.
  • Notes and countdowns: the text you type for Quick note widgets and the dates and titles of your countdowns are stored only on your device.
  • Battery: the Battery widget reads your battery level, charging state and temperature from Android. This stays on your device.
  • Installed apps (icon shortcuts): to create a custom icon shortcut, the app lists the apps on your phone that have a launcher icon so you can choose which one the shortcut opens. This list is used on your device only and is not sent anywhere.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not upload, back up, read or share your calendar events, photos, notes or widget designs.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require an account or login.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and similar device information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud; this data is sent to Google encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later. Premium turns off ads and stops ad requests. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Widgets Premium is a subscription handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. You can cancel anytime in Google Play.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your widget designs, notes, countdowns, chosen photos (resized copies), a cache of upcoming calendar events for the Calendar widget and your preferences only on your device. Removing a widget deletes its design, and uninstalling the app removes everything.

Children

The app is a general-purpose personalization tool and is not directed at children.
