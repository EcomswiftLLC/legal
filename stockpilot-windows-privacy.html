Privacy policy

Inventory & Barcode Manager: StockPilot for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 9 November 2026.

In short

  • StockPilot works on your PC. Your products, photos, suppliers, stock movements, purchase orders, counts and settings are stored only on your PC, in a local database in your Windows user profile. Backups are files you choose where to save.
  • StockPilot sends nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not upload your data anywhere.

Camera

If you choose Start camera on the Scan page, the video is read on your PC to find barcodes. Video is never recorded, saved or sent. You can turn camera access off in Windows Settings at any time.

Microsoft Store

Purchases, subscriptions and licence checks for Premium are handled by the Microsoft Store under Microsoft's privacy statement. StockPilot only receives whether Premium is active and the displayed prices.

Children

StockPilot is a business tool and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
