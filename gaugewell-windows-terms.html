What Gaugewell is and is not

Gaugewell is a PC maintenance and health app. It reads information about your PC, gives it a health score, explains each finding with the evidence behind it, and can remove temporary files that you have previewed and approved. A score is a guide and not a guarantee of how your PC will perform. Gaugewell is not an antivirus, a registry cleaner or a driver updater, and it does not replace Windows Security, Windows Update or a backup of your files.

Changes to your PC

Gaugewell removes files only after you have seen the exact list and approved it. Files from your own folders go to a quarantine for 30 days so that you can restore them; items in system locations are removed as Windows allows. A few optional actions, such as clearing the Windows Update cache, ask for administrator approval through the normal Windows prompt. You are responsible for your files and for the decision to clean or restore. Results can be affected by your organization's policies or by other software.
