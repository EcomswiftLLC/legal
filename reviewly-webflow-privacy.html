Privacy Policy - Reviewly: Photo, UGC Reviews

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Reviewly: Photo, UGC Reviews is a product reviews service published by Ecom Swift LLC. A site owner ("the merchant") installs the Reviewly app on their Webflow site. The app adds a small loader script to the site through the Webflow Custom Code API (or the merchant pastes one line of code by hand). Shoppers on that site can then read and write reviews of products, CMS items and pages, with photos and short videos. The merchant can also let the app read their Webflow Ecommerce orders so the service can mark real buyers. This policy explains what data the service handles, where it lives and why.

Short version. We store the merchant's account details, the reviews shoppers write (including any photos or short videos they add) and, if the merchant installs the Webflow app, the buyers found in their orders. We use no cookies, no advertising and no analytics trackers, and we never sell data. A shopper's IP address is turned into a salted one-way code that is used only to limit spam. The merchant decides what happens to the reviews on their store.

1. Who we are

The service is published by Ecom Swift LLC. Contact us at webflow@ecomswiftllc.com. For the review content on a store, the merchant is the data controller and Ecom Swift LLC acts as their processor.

3. Cookies and browser storage

We set no cookies. The review widget may keep a short-lived copy of review data in the shopper's browser session storage so a page does not reload the same data repeatedly. It is cleared when the browser tab is closed.

4. Where data lives and who sees it

Data is stored with Cloudflare, Inc. (Workers and D1) on its global network. The merchant can see all reviews on their store, including shopper emails. Reviews are shown publicly on the merchant's store only after the merchant's moderation rules allow it. We do not share data with advertisers or data brokers and we do not sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

Reviews are kept until the merchant deletes them or deletes their account. Deleting an account in the dashboard permanently removes the account and all of its reviews. Salted IP hashes, photos and videos are removed together with the review they belong to. If the merchant disconnects Webflow in the dashboard, or uninstalls the app so that Webflow revokes its access, the saved access token and the buyer list are deleted; reviews stay. Disconnecting in the dashboard also removes the Reviewly loader script from the Webflow site (the merchant publishes the site to take it off the live pages) and revokes the token at Webflow.

6. Your choices and rights

Merchants can export every review as CSV or JSON, edit or delete any review, disconnect Webflow and delete their saved order data, and delete their whole account at any time. A shopper who wants a review changed or removed should contact the store that published it, or write to us at the address above and we will help route or act on the request. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data. We honor such requests.

7. Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Admin keys are stored only as one-way hashes. Review text is displayed as plain text so it cannot run code on a store, and uploaded photos and videos are checked by their file contents and served only as images or video. We limit submissions per network and filter obvious spam, but no system is perfectly secure.

8. Children

The service is for store owners and their adult customers and is not directed at children under 13.

9. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Material changes will also be announced in the dashboard.
