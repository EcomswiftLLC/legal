Mamaloom Mom Planner: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Mamaloom (Mom Planner & Self Care) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your planner, journal, check-ins, family details and memory photos are stored on your phone and never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Mamaloom stores on your phone

  • Family: the names you add for yourself, a partner, children and helpers, and a child's age if you enter one. All optional.
  • Planner: tasks, calendar events (with times, places, notes and reminders), routines, checklists and packing lists, the grocery list, meal plans and recipe notes, chores and stars, budget envelopes and spending notes, brain-dump lines and weekly reviews.
  • Wellbeing: mood check-ins, feeling tags, energy, sleep, water, journal and gratitude entries, and the guided resets you finish.
  • Memories: moments, first words, favourites and the photos you choose. A photo you pick is copied, made smaller, into the app's private storage. It is not added to your gallery and is never uploaded.
  • Settings: reminders, theme, your country for support lines, and similar choices. If you turn on the journal lock, only a protected fingerprint of your PIN (a salted hash) is stored, never the digits.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Mamaloom excludes its data from Android cloud backup and device transfer, so it stays on this phone unless you make a backup file yourself. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and Settings > Delete all data removes it at any time.

Children's information

Mamaloom is made for parents and caregivers, not for children. Information about your children (names, ages, events, moments and photos) is entered by you, stays on your device, is never uploaded to us and is never used for advertising. Nothing about your family is shared with the ad service.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: making a backup file, exporting your journal (PDF or text), exporting or sharing calendar files (.ics), sharing a week, a list or a recap card, or sending a shared list to someone. In each case Android's share sheet or file picker lets you choose where the file goes, and that place or app then handles it under its own privacy policy. "Call" and "Text" on the support card open your phone or messaging app with the number filled in; Mamaloom never contacts anyone for you.

Permissions

  • Notifications: asked only when you switch on a reminder, for the reminders you set (tasks, events, routines, check-ins, the weekly reset). You can decline.
  • Fingerprint or face: used only if you turn on biometric unlock for the journal. The check is done by Android; Mamaloom never receives your fingerprint or face data.
  • Photos: chosen through Android's photo picker, one at a time, so no storage or camera permission is needed.

Mamaloom asks for no contacts, location, microphone, camera or storage permission. The optional voice in guided resets uses your phone's own text-to-speech engine. The app uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Mamaloom is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your planner, journal, check-ins, family details and photos are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on browse screens such as the planner and self-care hubs, lists, memories, learn and settings, never during a guided reset, in the journal, in a check-in or in an editor. A full-screen ad may appear after a finished weekly reset, export or article, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to use a Premium feature once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a good moment, for example after a finished weekly reset or a week of good days, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Mamaloom has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Wellbeing information

Mamaloom is a planner and wellbeing tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition, and its articles are general information. Support line numbers are taken from the services' public pages and may change.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
