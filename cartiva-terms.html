Sending is subject to your Wix Email Marketing allowance, your plan and Wix's limits and rules. If your allowance is used up, reminders wait or fail until Wix accepts them again. Reminders are timed to within about 5 minutes of their due time, and can be later if the scheduling service or Wix is unavailable, if quiet hours or sending limits apply, or if your Wix allowance is exhausted. If the scheduling service cannot be reached, reminders go out when you open the dashboard or when your store has other activity.
