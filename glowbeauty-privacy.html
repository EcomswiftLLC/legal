Glow Beauty Photo Editor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Glow is a beauty and portrait photo editor made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos are retouched on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your pictures, we don't build profiles of faces, and we don't sell personal information.

Your photos

  • You choose what Glow sees: the app opens only the photos you pick with Android's photo picker, take with your camera app, or send to it from another app. It does not browse your gallery.
  • Everything happens on your device: skin smoothing, blemish removal, face reshape, eye and teeth touches, background blur, light, looks, text, stickers and frames are processed locally.
  • On-device face and person models: to find faces and their outlines, Glow uses Google ML Kit face detection; for background blur it uses ML Kit selfie segmentation. Both models ship inside the app and run on your phone. The picture is not sent anywhere for them, and the face points they find are used only for the edit you are making. They are kept in memory while the photo is open and are discarded when you close it. Glow does not identify people and does not store face data.
  • What gets saved: when you tap Save, the finished picture is written to Pictures/Glow on your phone. If you tap Share, it is handed to the app you pick. Glow itself keeps only a short list of your recent saves so the home screen can show them, plus your settings (theme, export format and quality). Uninstalling the app removes them.

Camera and gallery

Glow does not ask for the camera permission. When you tap Camera, your phone's own camera app takes the picture and hands it to Glow. Photos are opened through Android's photo picker, which needs no storage permission. On Android 9 and older Glow asks for permission to save to your Pictures folder. Glow uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Glow is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or have removed ads. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home and save screens, never while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a photo, and it is limited in number. A rewarded video appears only when you choose to watch one to try the PRO tools for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's own privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium subscriptions, the lifetime purchase and the one-time ad removal are sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play of what you own, so it can unlock Premium on your devices.

Ratings

After you have saved a few photos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens; the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service, Google Play Billing and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, purchase history, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Your photos and face data are never part of it.

Children

Glow is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
