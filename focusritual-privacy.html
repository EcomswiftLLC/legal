Focus Ritual | Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Focus Ritual ("the app") is a focus timer and task planner made by Ecom Swift LLC. The app works on your device. It has no account or sign-in, and we do not operate any server that receives your sessions, tasks or notes. We never upload, collect, sell or share that content.

What the app stores, and where

  • Focus sessions, tasks, goals, statistics and settings: stored only on your device. You can export a backup file or import one using Android's file picker; the file goes only where you save it. Uninstalling the app removes everything.
  • Notifications: the app asks for notification permission only to tell you when a session or break ends. You can decline and the timer still works.
  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback when a session ends.
  • Ambient sounds: generated on your phone. No audio is downloaded or recorded, and the app never uses your microphone.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not upload, back up, read or share your sessions, tasks or notes.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and similar device information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud; this data is sent to Google encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required, you are asked for consent through Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your choice later in Settings. Focus Ritual Pro turns off ads and stops ad requests. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Focus Ritual Pro is an auto-renewing subscription of $3.99 per month handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. You can cancel anytime in Google Play, and the free version remains available.

Children

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
