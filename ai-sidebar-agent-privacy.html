Privacy Policy — AI Sidebar Agent

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: September 2026.

AI Sidebar Agent ("the extension") is an in-browser AI assistant. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you run a tool, the extension reads the text you selected or the current page's text, and can read the page's images and links, in your browser, to perform the action you asked for.
  • The default model runs on your device (Chrome's built-in AI). When you use it, your text never leaves your computer.
  • Your API keys, settings, and history are stored locally in your browser. They are not sent to us.

When you use a cloud model

If you choose a cloud model (for example by adding your own Google Gemini, Groq, OpenAI/ChatGPT, Anthropic/Claude, or xAI/Grok key), the text you ask the tool to process is sent directly from your browser to that provider's API using your key, so it can generate a response. That request goes to the provider you selected, not to us, and is subject to that provider's privacy policy. We do not run a server and never receive that text.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your identity, browsing history, passwords, or autofill data.
  • We do not sell or share your data. There are no ads.
  • We do not read pages you have not asked the extension to act on.
