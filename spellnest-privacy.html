Kids Spelling Games: Spellnest: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

Spellnest (Kids Spelling Games: Spellnest) is a spelling game for children aged 3 to 8, made by Ecom Swift LLC. It is designed for children and follows the Google Play Families Policy. Spellnest has no account and no sign-in, and it does not ask a child for a name, birthday, photo, voice or location. We do not run a server that receives anything from the app, and we do not sell personal information.

Kids section: what children can and cannot do in the app

  • Children play spelling games, collect stars and stickers, and choose a bird friend and a nickname from a fixed list. They never type a name or any other free text.
  • There is no chat, no sharing, no social features, no user-generated content and no push notifications.
  • Purchases, the rewarded video offer, settings, links that leave the app, the rating request and this policy are behind a grown-up check (a multiplication question, with a new question each time).

What Spellnest stores on the device

  • Progress: stars per level, stickers earned, the chosen bird friend and nickname, a list of recently practised words and today's play time.
  • Grown-up settings: daily play time limit, sound, music, reduce motion, British spelling, capital letters, theme, the weekly word list and any words a grown-up adds.

This stays in the app's private storage on the device. Uninstalling the app removes it, and Reset progress in the grown-up corner clears progress at any time. We cannot see or recover it.

Voice and sounds

All words, sentences and prompts are recorded audio files built into the app, so the voice works offline. If a grown-up adds their own words, those are read by the phone's own text-to-speech engine on the device. The app never uses the microphone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: only for ads and Google Play purchases. The games work fully offline.
  • Google Play billing: for the one-time purchases.

Spellnest does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts or storage, and the advertising ID permission is removed from the app.

Advertising

If no purchase has been made, Spellnest shows ads from Google AdMob, which takes part in the Google Play Families Ads Program. Every ad request is marked as directed to children and as from a user under the age of consent, and the maximum ad content rating is G (suitable for all audiences). Under these settings Google does not use personalised advertising, interest-based advertising or remarketing, and limits the data it processes to what Google describes for child-directed requests, such as device and app information needed to show and count ads, and diagnostics. The app does not send any progress or game data to the ad service.

Where ads appear: a banner on the home tree and in the grown-up corner, and occasionally a full-screen ad at a natural break after a finished round. Ads never appear during a game, never in the first three minutes of play, at most three full-screen ads per session and at least 150 seconds apart. A rewarded video is offered only in the grown-up corner, behind the grown-up check, to unlock a bonus sticker book for 24 hours. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy. More about how Google treats child-directed requests.

Purchases

Premium (lifetime) and Remove ads are one-time purchases made through Google Play Billing by a grown-up, behind the grown-up check. Google handles the payment; we never see card or payment details. The app only learns which products the Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

The rating request is shown only in the grown-up corner, never to a child during play. If a grown-up chooses to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens.

Data sent to Google services

Google Play services and the ad service may process device information, app interactions and diagnostics as described in Google's policies for child-directed traffic. We do not receive this data about anyone as an individual. Spellnest has no analytics or crash reporting of its own.

Parents' rights

Because Spellnest does not collect personal information from children, there is nothing for us to review or delete on a server. All data on the device can be removed by uninstalling the app or with Reset progress. If you have any question about your child's privacy, write to us and we will answer.

Changes to this policy
