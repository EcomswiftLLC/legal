Privacy Policy: Recurring Subscription Manager (Cadence)

Effective date: October 23, 2026 (2026-10-23)

Recurring Subscription Manager (Cadence) is a free Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that gives a Wix site owner one place to see and manage recurring payments sold through Wix Stores, Wix Pricing Plans and Wix Bookings, and gives subscribers a My Subscriptions page on the site. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives, what leaves your Wix site and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs the app (the "merchant") is the controller of their customers' data and decides how it is used. Ecom Swift LLC provides the software and acts as a processor on the merchant's instructions. Cadence keeps its data on the merchant's own Wix site. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive a copy of the merchant's customer data. The only data we hold ourselves is the small technical record of the scheduling relay described in section 5, for which we are the controller.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Ecom Swift LLC. You can reach us at support@ecomswiftllc.com.

2. What Cadence stores on your site

Cadence has no customer database of its own. It stores its working data in data collections (Wix Data) created for the app on the merchant's own Wix site. Each site has its own collections, so one merchant's data is never mixed with another's. Wix remains the source of truth for subscriptions, orders, plans, products, contacts and members. Cadence reads that information from Wix as it needs it and keeps the following records.

3. What the storefront parts do

The My Subscriptions widget lets a signed-in site member see and manage their own subscriptions, within the actions the merchant allows. The Subscribe and save plugin and the plan picker show the merchant's subscription options and plans to visitors. Cadence identifies a member through Wix's own member sign-in; it does not create its own accounts or log-ins and sets no cookies of its own. In the merchant's dashboard, Cadence may keep a few small non-personal preferences in the browser (for example when the review reminder was last shown or whether a tip was dismissed).

4. Emails

Cadence emails the merchant's own customers (welcome, renewal reminder, payment failed, paused, resumed, canceled, and an optional win-back) and, if the merchant turns it on, the merchant. Emails are sent through Wix (Wix Email Transmissions) from Wix's shared sending address, with the site name as the sender name and, if set, the merchant's address as the reply-to address. Ecom Swift LLC does not operate a mail server and does not see the emails. Each send counts toward the site's Wix email allowance.

Lifecycle emails about a subscription the customer holds are sent as transactional email. The win-back email is marketing email: it is off by default, it is sent only to contacts whose Wix email marketing status is subscribed, and Wix checks consent again and adds an unsubscribe link. If the merchant enters a notification address, Cadence emails that address directly, so Wix may list it as a contact on the site. The merchant is responsible for having a lawful basis to email their customers and for the wording they put in the templates.

5. What leaves your Wix site: the scheduling relay (Cloudflare)

Wix gives apps no built-in timer, but Cadence has timed work such as renewal reminders, resuming a paused subscription when a pause offer ends, daily summaries and clean-up. We therefore run a small scheduling relay on Cloudflare Workers, with a Cloudflare D1 database. Every 15 minutes it wakes the Cadence backend of each installed site, which then does its work inside the site. No customer data is sent to the relay or back from it; the reply to a wake-up contains only counts and a status.

For each site that has Cadence installed, the relay stores exactly this:

  • the Cadence app instance id of the site (a random identifier that Wix assigns to the installation),
  • the web address of Cadence's own backend that serves the site,
  • when the site was first registered and last updated,
  • when the relay last contacted the site, when that last succeeded and a short result code (for example "ok"),
  • a count of consecutive failed attempts, whether contact is paused, and when the next attempt is due.

The relay also stores the time of its own last scheduled run, and a short-lived counter used to limit abuse. That counter is keyed by a one-way hash of the caller's network address combined with the current minute, so the address itself is not stored; each counter expires after two minutes and is removed by the next scheduled clean-up, which runs at most every 15 minutes.

The relay does not store, and never asks for, information about a site's customers, subscribers, orders, products, emails or payments. It does not store access tokens: it asks Wix for a short-lived token for each wake-up and uses it from memory. It holds Cadence's app secret as a protected Worker secret, which is not site data. To confirm that a registration really comes from a site that has Cadence installed, the relay checks it with Wix and may receive a dashboard access token for that single check, which it does not keep.

The relay's logs hold event names, result codes and a short one-way hash of the instance id, and the registered backend address when a site registers. They contain no customer data. Cloudflare may also process technical connection data, such as network addresses, as part of delivering requests to the relay, under its own terms and retention.

The relay entry for a site is deleted when Cadence is removed from the site. If a site stops answering, the relay pauses it after repeated failures, retries it daily and deletes the entry 30 days after the last successful contact. If the relay is offline, timed tasks such as reminders can be late or may not run.

6. Service providers and sharing

  • Wix hosts and runs the app, stores its data on the merchant's site, provides the subscription, order, plan, product, contact and member data, sends the emails and identifies the site owner.
  • Cloudflare, Inc. runs the scheduling relay described in section 5. It is our only sub-processor for data we hold.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for advertising. Cadence contains no advertising, no third-party analytics and no AI model provider, and it does not profile anyone. The "More apps by Ecom Swift LLC" section in the dashboard only links to our other App Market listings. We disclose data only where the law requires it.

7. Purposes and legal basis

We process data only to provide the app to the merchant: listing and managing subscriptions, applying the changes the merchant requests, reporting, running the portal and cancel flow, sending the merchant's lifecycle emails and running timed tasks. For the merchant's customers' data, the merchant is the controller and determines the legal basis (for example performance of the subscription contract, legitimate interests or consent). For the technical relay record in section 5, our legal basis is our legitimate interest in running a reliable scheduling service.

Data that Wix itself holds (subscriptions, orders, contacts, members, sent emails) is governed by Wix and the merchant's Wix account, not by this app, and removing Cadence does not delete it.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every dashboard request is checked against a verified Wix identity, every request from a site member is checked against the signed-in member, and the scheduler endpoints accept only the Cadence app itself. The scheduling relay verifies registrations with Wix, calls only allow-listed Cadence addresses and does not follow redirects with a token. Stored data is limited to what the app needs. No system is perfectly secure, and the data on the merchant's site is also protected by the merchant's own Wix account security.

10. International transfers

Wix and Cloudflare operate globally, so data may be processed in countries other than where the merchant or the customer lives. Those providers apply their own transfer safeguards. We do not transfer personal data anywhere else.

11. Your rights

Because the merchant is the controller of their customers' data, a customer who wants to access, correct, delete or object to the use of their data, or to stop receiving emails, should contact the store first. Merchants can see and edit what Cadence holds for their site in the app and in the site's data collections. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA and similar state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to or receive a copy of your personal data, and to complain to a data protection authority. To exercise a right, write to support@ecomswiftllc.com. For data held by a merchant we will pass the request to the merchant and help them fulfil it; for the relay record in section 5 we will answer directly. We respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA, and we will not discriminate against anyone for exercising a right.

12. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age). We do not knowingly process children's data for our own purposes.
