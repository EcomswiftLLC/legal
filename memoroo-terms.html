Memory Games for Kids: Memoroo, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 23 November 2026.

For families

Memoroo is a set of memory games for children aged 3 to 8. A parent or guardian should install the app and decide on purchases and play time. By using the app you agree to these terms and to the privacy policy.

Free game and Premium

The free game includes 72 of the 150 levels and shows child-directed ads between rounds. Two optional one-time purchases are offered to grown-ups behind a parent check: Remove ads (removes all ads) and Premium (opens all 150 levels, removes all ads and adds two sticker books). There are no subscriptions. Prices are shown by Google Play in your currency before you confirm. Purchases are tied to the Google account and can be restored from the Grown-ups corner. Refunds follow Google Play's refund policy.

Bonus sticker pack

In the free game a grown-up may choose to watch a video in the Grown-ups corner to open the Party sticker pack for 24 hours. Stickers already placed on the meadow stay there.
