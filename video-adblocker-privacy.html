Privacy Policy — Video AdBlocker

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: November 2026.

Video AdBlocker blocks ads, video ads, pop-ups, trackers and known-malware requests on the websites you visit. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • Blocking happens locally in your browser using Chrome's declarativeNetRequest engine and bundled filter rules. We do not see the pages you visit.
  • On video sites, the extension's page scripts look at the video player on the page to skip, mute or fast-forward an ad. This happens locally. No page content, video title or address is sent to us.
  • Your settings, your per-site pause list, your own filter rules and your statistics (ads blocked, video ads skipped, seconds saved, per day) are stored locally on your device. They are never sent to us.

Why the extension asks for its permissions

  • Access to all sites: needed to block ads and skip video ads on any site you visit.
  • declarativeNetRequest (and feedback): to block requests and to count what was blocked so the report is accurate.
  • storage: to keep your settings and statistics on your device.
  • scripting: to turn the YouTube player filter on only while Pro is active.
  • downloads: Pro "Risky downloads" cancels downloads from known malware hosts.
  • notifications: to tell you when Pro has been unlocked.
  • alarms: to refresh the malware list daily, re-check Pro, and show your report page about every 48 hours on the Free plan.

Your report page

On the Free plan the extension opens its own report page about every 48 hours. It shows numbers measured on your device and an option to upgrade. It is a page inside the extension, it contains no third-party content, and nothing about you is sent anywhere to build it. Paying customers do not see it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell or transfer your browsing history, page content or personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or mine cryptocurrency. There are no third-party analytics.

The only network calls the extension makes

  • Malware host list: about once a day the extension downloads a public list of known-malware hosts (from urlhaus.abuse.ch) so it can block them. This is a download only; no information about you is sent.
  • Pro subscription (optional): if you buy Pro, checkout happens on our website (ecomswiftllc.com) and you may check out as a guest. When you open that site from the extension, the extension can read the email you typed at checkout so it can unlock Pro for you. That email is sent to our license server (operated by Ecom Swift LLC on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification. We do not sell or share it, and the Free features never send it. Payments are handled by Wix Payments; the extension never sees card details.
