Privacy Policy — Favicon & Touch-Icon Checker

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Favicon & Touch-Icon Checker lists the icons a web page declares (favicons, apple-touch-icons, the mask-icon, theme-color and the web-app-manifest icons) and runs a best-practice checklist. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup on a page, the extension reads that page's <link> and <meta> icon tags and, if the page links a web-app manifest, fetches that manifest (from the page's own site) to read its icons list. This happens in your browser and only for the tab you are on.
  • The checklist and the icon list are built entirely on your device. Copy, the generated snippet, and the CSV export are produced locally.
  • The optional Pro "Resolve sizes" feature loads each declared icon URL as an image in your own browser to read its real pixel dimensions (and to flag an icon that fails to load). Those requests go to the icon's own address, not to us, and we never receive the results.
  • Nothing you view or generate is sent to us or any third party.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — only to read the current page's declared icons and (if present) its linked manifest when you open the popup.
  • storage — to save your settings locally.
  • alarms — only for the optional reminder below.
  • host access (ecomswiftllc.com) — only to verify a Pro purchase (license check). No other hosts.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, the pages you check, their content, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
