Street Racing Game: Kinetra: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Street Racing Game: Kinetra ("Kinetra") is a single-player racing game made by Ecom Swift LLC. You do not need an account to play and the whole game works offline. We do not run a server that collects your game data, and we do not sell personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Progress and settings: your driver name, tokens, level, vehicles, upgrades, paint and wheel choices, career stars, best lap times, awards, daily-reward days and your control, audio and graphics settings are stored only in the game's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or choosing Settings, Erase career progress, removes them.
  • Photos: if you use Photo mode and tap Save photo, the picture is saved to your phone's Pictures/Kinetra folder (on Android 9 and older it is kept in the game's own storage instead). The game does not read your other photos and does not ask for storage permission.

Advertising

The free version may show ads from Google AdMob: a banner in menus and on the race result screen, a full-screen ad between races (never during a race), and optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch to double your race tokens, collect free tokens or try a locked car in one race. Owners of Remove Ads see no ads.

To show and measure ads, Google AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device and app information such as device model, operating system, language, app version, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions) a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears first, and ads are requested only if you allow it. You can change your choice at any time in Settings, Ad privacy choices (shown where required). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Remove Ads, the Pearl Paint Collection, the Hypercar Pack, the Weather and Atmosphere Pack, the Kinetra Premium bundle and single vehicles are one-time purchases handled by Google Play Billing. Tokens are earned only by playing and are stored on your device; they cannot be bought. We receive only confirmation from Google Play that a purchase is active, so the game can unlock it. We never see your card or payment details. There are no loot boxes, no random rewards for money and no subscriptions.

Permissions

The game uses internet access and network state (to load ads and talk to Google Play), billing, the advertising ID, vibration (optional haptics, can be turned off in Settings) and the motion sensor for tilt steering if you choose it. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

Kinetra is made for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and deleting data

Because your game data lives only on your device, you control it: erase progress inside the game, or uninstall it. For advertising data held by Google, use Google's tools at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
