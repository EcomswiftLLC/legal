Spanish Language Tutor: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Spanish Language Tutor is made by Ecom Swift LLC. It helps you learn Spanish from beginner to advanced. You don't need an account, and we don't run any server of our own to collect your data: we don't receive, collect or sell your personal information.

What the app does on your device

  • Lessons, tests and progress: your completed lessons, scores, streaks, review schedule, achievements and settings are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. You can reset them in Settings, and uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Reminders (optional): if you turn on the daily reminder, Android's WorkManager schedules a local notification on your device. Android may ask for notification permission; nothing leaves your phone.
  • Pronunciation and listening: spoken examples use your device's built-in text-to-speech engine. The text to be spoken is passed to that engine, which is provided by your phone maker or by Google (or another voice provider you installed) and is governed by that provider's own privacy policy. The app does not record your voice and does not use the microphone.

Internet use

The core lessons work offline. The app uses the internet to load ads (free version) and to check your subscription with Google Play.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not operate servers that collect your learning data.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • We do not require an account or login.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier and similar device information to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud; this data is sent to Google encrypted in transit. In regions where consent is required (such as the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you are asked for consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are shown, and you can review your choice later in Settings. Premium turns off ads and stops ad requests. Learn more about how Google uses data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Purchases

Spanish Language Tutor Premium ("Remove ads") is a monthly subscription handled by Google Play Billing. We never see or store your payment details. You can cancel anytime in Google Play.

Deleting your data

Everything the app stores is on your device. Use Settings > Reset all progress, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app to remove it.

Children

The app is a general audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
