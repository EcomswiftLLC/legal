AI Chat Assistant: Talkwell, Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Talkwell (AI Chat Assistant: Talkwell) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You do not need an account. When you send a message, it goes to our server and to an AI provider so an answer can be written. Your chats are stored only on your phone. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your chats: conversations, saved answers, folders and pinned chats are stored only on your phone. You can delete a chat, or everything, in the app (Settings, Delete all my data). Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Settings and counters: your theme, assistant style, reading speed, and counters that pace the Premium offer and the rating request stay on your phone.
  • Exports: a Markdown or PDF export of a chat (Premium) is created in the app's temporary storage and handed to the app you choose in the share sheet. We do not receive it.

What is sent when you ask something

  • Your message and the recent part of the chat are sent to our server and to an AI provider, which generates the answer. This includes text you paste, text you dictate with the microphone, and text you share into the app from another app.
  • Retention: our server does not keep the text of what you send or of the answers. It keeps only anonymous usage counters and a random install identifier, so that free daily credits can be counted and abuse can be limited. The AI provider processes the request to answer it under its own terms.
  • No account, no sale: there is no sign-in, and we do not sell your data or use your chats to build advertising profiles.

You are shown a plain-language notice before your first request, and you can decline it. Please do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.

Microphone and voice

The microphone permission is asked for only when you tap the microphone button, after a short explanation. Your phone's own speech recognition service turns your voice into text. Depending on your phone and your settings, that service may process audio on the device or send it to its provider, under that provider's terms. Talkwell itself does not record or store audio. Reading answers aloud uses your phone's text-to-speech service.

Reports

Every answer has a Report action. If you send a report, we keep the reported answer, the question it answered, the reason you chose and any note, so that we can review it and make answers safer.

Text shared into the app

Talkwell can receive text you share from another app, or select and send with "Ask" from the text selection menu. It reads only the text you send to it. It asks for no contacts, location, camera or storage permission.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one. AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information to show and measure ads. Banners appear only on the Chats and Settings screens, never over a conversation. A full-screen ad may appear after you have copied or exported a result, with limits on how often, and never while an answer is being written. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one for an extra free credit. Premium removes ads. You can change your consent in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app learns only which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play.

Ratings

After you have used the app a few times, it may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Answers can be wrong

Talkwell is an AI assistant. Answers can be wrong, incomplete or out of date. Check important facts. It is not medical, legal or financial advice.

Children

Talkwell is made for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
