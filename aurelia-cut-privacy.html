Aurelia Cut privacy policy

Aurelia Cut: Cinematic Video Editor, a Windows app by Ecom Swift LLC. Effective 3 November 2026.

In short: Aurelia Cut edits and exports your videos on your own PC. It does not upload your footage, projects or any other files, it has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only network traffic is the Microsoft Store checking your Premium licence and app updates, handled by Windows.

What stays on your PC

  • Your media files, projects (.aurcut files), imported .cube LUTs, voiceover recordings and exports are read and written only on your computer, in the folders you choose (by default your Documents and Videos folders).
  • Thumbnails, waveforms and preview copies are kept in the app's own data folder so editing stays fast. You can delete them at any time in Settings, Storage.
  • Settings (language, editing preferences, recent projects list, window size) are stored in the app's data folder.
  • Beat detection, colour grading, scopes and exports run entirely on your PC with the bundled FFmpeg.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when you record a voiceover. The recording is saved as a file next to your project and is never sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off in Windows Settings, Privacy and security, Microphone.

Microsoft Store (Premium and updates)

Premium is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons. When you buy Premium, check your licence or look for an update, the app asks Windows and the Microsoft Store. That exchange is between your Microsoft account and Microsoft; Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your payment details or your account data. Microsoft's handling is described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

What we do not collect

Ecom Swift LLC does not collect, store, sell or share personal data through Aurelia Cut. The app contains no advertising, analytics, crash reporting or tracking code, and it does not create an account.

Links that leave the app

The app can open your web browser or the Microsoft Store when you choose to (for example to write a review, manage a subscription or read this policy). What those sites collect is covered by their own policies.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
