AI Resume Builder: Penvita, Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

Penvita (AI Resume Builder: Penvita) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You do not need an account. Your resumes and cover letters are stored only on your phone. When you use an AI tool, the text for that one request goes to our server and on to Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs the AI model that writes the answer. We do not sell personal information.

What stays on your phone

  • Your resumes and cover letters: everything you type, your template choices, copies made for different jobs, imported text and your interview practice notes are stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. You can delete one resume, or everything, in the app (Settings, Delete all my data). Uninstalling the app removes them.
  • Backups and exports: a backup file, a PDF or a Word file is created on your phone and goes only where you choose to send or save it, through the Android share sheet or a file you pick. We do not receive it.
  • Settings and counters: your theme, default template, and counters that pace the Premium offer, the rating request and ads stay on your phone.
  • Import: text you paste, or a Word or text file you choose, is read on your phone to fill the form. Nothing is sent when you import.
  • On-device tools: the preview, the resume strength check, the keyword check against a job post and the interview practice cards run entirely on your phone.

What is sent when you use an AI tool

  • The text for that request is sent to our server and to Cloudflare Workers AI (Cloudflare, Inc.), which runs the AI model that writes the answer. Depending on the tool, that is the summary or job bullets you chose to improve, your work, education and skills text for context, your name (used for a letter signature), and the job post you pasted. Your email address, phone number, city and links are never sent.
  • Retention: our server does not keep the text you send or the answers. It keeps only anonymous usage counters and a random install identifier, so that the daily AI request limit can be counted and abuse can be limited. Cloudflare processes the request only to produce the answer. Cloudflare's published Workers AI data usage terms say that customer content is not used to train its models and is not retained (see developers.cloudflare.com/workers-ai/platform/data-usage); Cloudflare's own privacy policy also applies to its service.
  • No account, no sale: there is no sign-in, and we do not sell your data or use your resumes to build advertising profiles or to train models.

You are shown a plain-language notice before your first AI request, and you can decline it and keep using the rest of the app. Please do not include anything in a resume or job post that you would not want an online AI service to process.

Reports

Every AI result has a Report action. If you send a report, we keep the reported result, the text that produced it if you leave "Include my original text" ticked, the reason you chose and any note, so that we can review it and make results safer.

Advertising

The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one. AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information to show and measure ads. A banner appears only on the resume list and Settings screens, never over a resume you are editing, a preview or an AI result. A full-screen ad may appear after you have exported a file, with limits on how often, and never while a result is being written. Premium and Remove ads turn ads off. You can change your consent in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium is sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app learns only which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them. Subscriptions renew automatically until you cancel them in Google Play.

Ratings

After you have exported a resume a few times, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

AI results can be wrong

Penvita works from the facts you give it and is designed not to invent any, but an AI can make mistakes. You are responsible for what is on your resume, so read every line before you send it. Penvita does not guarantee interviews, offers or any outcome with an employer.

Children

Penvita is made for adults aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
