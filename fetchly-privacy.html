Pet Health Tracker: Tailvia: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Tailvia (Pet Health Tracker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Everything you enter about your pets stays on your phone and is never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, Tailvia has no analytics tool of our own, and we don't sell personal information.

What Tailvia stores on your phone

  • Your pet records: pet profiles (name, species, breed, sex, birthday, adoption day, microchip number, your vet's name and phone number, notes, a colour tag and an optional photo), vaccines, treatments, vet visits and other health events (dates, clinics, costs, batch numbers, notes, next due dates), medicines (name, the dose text you type, times, start and end dates, and which doses you marked as given or skipped), weights, feeding, walk and other care logs, reminders, and the documents and photos you add, such as a vaccine card, an insurance paper or a receipt.
  • Settings: reminder time, units, currency, theme and similar choices, plus a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Photos and documents you choose with Android's photo picker or file picker are copied into the app's private storage; Tailvia does not get access to the rest of your photos or files. Tailvia has no camera, storage, location, contact or microphone permission, and it does not read your messages, your email or your notifications. Android cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and "Erase everything" in Settings removes it at any time. We cannot recover erased data.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to. Saving a backup file, exporting your records as a spreadsheet (CSV), creating a PDF summary for your vet and sharing a document or photo each create a file that is saved or shared to the place or app you pick in Android's file picker or share sheet. That place or app then handles the file under its own privacy policy. Restoring a backup or importing a file reads only the file you pick, on your phone.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for your pet reminders and medicine reminders. Android asks when you save your first reminder, and you can decline. Reminders arrive around the time you chose; Tailvia does not use exact alarms, so Android may move them by a few minutes to save battery.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Tailvia plans your reminders again.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.

Tailvia asks for no camera, storage, location, contact or microphone permission.

Home screen widget

The optional widget shows the pet you selected, its next due items and the next medicine dose. It is drawn on your phone from your own records and makes no network calls. Anyone who can see your home screen can see what the widget shows.

Advertising

Tailvia is free to start and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them, and it may use approximate location derived from your IP address. Your pet records, photos, documents and notes are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on list screens such as Today, Health, Meds, Care and Settings, never while you add or edit a record, during a dose action or on the purchase screen. A full-screen ad may appear on the Today screen after you save a record, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium item for a limited time or to add room for more pets, reminders, medicines or documents.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a good moment, for example after you have saved your first vaccine record, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Tailvia has no analytics tool of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Record keeping, not veterinary advice

Tailvia is for record keeping and does not give veterinary advice. It does not diagnose, treat or suggest medicines or doses. Medicine dose entries are free text that you type or copy from your vet or the label. The care ideas shown in the app are general and are not a substitute for your vet. Ask your vet about your pet's health.

Children

Tailvia is made for adults and families who own pets. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
