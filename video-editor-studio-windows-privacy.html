Privacy policy

Video Editor Studio for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 29 October 2026.

In short

Video Editor Studio edits and exports your videos on your own computer. Your videos, photos, music, recordings and projects are never uploaded. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only time it goes online is to ask the Microsoft Store whether you have Premium, and when you choose to buy it.

What stays on your PC

  • Your media and projects. Files you import are read where they are. Projects are saved as .vedproj files in your Documents folder (or wherever you save them). Exports are written to the folder you choose.
  • Working files. Thumbnails, timeline filmstrips, waveforms and preview copies of heavy files are kept in the app's own data folder so the editor stays fast. You can clear them in Settings > Storage. Temporary files made while exporting are deleted when the export ends.
  • Voiceover recordings. When you record a voiceover, the microphone is used only while you record. The recording is saved next to your project. Windows asks for your permission the first time, and you can turn it off at any time in Windows Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone.
  • Settings. Your preferences, the list of recent projects and the window position are stored in the app's data folder.

What goes online

  • Microsoft Store licence check. To unlock Premium, the app asks the Microsoft Store (through Windows) which add-ons your Microsoft account owns: at start, after a purchase, when you come back to the window and every 10 minutes. No personal data or content from the app is sent; Windows handles this exchange with Microsoft.
  • Purchases. Buying Premium opens the Microsoft Store purchase window. Payment is handled entirely by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We never see your payment details.
  • Links you click. "Manage subscription" opens your Microsoft account page in your browser, and "Contact support" opens your email program. Nothing is sent unless you send it.

Nothing else: no crash reports, no usage statistics, no update pings and no third-party services.

Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its data folder (settings, thumbnails, preview copies). Your projects, recordings and exports are your own files; delete them like any other file.
