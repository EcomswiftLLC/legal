AI Writing Assistant: Penlark: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 November 2026

Penlark (AI Writing Assistant: Penlark) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. It writes drafts for everyday and work messages, such as emails, replies, posts, bios, listings and notes. You don't need an account. This page explains, in plain words, what stays on your phone, what is sent to our server and an AI provider, and what we keep.

The short version

  • The details you type to get a draft are sent to our server and to an AI provider, so that the draft can be written. The app tells you this before your first request, and asks you to accept it.
  • Our server keeps no copy of your text and no copy of the draft. It keeps only an anonymous count so that free daily credits work.
  • If you report a draft, the reported draft is kept so we can review it. Nothing else you wrote is kept.
  • Your saved drafts, folders and voice profiles stay on your phone. We don't have them.
  • We do not sell your data, and there is no account.

What Penlark stores on your phone

  • Drafts you save, with their folders and favourite marks, in a database in the app's private storage.
  • Voice profiles: the name, role, business, usual tone, sign-off and style note you enter. They are stored only on your phone. They are included in a request only when "Use my voice" is on, so the draft can sound like you.
  • Settings and counters: language, tone, length, theme, how many drafts you copied or exported (used to space out the rating request and ads), and your remaining daily credits.
  • An anonymous install ID, a random number made when the app first starts. It is not linked to your name, email, phone or Google account.

You can remove everything on your phone at any time in Settings, under Delete all my data. Penlark turns off Android backup for its data, so drafts are not copied to the cloud by Android.

What is sent to our server and an AI provider

When you tap Write, Penlark sends the answers you gave, the template you chose, your tone, length and language choices, and (if "Use my voice" is on) your voice profile. The same goes for the refine buttons, together with the draft being changed. Everything travels over an encrypted connection to our server, which adds safety rules and passes the request to an AI provider to write the text. The draft comes back the same way.

Please don't type anything into Penlark that you don't want to share with an AI provider, such as passwords, card numbers or health details.

  • Retention: our server does not store the text of requests or drafts. It stores the anonymous install ID, the number of drafts used today and the number of rewarded videos watched today, so that free credits and limits work, and counters used to keep the service running and to stop abuse.
  • The AI provider processes the request to write the draft, and its own terms and privacy policy apply to that processing.
  • Safety checks: requests and drafts are checked automatically by rules on your phone and on our server. Requests that break the rules are refused and not charged.

Reporting a draft

Every draft has a Report button. If you send a report, we receive the reported draft, the reason you picked, your optional note and, if you leave the box ticked, the request that produced it. We keep reports so we can improve the safety rules, and we delete them when we no longer need them. You can untick "include my request" before you send.

Ads

Penlark shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Banners appear only on the Library and Settings screens. A full-screen ad can appear after you finish copying, sharing or exporting a draft, and never while a draft is being written. A rewarded video is always your choice: it gives you free credits when you watch it to the end. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). Ad requests do not include what you wrote.

To give you the credit for a rewarded video, the anonymous install ID is passed to the ad service and checked by our server. You can change your ad consent in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it), and you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves.

Purchases

Premium (weekly, monthly or yearly) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you copy a draft you like, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and Google Play handles the review. The app asks rarely, at least 30 days apart, and never after an error or a limit.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to write drafts, and for ads and Google Play.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described above.
  • Billing: for Google Play purchases.

Penlark asks for no camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage or notification permission. When you tap "Open in email" or "Open in messages", your own email or messaging app opens with the draft filled in, and you decide whether to send it. Penlark never sends anything for you.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Penlark has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Who Penlark is for

Penlark is for adults, aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children. Drafts are written by an AI and can contain mistakes, so read and edit them before you send them. Penlark does not give legal, medical, tax or financial advice.

Your choices and rights

Because we keep no profile of you and no copy of your drafts, there is little to delete on our side. You can delete everything on your phone in Settings. To ask about a report you sent, or any question about your data, write to us and include the approximate date. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete data about you, and we will answer such requests within the time the law requires.
