Privacy Policy — Cookie Inspector & Editor

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Cookie Inspector & Editor shows and edits the cookies for the site you're viewing. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you open the popup, the extension reads the cookies for the active tab's site only, using the browser's cookies API scoped to that tab (the activeTab grant you give by opening the popup). It does not read cookies for other sites or run in the background.
  • Viewing, editing, deleting, adding, exporting and importing cookies all happen on your device. Your cookies are never uploaded. An export is a file saved locally by you.
  • The extension does not read page content and does not request broad access to your browsing.
  • Nothing you view or change is sent to us or any third party.

Permissions

  • cookies — to read, edit, add and delete cookies for the site you're on, when you open the popup.
  • activeTab — to scope cookie access to the current tab's site (no broad host access).
  • storage — to save your settings locally.
  • alarms — only for the optional reminder below.
  • host access (ecomswiftllc.com) — only to verify a Pro purchase (license check). No other hosts.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your cookies, browsing history, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
