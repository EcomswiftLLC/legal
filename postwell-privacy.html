Postwell: Poster & Flyer Maker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 19 November 2026

Postwell (Poster & Flyer Maker) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. The designs, photos and brand details you add are stored on your phone and are never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Postwell stores on your phone

  • Your designs: the text, shapes, colours, layout and pages of every design you make or open from a template, with a small preview picture of each.
  • Your photos: pictures you choose to add to a design. They are copied into the app's private storage.
  • Your brand kit: colours, fonts and a logo picture you choose to save.
  • Settings and counters: your theme and recent colours, how many exports you have left, and a few counters used to space out ads and the rating request.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Photos are chosen with Android's photo picker, so Postwell does not get access to the rest of your photos. Cloud backup of the app's data is switched off. Android's phone-to-phone transfer during setup of a new phone may carry the data over. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and you can delete any design inside the app at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: saving a PNG, JPG or PDF, saving a backup file, sharing a design or printing it. Saved files go to the Pictures/Postwell folder or to the place you pick, and shared or printed files go to the app or printer you pick in Android's share sheet or print dialog. That app or service then handles the file under its own privacy policy. Importing a backup reads only the file you pick, on your phone. QR codes you add only contain the text you type, and they are drawn on your phone.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, as described below.
  • Billing: used for purchases through Google Play.

Postwell asks for no location, camera, microphone, contacts, notification or storage permission.

Advertising

Postwell is free to start and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your designs, photos and brand kit are never shared with the ad service. Banner ads appear on the Home screen and in the export window, never while you edit a design. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to take one design out of the app.

Where the law requires it, you can change your consent in the app under Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime), Remove ads and the small export pack are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have successfully saved or shared a few designs, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Postwell has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Your content

You decide what text and pictures go into your designs. Postwell does not check them, and you are responsible for having the right to use them.

Children

Postwell is made for adults. It is not directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
