Pitchloom Tuner and Metronome: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

Pitchloom (Tuner and Metronome) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your settings, saved tempos and practice history are stored on your phone and never uploaded by us. Sound from the microphone is analyzed on your phone while the Tuner is open and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.

The microphone

Pitchloom asks for the microphone permission so the Tuner can hear the note you play. This is how it is used:

  • The microphone is on only while the Tuner screen is open and in front. It stops when you switch to another tab, leave the app or turn off the screen.
  • Each short block of sound is turned into a pitch (a note and how many cents sharp or flat) in memory and then overwritten. Nothing is recorded, saved to storage or sent over the internet, and Pitchloom has no speech recognition.
  • Pitchloom never listens in the background and runs no background service. Android shows its microphone indicator while the Tuner is listening.
  • If you decline the permission, the Tuner stays off and shows how to turn it on later. The metronome, setlist and practice timer work without it.

What Pitchloom stores on your phone

  • Settings: reference pitch, note names, sensitivity, instrument and tuning, theme, and your choices for the beat lights, vibration and screen flash.
  • Metronome and setlist: the tempo, time signature, accents and sound you last used, and the tempos you save with a name.
  • Custom tunings: tunings you create (a Premium feature).
  • Practice history: the time you practice each day, your daily goal and your streak.
  • Purchase and ad state: whether Premium or Remove ads is owned and when a 24 hour unlock ends.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Android's own backup (to your Google account, if you turned it on in Android settings) may include it; that backup is controlled by you and by Google.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to: Share sends a plain text summary of your setlist or practice stats to the app you pick in Android's share sheet. That app then handles the text under its own privacy policy.

Permissions

  • Microphone: the Tuner, as described above.
  • Vibration: an optional tick when a note is in tune and an optional pulse on the beat.
  • Internet and network state: used only for ads and Google Play purchases.
  • Advertising ID: used by the ad service, unless you own Premium or Remove ads.

Pitchloom asks for no location, contacts, photos, camera, calendar or storage permission.

Advertising

Pitchloom is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your tempos, tunings and practice history are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear on the Setlist, Practice and Settings screens (on Practice only while no session is running), never on the Tuner or the Metronome. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish sharing a setlist or your stats, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to unlock the Studio pack for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after you have tuned a few times or finished a practice session, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Pitchloom has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Children

Pitchloom is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.
