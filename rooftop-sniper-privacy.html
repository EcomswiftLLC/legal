Rooftop Sniper: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Rooftop Sniper is a single-player sniper game made by Ecom Swift LLC. It works offline, needs no account, and we do not run any server that receives your data. We do not collect, store, sell or share your personal information ourselves.

What the game stores on your device

  • Progress: your best scores, stars and which missions are unlocked are saved in a small file on your phone.
  • Settings: look sensitivity, volume, graphics quality and similar options are saved on your phone.

This information stays on your device. Uninstalling the game removes it.

Advertising

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a small banner on the title and mission screens, an occasional full-screen ad when you return to the mission list, and an optional video you can choose to watch for bonus rounds. Ads never appear during a mission.

To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, Google AdMob may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address (for approximate location), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. This data is sent to Google, encrypted in transit, and is handled under Google's own policies: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Where the law requires consent (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), the game asks for your choice through Google's User Messaging Platform before any ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time from Settings, under Privacy choices (shown wherever it applies). On Android you can also reset or opt out of your advertising ID in your phone's Settings under Privacy, Ads.

The game asks AdMob for ads suitable for a general audience (content rating PG at most).

What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or login.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.
  • We do not sell your data.
  • The game requests no special Android permissions beyond internet access and network state (used for ads).

Children

Rooftop Sniper contains stylised armed conflict and is intended for players aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Deleting your data

Everything the game itself stores is on your device; uninstalling the game removes it. To manage data held by Google for advertising, use your Google account's ad settings.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
