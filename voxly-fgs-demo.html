Tapewave: Microphone Foreground Service

Ecom Swift LLC · demonstration for the Google Play foreground-service declaration

What the video shows

  1. The user opens Tapewave and taps Voice recorder. Recording starts only because the user tapped it, and the screen shows the timer, level meter and the Pause, Mark and Save controls.
  2. The user leaves the app (Home). Recording continues in the background and a persistent notification shows the running timer. The system microphone indicator is visible in the status bar.
  3. The screen is turned off and back on. The recording kept running and the notification timer kept advancing.
  4. The notification offers Pause, Mark and Stop. The user taps Stop and the take is saved to the library.

Why a foreground service is required

Tapewave is a voice recorder. People record lectures, meetings and voice memos with the screen off or while using other apps. The recording must not be cut off when the app is not on screen, so it runs as a foreground service of type microphone (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE). It is always started by the user, always shows a notification, and ends when the user taps Stop. Audio stays on the device.

How to reproduce in the app

  1. Open Tapewave and tap Voice recorder on the Home screen.
  2. Press Home or turn the screen off; open the notification shade to see the recording notification.
  3. Tap Stop in the notification (or open the app and tap Save).
