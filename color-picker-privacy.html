Privacy Policy — Color Picker & Eyedropper

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Color Picker & Eyedropper lets you pick a color from your screen, convert it between formats, check WCAG contrast, and save palettes. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • Picking a color uses your browser's built-in eyedropper. The color you pick, the conversions, contrast results, recent colors and saved palettes are all handled and stored locally in your browser.
  • This extension does not read the pages you visit — it has no page access. It only works with the colors you pick or type.
  • Nothing you pick or save is sent to us or any third party.

Permissions

  • storage — to save your recent colors, saved palettes and settings locally.
  • alarms — only for the optional reminder below.
  • host access (ecomswiftllc.com) — only to verify a Pro purchase (license check). No other hosts.

Note: this extension requests no access to your tabs, browsing or page content.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your browsing history, your colors or palettes, or any personal data.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
