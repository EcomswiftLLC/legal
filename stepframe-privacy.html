Stepframe privacy policy

Stepframe: Screen Recording Editor, by Ecom Swift LLC. Effective 5 November 2026.

Stepframe is a Windows app for editing screen recordings into tutorials and demo videos. This policy explains what the app does with information. In short: everything you edit stays on your PC, and Stepframe does not collect, send or sell personal data.

What stays on your PC

  • The recordings, webcam clips, voice-overs, music and images you import are read from your PC and never uploaded.
  • Projects (.stepframe files), exported videos, caption and chapter files are saved where you choose.
  • Settings, a list of recent projects and a cache of thumbnails, waveforms and preview copies are kept in the app's own folder on your PC. You can clear the cache in Settings, and Windows removes the app folder when you uninstall.

Microphone

Stepframe asks Windows for microphone access only when you press Voice-over to record narration. The recording is saved as a file next to your project on your PC. It is not sent anywhere. You can turn microphone access off in Windows Settings, Privacy and security, Microphone.

No accounts, ads or analytics

Stepframe has no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics or tracking. The app makes no network connections of its own. Analysis features (zoom suggestions, silence and idle detection, audio clean-up) run on your PC.

Purchases

Stepframe Pro is sold as Microsoft Store add-ons. Payment, receipts, renewals and cancellations are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Stepframe only asks the Microsoft Store on your PC whether a Pro add-on is active; it does not see your payment details.

Children

Stepframe does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
