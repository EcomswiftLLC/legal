Privacy Policy - Cartiva: Abandoned Cart & Checkout Recovery

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Cartiva - Abandoned Cart & Checkout Recovery is a Wix app, published by Ecom Swift LLC, that helps a Wix store owner win back shoppers who left a checkout unfinished. It watches abandoned checkouts in Wix eCommerce and sends the store's own reminder emails through Wix to customers who agreed to receive marketing email. This policy explains what data the app handles, where it lives and why.

Two roles. The site owner who installs the app (the "merchant") is the controller of their customers' data and decides who is emailed. Ecom Swift LLC provides the software and acts as a processor on the merchant's instructions. Wix hosts the app, stores its data on the merchant's own site and sends the emails. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive a copy of the merchant's customer data.

1. Who we are

The app is published by Ecom Swift LLC. You can reach us at wix@ecomswiftllc.com.

3. Where data is stored and who sends the email

The app has no database of its own. Its data lives in private data collections (Wix Data) on the merchant's own Wix site, in a space reserved for the app. Each site has its own collections, so one merchant's data is never mixed with another's. Only site administrators and the app's backend, after Wix has verified the caller, can read or change them.

Emails are sent through Wix (Wix Email Marketing transmissions) as marketing email. Wix adds a per-recipient unsubscribe link, enforces marketing consent again at send time and applies its own sending limits. Ecom Swift LLC does not operate a mail server.

4. Purposes and legal basis

We process the data only to provide the app to the merchant: detecting abandoned checkouts, sending the merchant's reminders to customers who agreed, applying quiet hours and sending limits, creating the merchant's discount codes, reporting recoveries and keeping the do-not-contact list.

The merchant decides whether and how to email its customers and needs a valid legal basis to do so. The app is designed around consent: it sends only to contacts whose Wix marketing status is subscribed. Merchants remain responsible for collecting that consent, for a postal address and sender identification in their emails where the law requires it, for their own privacy policy, and for complying with laws such as the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CAN-SPAM Act and CASL.

5. Scheduling relay (Cloudflare)

Wix gives apps no built-in timer, so the app uses a small scheduling relay running on Cloudflare Workers, operated by Ecom Swift LLC. It exists only to wake the installed app when a reminder is due. For each site it holds the app instance id, the web address the app is woken at, the next time something is due and a failure counter. It holds no customer data: no emails, names, cart contents, orders or Wix credentials. When a site has nothing scheduled, or the app is removed, the relay's entry for it is deleted. Cloudflare, Inc. acts as our sub-processor for this limited data. If the relay is offline, reminders still go out when the merchant opens the dashboard or the store has other activity, and may then be late.

6. Other service providers

  • Wix hosts the app and the data, provides the abandoned checkouts, contacts and orders, creates discount codes, sends the emails and identifies the site owner.
  • Cloudflare runs the scheduling relay described above.

We do not sell personal data, do not share it for advertising and use no advertising network. The app has no third-party analytics and no AI model provider. It sets no cookies of its own. The optional storefront sign-up form is loaded through Wix only when the visitor's cookie choices allow it, and may keep a few small non-personal values in the visitor's browser (for example a short-lived copy of the form's settings).

8. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Every dashboard request is checked against a verified Wix identity, and every event from Wix is verified before it is processed. Stored data is limited to what the app needs: customer emails are kept only as a masked form and a salted one-way hash. The scheduling relay and the app sign their messages and the relay only calls allow-listed addresses. Raw technical errors are not shown to users. No system is perfectly secure, and the data in the merchant's site is also protected by the merchant's own Wix account security.

9. International transfers

Wix and Cloudflare operate globally, so data may be processed in countries other than where the merchant or the customer lives. Those providers apply their own transfer safeguards. We do not transfer personal data anywhere else.

10. Your rights

Because the merchant is the controller, customers who want to access, correct, delete or object to the use of their data, or who want to stop receiving emails, should contact the store first. Every email carries an unsubscribe link provided by Wix. Merchants can view and delete what the app stores for their site and can add any customer to the do-not-contact list. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR or the CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict personal data. You can also write to wix@ecomswiftllc.com and we will help the merchant fulfil the request. We respond within 30 days. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined in the CCPA.

11. Children

The app is built for businesses and is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 where the law sets that age). We do not knowingly process children's data for our own purposes.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way we will update the date above and notify merchants in the app.
