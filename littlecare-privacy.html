Littlecare Child Care Log: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Littlecare (Child Care Log) is made by Ecom Swift LLC for parents and caregivers. You don't need an account to use it. Everything you enter about your children is stored on your phone and never sent to us. We don't run a server that receives your data, and we don't sell personal information.

What Littlecare stores on your phone

  • Child profiles: names, birth dates, an optional photo or a drawn avatar, allergies, conditions and medicines as you describe them, doctor and school details, preferences, house rules and notes for caregivers.
  • People: emergency contacts and the people allowed to collect a child, with the phone numbers you type.
  • The daily log: meals, drinks, naps, mood, activities, potty, medicine given, temperatures, symptoms, incidents (with optional photos), milestones, learning moments, behaviour notes, drop-offs and pickups, and the name and role of the caregiver who logged each entry.
  • Health records: growth measurements, vaccination records, doctor visits, the allergy action plan you write and medicine settings (dose and timing as you enter them).
  • Plans: routines, timetable, checklists and meal plans.
  • Settings: units, reminders, theme, your name and role on this phone, the emergency number, and a babysitter-mode PIN stored only as a salted hash.

All of this is kept in the app's private storage on your device. Littlecare turns off Android's cloud backup for its data; a direct transfer to a new phone with Android's own transfer tool is allowed. Photos are re-encoded when you add them, which removes camera details such as location. Uninstalling the app removes everything, and Settings > Erase all data on this phone removes it at any time.

When data leaves your phone

Only when you choose to. Sharing a day report, a handover sheet, a handover code or QR code, a PDF, an image card, a spreadsheet (CSV) or a backup file sends it through Android's share sheet or file picker to the app or place you pick, which then handles it under its own privacy policy. Handover codes travel only through the channel you choose; there is no Littlecare server in between. Photos are never included in handover codes.

Permissions

  • Notifications: for the reminders you turn on (medicines, drop-off and pickup, routines, doctor visits, vaccines, the evening report and backups).
  • Camera: asked only when you tap "Scan a QR code" to import a handover. Frames are read on the phone to find the code and are not saved. You can paste the code instead.
  • Start at boot: after your phone restarts, Littlecare plans your reminders again.

Littlecare asks for no location, contacts, microphone or storage permission. Photos come from Android's photo picker or your camera app. Phone calls open your dialer with the number filled in; Littlecare never places calls itself. The app uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Littlecare is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show and measure ads. Your children's names, logs, photos and health records are never shared with the ad service and are never used for ads. Banners appear only on main list screens. A full-screen ad may appear after a completed entry or shared report, limited in number and spacing. No ads appear in logging forms, health screens, timers, routines, babysitter mode or any screen a child may be using. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to open a Premium feature once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

At a calm moment, for example after a week of logging or after you first share a report, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Littlecare has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health information and reference data

Littlecare is a record-keeping and organisation tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or give medical advice, and it never suggests medicine doses: medicine names, doses and intervals are only what you enter. Growth percentiles use public reference tables (WHO Child Growth Standards and Growth Reference, World Health Organization; CDC 2000 growth charts, U.S. Centers for Disease Control and Prevention). Vaccination schedules are summaries of official national schedules, each shown with its source. The "when to get help" card summarises public guidance from the NHS and the American Academy of Pediatrics.

Children

Littlecare is made for adults who care for children. It is not directed at children, and children are not asked to enter any information. Information about children is entered by their parent or caregiver, stays on the device, and is never sent to us or used for advertising. Screens a child may look at, such as picture routines and timers, show no ads and no links and can be left only through a grown-up check.
