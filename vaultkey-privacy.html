Privacy policy

Offline Password Manager: VaultKey for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • VaultKey works on your PC. Everything you save (logins, notes, cards, identities and attachments) is kept in an encrypted vault file on your PC, encrypted with a key derived from your master password.
  • There is no account and no cloud copy. Ecom Swift LLC never receives your vault, your master password or any item in it.
  • VaultKey contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting.

What VaultKey sends over the internet

  • Microsoft Store: licence checks, purchases and the rating dialog are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. VaultKey only receives whether Premium is active and the displayed prices.
  • Breach check (Premium, only when you press the button): for each password, only the first 5 characters of its SHA-1 fingerprint are sent to the Have I Been Pwned Pwned Passwords range service (api.pwnedpasswords.com). Your passwords and full fingerprints never leave your PC. Nothing is sent unless you start the check.
  • Opening a website saved in an item opens it in your browser. That website has its own privacy policy.

Files you create

Backups, CSV exports and the emergency kit are saved where you choose. Backups are encrypted. CSV exports are not encrypted, and VaultKey warns you before creating one.

Master password and security limits

There is no recovery without your master password: no reset and no back door. VaultKey has not been independently audited, and no software can promise to be unbreakable. Questions about security: support@ecomswiftllc.com with "VaultKey security" in the subject.

Children

VaultKey does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
