Privacy Policy — Quran Majeed: Flip & Read

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Quran Majeed: Flip & Read is a deliberately simple, offline page-flip Quran reader. It has no account, collects no personal information itself, and works without a network connection.

What stays on your device

  • Your reading position (the page you left off on) and your app settings are stored only in a local database on your device. Nothing you read or set is ever sent to any server.
  • The Quran text is bundled inside the app, so reading works fully offline with no network access.
  • There is no account, no sign-in, and no cloud sync. The app collects no name, email, contacts, photos, or location.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The app is free and ad-supported, with a one-time "Remove Ads" purchase to go ad-free. Ads are the one place data leaves the app, so it is stated plainly:

  • Google's Mobile Ads SDK is included and collects data for ad serving and measurement (device identifiers, coarse usage signals) per Google's own AdMob disclosures. This is Google's data collection, governed by Google's privacy policy, not something the app adds on top.
  • Before requesting any ad, the app runs Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent flow, so users in regions that require it (EEA, UK, and applicable US states) are asked for ad-personalization consent. Only non-personalized ads are requested if they decline.
  • A single banner appears at the edge of the reader screen. No reading data is passed to the ad SDK — it only sees what any AdMob-integrated app's ad requests inherently carry.
  • The "Remove Ads" purchase is handled by Google Play Billing; the app never sees payment details, and purchase status is verified against Google Play's own records on every app start.

No other tracking

No analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK, no Firebase, no Google Analytics — no tracking beyond what the ads system above uses for ad delivery itself.

Children

The app is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ad content is capped to a General maturity rating.

Deleting your data

Because the only data the app stores is your local reading position and settings, uninstalling the app removes all of it. There is no server-side copy to separately delete, because none is ever created.
