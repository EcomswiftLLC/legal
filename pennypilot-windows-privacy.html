Privacy policy

Budget Planner & Expense Tracker: PennyPilot for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • PennyPilot works on your PC. Your accounts, transactions, budgets, goals, bank files you import, receipts you attach, backups and settings are stored only in the app's data folder on your PC and in files you choose to save. They are never uploaded.
  • PennyPilot has no account, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

No bank connection

PennyPilot never connects to your bank and never asks for bank usernames or passwords. You import files that you download yourself (CSV, OFX, QFX or QIF), and they are read on your PC.

Network use

The app works offline. It contacts the Microsoft Store only to check your Premium licence, show prices, process a purchase you start, check for app updates, and open the Microsoft Store rating window when you choose "Rate in Microsoft Store". Microsoft handles payments, licences and ratings under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Links you click (privacy policy, support email) open in your browser or mail app.

Exchange rates

PennyPilot does not download exchange rates. Rates are typed in by you and stored on your PC.

Deleting your data

You can delete transactions, accounts and receipts in the app. Uninstalling PennyPilot removes its data folder. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you saved them.

Children

PennyPilot does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
