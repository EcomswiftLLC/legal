Toneva: Women Workout at Home: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Toneva (Women Workout at Home) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your workouts, body log, progress photos, habits and optional cycle notes are stored on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your fitness or health information, and we don't sell personal information.

What you enter, and where it stays

  • Fitness information: your answers from the first-run questions (age, goal, focus areas, level, training days, session length), finished workouts, plan progress, ratings, favourites, your own workouts, badges, streaks and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Body information (optional): height, weight, a goal weight, weekly pace and body measurements are stored on your phone only. Weight goals are available to adults only and are checked against a healthy minimum for your height.
  • Cycle notes (optional, off by default): if you turn on cycle-aware suggestions, the date your last period started and your typical cycle and period lengths are stored on your phone only and used to suggest a lighter, as-planned or stronger workout. This is comfort and energy guidance, not medical advice, and it makes no fertility, ovulation or contraception predictions. You can turn it off and delete the dates at any time.
  • Habits: water, sleep, mood and energy check-ins are stored on your phone only.
  • Progress photos (optional): photos you add are copied into the app's private storage. They do not appear in your gallery, are excluded from Android's device backup and from the app's backup files, and are never uploaded. You can delete one photo or all photos in the app, and Erase my data deletes them too.
  • Steps (optional): if you turn on step counting, the app reads your phone's built-in step counter while the app is open, after asking for the Physical activity permission. Daily totals are stored on your phone only.
  • Backups and exports: a backup file or CSV export is created only when you tap Save a backup or Export CSV, and it is saved wherever you choose. Android's own device backup (if you have it turned on) may include your workout history and settings, but never your progress photos.
  • Deleting: You, Erase my data deletes everything the app stored on your phone. Uninstalling the app does the same.

Permissions

  • Physical activity (Android 10 and newer): only for the optional step counter, asked when you turn it on.
  • Notifications (Android 13 and newer): only for reminders you turn on (workout, missed day, streak, water, weekly weigh-in) and for the workout notification with pause and skip controls while a workout runs.
  • Camera and photos: no camera or storage permission is requested. Progress photos come from Android's system Photo Picker (only the photo you select) or from your camera app, which hands the app only the photo you take.
  • The app asks for no contacts, location or microphone permission. It uses the internet only for ads and Google Play purchases.

Advertising

Toneva is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Your workouts, body log, photos, habits and cycle notes are never shared with the ad service. Banners appear only on the Today, Progress and You screens, never during a workout, a rest, a timer or while you log anything. A full-screen ad may appear after you finish a workout and leave the results screen, limited in number and spacing, and never after your first finished workout. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to unlock a single Premium item once. If you tell the app you are 13 to 17, ad requests are marked as from a user under the age of consent and limited to general audience ad content.

You can change your consent at any time in You, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After a few finished workouts, a 7-day streak or a weight milestone, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Toneva has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data.

Health information

Toneva is a fitness app, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat or prevent any condition. Calories, distance and steps are estimates.

Children and teens

Toneva is not directed at children under 13 and cannot be used by anyone who says they are under 13. People aged 13 to 17 get a healthy-habits mode with no weight targets, no weight log and no progress photos. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
