Privacy policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Your invoices, clients and settings are kept in a file in your own Google Drive. We do not sell data, show ads or use your Google data to train AI models. Our servers hold only what is needed to run your subscription.

Who we are

InvoiceOak is made and sold by Ecom Swift LLC, a Wyoming limited liability company at 30 N Gould St #37906, Sheridan, WY 82801, USA. In this policy, "we" means Ecom Swift LLC. Questions go to support@ecomswiftllc.com.

PDF copies of sent invoicesA folder called "Invoices (PDF)" in your DriveSo you keep a copy of what you sent.

Who sees your data

  • Google, because the app runs on Google Workspace and your data is in your Drive.
  • Your clients, only when you send them an invoice or message.
  • Our payment provider, for subscription billing.
  • Nobody else. We do not sell or rent data, and we run no advertising or tracking pixels inside the app.

Optional AI help

Everything in the app works without AI. If you add your own free Gemini key, the app sends the text you chose (for example an email you pasted to draft an invoice) to Google's Gemini service, only when you press a button that says Gemini. On Google's free tier, Google may use that text to improve its products, so do not use it for anything private, and never paste card or bank details. We do not receive that text. Remove the key at any time in Google Workspace settings inside the app.

Your controls

  • Turn each Google connection and each automation on or off in the app. Everything is off until you switch it on, and "Pause all" stops every automatic action.
  • Review every automatic action in the Activity list.
  • Remove app access in your Google Account permissions.

Keeping and deleting

Your data file and PDFs stay in your Drive until you delete them. They are yours. If you stop using the app, nothing more is created. On request we delete the account record in our subscription service within 30 days, except billing records we must keep by law. To ask, email support@ecomswiftllc.com from your account address.

Security

Data moves over encrypted connections. Your Gemini key is stored in your own account settings for the app and is never shown back in the app. Formulas in exported spreadsheets are neutralised so they cannot run. No system is perfectly secure, so tell us right away if you suspect a problem.

The app is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

If we change this policy in a meaningful way, we will update the date above and tell you in the app before the change applies.
