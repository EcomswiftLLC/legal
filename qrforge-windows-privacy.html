Privacy policy

QR Code Generator & Scanner: QRForge for Windows, by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated 10 November 2026.

In short

  • QRForge works on your PC. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The codes you create, the designs you save and the codes you scan stay on your PC and are never uploaded by QRForge.

Data stored on your PC

  • Saved designs (content, colours, styles and any logo you add), your scan history (if you keep it switched on) and your settings, in a local database in the app's data folder.
  • Automatic daily copies of that database for 14 days, and backup files you choose to create.
  • Files you export (PNG, SVG, PDF, EPS, ZIP, CSV, contacts and calendar files) are saved only where you choose.

You can switch off the scan history in Settings, delete scans and designs at any time, and remove everything by uninstalling the app.

Camera and screen

When you scan with the camera, QRForge reads video frames on your PC to find codes. Frames are not recorded, stored or sent anywhere, and the microphone is never used. When you scan your screen, QRForge takes one picture of the screen, looks for a code in memory and discards the picture. Windows privacy settings control camera access.

Network use

  • Microsoft Store: to check whether you have Premium, show prices, process purchases and show the Store rating dialog, the app uses Microsoft Store services. Payments are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Ecom Swift LLC does not receive your card details.
  • Links: QRForge opens a link from a scanned code only when you choose to, after showing you the address. The website you open has its own privacy policy.
  • Nothing else: QRForge does not send your codes, scans, files or usage data to Ecom Swift LLC or anyone else.

Children

QRForge does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If the app's behaviour changes, this page will be updated with a new date before the change ships. Questions: Ecom Swift LLC, support@ecomswiftllc.com.
