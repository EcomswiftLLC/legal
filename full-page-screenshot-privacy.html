Privacy Policy — Full-Page Screenshot

Extension by Ecom Swift LLC. Last updated: October 2026.

Full-Page Screenshot captures the page you're on — the visible area or the whole page — and lets you download it. It is built to keep your data on your device.

What happens on your device

  • When you start a capture, the extension scrolls the active tab and captures its visible area (using the browser's own capture of the current tab), then stitches the slices into one image in your browser. For a full-page capture it briefly hides fixed/sticky elements so they don't repeat, then restores them.
  • The screenshot is created and previewed locally. It is never uploaded — you download it to your own computer (PNG, or JPG/clipboard/area with Pro).
  • Settings are stored locally.

Permissions

  • activeTab / scripting — to read the page size, scroll the page and capture the current tab when you start a capture.
  • storage — to save your settings locally.
  • alarms — only for the optional reminder below.
  • host access (ecomswiftllc.com) — only to verify a Pro purchase (license check). No other hosts.

Reminders

  • On the free plan, the extension may open a single in-extension upgrade page about every 48 hours. Paying users never see it, and it has a "Don't remind me again" option. The page ships inside the extension — no browsing data is involved.

Pro subscription (optional)

  • If you unlock Pro, you can enter the email you used at checkout. It is sent to our license server (Ecom Swift LLC, on Wix) only to confirm your purchase, unlock Pro, re-check it periodically, and cancel when you ask. We use it solely for license verification; the free features never send it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, sell, or transfer your screenshots, your browsing history, the pages you capture, their content, or any personal data. Screenshots never leave your device.
  • We do not inject ads, track you, or use third-party analytics.
