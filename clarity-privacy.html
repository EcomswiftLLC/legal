Clarity Photo Enhancer HD: Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

Clarity (Photo Enhancer HD) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your photos are enhanced on your phone and are never uploaded to us. We don't run a server that receives your pictures, and we don't sell personal information.

Your photos

  • You choose what Clarity sees: the app opens only the photos you pick with Android's photo picker, take with your camera app, or send to it from another app. It does not browse your gallery.
  • Everything happens on your device: auto enhance, the modes (portrait, landscape, night, food, document, old photo, dehaze, HDR, black and white), manual adjustments, face enhance, HD upscale, batch enhance and cropping are all processed locally with on-device algorithms.
  • Face detection on the device: to protect skin from over-sharpening and for Face enhance, Clarity uses Google ML Kit face detection. The model ships inside the app and runs on your phone. Your photo is not sent anywhere for it, and no face data is stored or shared.
  • What is kept: while you edit, a private copy of the photo you opened and your current settings are kept inside the app so you can continue after the app is closed. They are deleted when you finish or discard the edit. The app also keeps your saved looks, a short list of your recent results and your settings (theme, export format and quality). Uninstalling the app removes all of this.
  • What gets saved: when you tap Save, the enhanced picture is written to Pictures/Clarity on your phone. If you tap Share, it is handed to the app you pick.

Camera and gallery

Clarity asks for no camera, contacts, location or microphone permission. Taking a photo opens your phone's own camera app, and choosing a photo uses Android's photo picker, which gives the app only the photos you select. On Android 9 and older the app asks for permission to save to your Pictures folder, only when you save.

Advertising

Clarity is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or removed ads. Before ads load, a consent form appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. A banner appears on the home and save screens, never over the photo while you edit. A full-screen ad may appear after you save a photo, at most once every few saves. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one to unlock the PRO tools for 24 hours.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have saved a few photos, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location, app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual.

Children

Clarity is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
