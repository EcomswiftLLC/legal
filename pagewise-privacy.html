Folivo PDF Editor & Reader: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Folivo (PDF Editor & Reader) is made by Ecom Swift LLC. You don't need an account to use it. Your documents are opened, edited and saved on your phone and never leave it. We don't run a server that receives your files, and we don't sell personal information.

Your documents

  • Everything happens on your device: reading, searching, signing, annotating, filling forms, merging, splitting, organizing pages, compressing, protecting and unlocking, converting to and from images, watermarks, page numbers, cropping and grayscale are all processed locally on your phone. Folivo uses no artificial intelligence model.
  • You choose what Folivo sees: PDFs are opened through Android's file picker, or when you choose "Open with" or "Share" in another app. Pictures come from Android's photo picker, your camera app, or the document scanner. Folivo does not browse your storage and does not ask for broad storage access.
  • Working copies: to work fast and safely, Folivo keeps a private copy of an open file and page previews in its cache. A password-protected PDF you unlock is decrypted into this private cache so it can be displayed. These copies are removed automatically after two days, with "Clear cache" in Settings, or when you uninstall.
  • Passwords: passwords you type to open or protect a PDF are used in memory only. They are never stored or sent anywhere.
  • What is kept: a list of recent files with a small picture of the first page and the page you stopped on, your starred files, unsaved marks (kept as a draft until you save or discard them), your saved signatures (stored as drawing strokes), pictures you placed on pages, and your settings. You can clear the recent list in Settings, delete signatures in the editor, and uninstalling removes all of this.
  • What gets saved: new files are written to Downloads/Folivo (or Pictures/Folivo for images), or to the folder you pick if you choose "Ask each time". Your original file is changed only if you choose "Replace the original". If you tap Share, Open with or Print, the file is handed to the app or printer you pick, and its own privacy policy then applies.

Document scanner and camera

Scan to PDF uses Google's ML Kit document scanner, which is part of Google Play services and runs on your phone. Google Play services may download the scanner the first time you use it. The scanner takes the pictures, finds the page edges and hands Folivo only the pages you keep. Folivo itself does not ask for camera permission; if the scanner is not available, Folivo opens your phone's camera app to take a photo.

Other permissions

Folivo asks for no contacts, location, microphone or phone permission. On Android 9 and older it asks for permission to save to your Downloads and Pictures folders, only when you save. It uses the internet only for ads, Google Play purchases and the scanner download.

Advertising

Folivo is free to use and shows ads from Google AdMob, unless you have Premium or bought Remove ads. Before ads load, a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform appears where the law requires one (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom). AdMob can use your device's advertising ID and general device information, such as model, language and app version, to show ads and measure them. Banners appear on the home, result and settings screens, never over a document you are reading or editing. A full-screen ad may appear after a file is saved, limited in number and spacing. A rewarded video plays only when you choose to watch one, to use a Premium tool once.

You can change your consent at any time in Settings, under Ad privacy choices (shown where the law requires it). You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's privacy policy applies to the ads it serves: policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) and Remove ads are sold through Google Play Billing. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card or payment details. The app only learns which products your Google account owns, so it can unlock them, also offline.

Ratings

After you have saved a few files, the app may ask whether you are enjoying it. If you choose to rate, Google Play's own review sheet opens and the review is handled by Google Play.

Data sent to Google services

The ad service and Google Play services may collect approximate location (from your IP address), app interactions, crash logs, diagnostics and device identifiers, as described in Google's policies. We do not receive this data about you as an individual. Folivo has no analytics of its own and does not sell or rent any data. Your documents are never part of this data.

Children

Folivo is made for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy
